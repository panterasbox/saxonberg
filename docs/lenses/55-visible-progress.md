# #55 · The Lens of Visible Progress

> One of the [Schell deck](./README.md). Lens named from *A Book of
> Lenses*; questions paraphrased, analysis our own.[^aogd-vp]
>
> ⭐⭐ **This entry changed the doctrine.** Its third question exposed a
> contradiction in [measurement.md](../measurement.md) that had stood
> unnoticed, and the doc was amended 2026-09-29 (B4 re-scoped, Part 6.4
> added). The entry is rewritten against the amended version.

## The lens

Players need to see that they are making progress on a difficult
problem. What does it mean to make progress here? Is there enough of
it, and could there be more interim steps of progressive success?
**What progress is visible, what progress is hidden — and can the
hidden be revealed?**

> **From the book.** ⭐ **This is a *puzzle* lens**, not a progression
> one: it sits in the puzzles chapter as *Puzzle Principle #3: Give a
> Sense of Progress*, and its immediate neighbour is *Puzzle Principle
> #4: Give a Sense of Solvability* — *"If players begin to suspect that
> your puzzle is not solvable, they will become afraid that they are
> hopelessly wasting their time and give up in disgust. You need to
> convince them that it is solvable."* His example is Rubik's Cube,
> which ships **already solved** in the box, so the player has seen the
> answer state with their own eyes before they ever scramble it.

## Which of our seven it sharpens

**[Lens 4 · Values](../design-lenses.md)**, where it corrected the
instrument rather than the lens. Secondarily lens 2, which gains an
argument it did not know it had (solvability, below).

## Why our design prompts it

Because the third question had an answer we were giving wrongly.

**What "the mirror" is** — the term appears across these entries, so
state it once. It is **not a screen**. It is a legibility commitment,
`measurement.md` **B3**, derived from the platform defining itself
against the Feed:

> The feed measures you and hides the measurement. The mirror measures
> you and shows you. ⇒ **Any measurement the platform makes of you is
> one you can read.**

**Four delivery modes**, after the amendment:

| mode | what | when |
|---|---|---|
| **routine qualitative** | the body line in `look` — your condition, in words | every time you look |
| **routine quantitative** | performance — quantity, output, competence, hours | freely, numerically *(added 2026-09-29)* |
| **deviation narration** | *"You'd not have done that a year ago"* | only when a **disposition** write pushes `expressed` from `equilibrium` |
| **the record** | acts, transcript, evidence | on request |

⚠⚠ **What the third question found.** Part 6.3 said a self-view may
never show *your position*, and Part 9 listed *"no progress bar"* among
the forbidden. Read generally, that is **the platform making a
measurement of you and declining to show it** — B3's exact prohibition,
one row above B4 in the same table. The rule contradicted its own
warrant and had done since it was written.

## What the design answers

**Question three, after the amendment, has a clean answer split by
decidability:**

- **A declared standard** — disposition, virtue, faith — is shown as
  bands and narrated acts, never a position. Not because we are coy,
  but because *you cannot read yourself; disclosure is discovery*, and
  a number there is a farming target for something that has no right
  answer.
- **Performance** — the decidable half — is **readable, numerically,
  routinely.** You already know you lifted more. Hiding it was never
  protection.

⭐⭐ **And the solvability half has an answer we built for other
reasons: the refusal.** In a derivable world, tractability is
guaranteed in principle and invisible in practice — a player cannot see
that the wall in front of them is passable. *The refusal is the
progression UI* exists precisely so the world can say **this is
reachable, and here is what lifts it.**

> **A refusal that names what lifts it is our Rubik's-cube-in-the-box.**
> It shows the player the solved state before they have solved anything.

That is a stronger reading of the refusal doctrine than the one it was
adopted under, and it is why a verb must **exist** in order to decline.

## The verdict

⭐⭐⭐ **Schell wins, and the win was worth more than an entry.** His
third question did not find a design gap; it found a **doctrine that
contradicted itself**, which no amount of internal review had caught in
the fourteen months the rule stood. The lens's value here was entirely
in being asked from outside.

**Adopt solvability as a first-class obligation** rather than an
accident of refusals happening to name their remedies.

## Tensions & risks

⚠⚠ **The firewall is now under more pressure, not less.** Once numbers
are permitted for performance, the cheapest next step is a number for
everything — and the one place they are genuinely forbidden (a declared
standard) is the place a cockpit card will most want one. The doc's own
residual open question is exactly this, and rule 3 has to hold without
the general ban standing behind it.

⚠⚠ **`bands-not-theta` lost its warrant and needs a new one.** Part 6.3
cited it as *"the same firewall,"* and 6.3 no longer reaches
competence, which is performance. If coarse competence bands survive —
and they may well should — it must be on their own argument (**false
precision**: a derived band claims only what the evidence supports,
where a decimal claims more than it can), not on this one. ⭐ **That
argument has not been written.**

⚠ **Interim steps are still thin where it matters.** The amendment
gives performance fine-grained readings, so the second question is
answered for a trainee. It is *not* answered for a person growing into
a character, where bands are coarse by design and long stretches pass
with nothing that may honestly say so. The honest options there remain
(a) accept it, or (b) supply interim signal **perceptually** — the way
expertise-as-discrimination does — never numerically.

⚠ **Hidden and absent are still indistinguishable** for the record
channel. A player cannot tell "this is tracked and readable on request"
from "nothing is tracked," and one who never believes there is
something to read never asks.

## Implications

1. ⭐⭐ **Write the `bands-not-theta` argument or drop the rule.** It is
   currently a convention with a retired justification, which is the
   condition in which rules get deleted by whoever finds them
   inconvenient.
2. ⭐⭐ **Recognize the refusal as our solvability device and hold it.**
   Every refusal names what lifts it; that is not only fairness
   ([#25](./25-judgment.md)), it is the mechanism that tells a player a
   wall is a door. A refusal that names no remedy fails two lenses.
3. ⭐ **The record channel needs a first-time on-ramp.** Routine
   quantitative reading rescues the trainee case, but the *record* —
   your acts, your evidence — is still pull-only, and *"it's there if
   you look"* is how a design fails this lens while believing it
   passed.
4. **Decide interim signal for the coarse half.** Either long silences
   between bands are the design, or the signal is perceptual. Not finer
   bands, which is a gauge with extra steps.
5. ⚠ **Add "hidden vs. absent" to design review.** For any measured
   thing: can a player who has never asked distinguish this from
   nothing being measured?
