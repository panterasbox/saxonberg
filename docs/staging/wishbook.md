# The Wishbook

**The mature world, seen through the eyes of the people study.com
serves.** A staging document: none of these scenes is built as a
scene, but every one is composed from named systems, almost all
shipped — the fast-forward the design has earned. Premise: the world
has been running for years. The accretion thesis paid off — hundreds
of contributors have added rooms, goods, brains, scripts, and whole
localities over the fixed honest physics. Coolness is the north
star; the audience is the adult with a high-stakes real goal.

The one wish under every vignette (the [fantasy
lens](../lenses/fantasy.md)):

> **Be who you're becoming — now, visibly, among others, in a world
> where your competence is real power.** Not escape from the
> vocation: the vocation transfigured.

## The texture of year three

What "running for years" *looks like* here, because the ledgers make
age visible: chains-of-title a dozen owners deep — the clasp-knife
whose provenance runs through two shops, a duel, and an estate.
Chronicle pages that read like sagas. Offices held by their third
elected occupant; a founder who genuinely stepped back. Shelves of
consigned goods whose maker's marks are reputations. Scripts taught
hand-to-hand down cohorts of dormmates. Forum argument-maps, mature
and cited like case law. Renown weathered into localities — the name
everyone at the counting-houses knows that means nothing at the
treeline. Corridors of Duncan Hall re-themed a hundred ways. A
skyline of player-titled parcels where the avenue used to end.

## Vignettes

### 1. The night the gallery flooded — *the nursing student (HESI/TEAS)*

The storm that had been reading on the sky all evening breaks over
the delving; a gallery floods, a support fails, and six miners come
up wrong — two bleeding, one pinned and fractured, one seizing on
CO from the sealed pocket, two walking-wounded and loud. She's
second-year on the platform and first on the scene. `assess` is her
exam made flesh: real blood volumes dropping, real SpO₂, a fracture
impairing a slot, the quiet one who's actually the emergency. She
triages by physiology, not by health bar — binds, dresses, delegates
the walking to hold pressure, gets the CO case to air. One she
cannot save; the world is honest and the chronicle records the
night either way. Her medicine band moves because the *difficulty*
was real. The delving knows her name now.
*Systems: weather, hazard, harm/medic, vitals, respiration/CO,
perception, party, chronicle, renown, advancement. All shipped.*
**The wish: being the calm one in the crisis — before any license
says she may.**

### 2. The storm contract — *the trades student (electrician track)*

The board posts a gig: the Foundry Row substation's flooded cell
must be re-energized before the next front — the forecast is a
sky-read with honest hedge, so the deadline is a judgment call too.
He walks the cell with `analyze electrical`: standing brine, a
grounded frame, a covering stack that will conduct if he's careless.
Drains the pool, dries the contacts against the clock (latent heat
is not a suggestion), chooses the rubber over the quick metal
splice, stages the re-energize from outside the circuit. One
mistake here is a SustainedShock and everyone on the row knows it —
which is exactly why the completed contract pays renown along with
crowns, and why his transcript credits a deed, not attendance.
*Systems: electricity, weather/wetness, thermal, contracts/escrow
(built, in review), banking, analyze legibility, advancement. *
**The wish: the one who reads the circuit and gets it right under
pressure.**

### 3. The Tuesday seminar — *the teacher candidate (Praxis)*

Her seminar meets at dusk at the watchpost: six newcomers, one
planted TrapKit, a sentry who doesn't grade on a curve. She teaches
awareness the only way it mints — by supervised doing: sweep, spot
the hint, cost the search, disarm. Her lesson-plan is a script she
wrote (cues the drills, staggers the reveals); her students' first
deeds land on *their* transcripts, and the command discipline
credits *her* only for what she did for others — teaching pays,
formally. Three cohorts later, her chronicle reads like a teaching
portfolio with receipts, and two of her graduates run seminars of
their own.
*Systems: advancement/conferrals, concealment/stealth, hazard,
scripting, command discipline, producer standing, chronicle. All
shipped.*
**The wish: the teacher whose students visibly grow — a classroom
before the state grants one.**

### 4. The audit — *the accounting student (College Saver, business)*

The proprietor thinks his bookkeeper is skimming; the bar's regulars
think so too, which is worse. She sits down with the books — and the
books are *ledgers*, append-only, every leg balanced by
construction. She walks the wage postings against the roster, the
tips recorded against the jar collections, the remittance splits
against the terms schedule — and finds it: not theft, a mis-keyed
fee split, twelve weeks compounding. The bookkeeper is cleared in
public because the receipts are public to the parties; regard moves;
suspicion here is *answerable*. The proprietor offers her the books
for keeps.
*Systems: banking ledger, employment/wages, tips, terms/fees, P&L,
regard, chronicle. All shipped.*
**The wish: the one who reads the books and finds the truth.**

### 5. The block on Foundry Row — *the real-estate candidate*

A contributed locality opened last season and the avenue is minting
storefronts. The "bargain" corner lot is the talk of the row — until
she pulls its chain-of-title and reads what the flippers didn't: the
extent was subdivided under a grant with a revert clause; the
"sale" on offer is a lease wearing a deed's clothes. She brokers the
honest transaction two doors down instead — bilateral consent,
clean transfer, her commission settled on-ledger — and posts the
title-reading to the forum thread where the block's buyers argue.
Nobody on Foundry Row closes without her anymore.
*Systems: parcel/title/chain-of-title, grants/leases, transfer
consent, banking settlement, forums. All shipped.*
**The wish: the sharp-eyed broker whose diligence is the deal.**

### 6. Holding the ford — *the ASVAB candidate*

The caravan contract is hers: three wagons, two greenhorns, a
mercenary she can afford because she planned the rations right
(encumbrance math is logistics; satiation is a supply line). She
adopts vanguard before the treeline, assigns the mercenary to
intercept, briefs the greens on `defend`. The wolves hit the ford
at dusk exactly like the old hands said. The formation holds — the
interception fires, the line bends around the wagons, her one call
redirects the mercenary at the moment that matters. One casualty,
triaged on the far bank. Command credits her for the interceptions
and the call — leadership measured, not claimed.
*Systems: parties, formations, multi-party combat, encumbrance/
haulage, metabolism/rations, medic, command discipline, contracts.
All shipped or in review.*
**The wish: the captain whose line holds.**

### 7. The compiler of Duncan Hall — *the CS student (College Saver)*

His floor wakes to his scripts — the morning routine he wrote as a
sophomore prank and kept because it was good. His real portfolio is
downstream: a shop-brain in the vetted catalog that three
storefronts run, an item-effect bundle other authors compose, a
recipe he teaches only by demonstration (the knowledge ladder makes
his lesson literal). Producer standing pays him something like
royalties — engagement with what he built, credited to his name by
the provenance ledger. New players know him by maker's marks before
they ever meet him.
*Systems: scripting, document store, provenance, producer stock,
chattel marks, recipe knowledge. Leans on the three unbuilt
extensibility bridges (item-effect envelope, vetted brain catalog,
scripted-behavior brain) — the wishbook's strongest argument for
building them.*
**The wish: shipping code that runs somewhere real, with users.**

### 8. The cartographers of the Third Node — *the science-minded, any track*

The liberty zone, played as endgame. The magic grid's frontier
nodes are real-but-unmapped law — invented science that keeps its
own rules as strictly as chemistry keeps hers. A four-person guild
rents lab space, builds instruments, and runs controlled trials
against the Third Node: hypothesize, measure, predict, verify. They
argue their interpretation on the argument forum — open objections,
structured, cited — until the map converges. The finding is minted
category-first in the chronicle: *their names, first*. The
conferral it unlocks exists nowhere else on the grid until they
teach it. Study.com students know exactly what this is: it's the
scientific method — except here, being first is possible.
*Systems: capability magic, the discipline grid's typed edges,
inquiry substrate (designed), argument forums, chronicle
recordOnce, renown, advancement. Magic core built; inquiry is the
gap.*
**The wish: discovery with priority — a finding that bears your
name.**

### 9. The regulars' table — *the TOEFL student*

The world is in English, and that is the product. He took the
counter shift at the tea house because service English under mild
time pressure is the gym his test-prep videos can't be: real
requests, real idioms, real repair-moves when he mishears — and an
NPC clientele with infinite patience plus a human crowd with none.
The dialogue wheels scaffold him early (hover shows the line before
he commits); the ghost command line teaches him register by
example; the forum's argument threads are academic English with
stakes, which is TOEFL Writing wearing better clothes. Months in,
the regulars' table waves him over — and he banters back without
rehearsing it first. Nobody at that table knows they're his
practice partners. That's fluency: when the language stops being
the task.
*Systems: dialogue trees/prompt wheels, attendant/service loop,
chat/forums (argument organizer), employment, recognition/belief,
register-aware rendering. All shipped.*
**The wish: to banter — fluency as social ease, not a score.**

### 10. The long transcript — *every College Saver student*

She arrived a nobody in year one — the roster shows it: guest, then
enrolled, then the dorm key from Katie's hand. Year three: an
apartment on a titled floor, a seat behind the counting-house
counter on weekday evenings, a medicine band she earned in rooms
like vignette 1, a chronicle that partitions cleanly into the
prologue she wrote at char-gen and the deeds nobody could have
written for her. Her real degree posted last spring — the conferral
fired in the University the same week, and both transcripts say the
same thing, which is the whole point. She logs in tonight because
her people are here and her work is real.
*Systems: all of them; that's the vignette.*
**The wish: the becoming, made durable — a transcript that never
stopped mattering.**

## Daily loops (the engagement between crises)

The vignettes are peak moments; retention lives in the loop a
student runs on an ordinary Tuesday. One per vertical, all
composable today:

- **Clinical (HESI/TEAS):** rounds. A standing evening walk of the
  delving's aid post — `assess` whoever's come up hurt, dress and
  bind, restock bandages from the store, log the interesting case
  in chronicle. Low drama, real practice; the crisis (vignette 1)
  is the loop under pressure.
- **Teacher cert (Praxis):** office hours. A posted hour at the
  hall — walk-ins get one drill each; the roster of her regulars
  is her gradebook; command credit trickles from every genuine
  assist.
- **Trades (electrician track):** the maintenance round. Standing
  gig: walk Foundry Row's fixtures, `analyze` for wear and wet,
  swap what's failing, invoice the parcels' owners. The storm
  contract is this round at 10× stakes.
- **ASVAB:** the escort rotation. Scheduled caravan slots — rations
  math, formation brief, usually nothing happens; readiness is the
  practice. The ford (vignette 6) is the rotation on the bad day.
- **Real estate:** the listings hour. Read the week's new titles on
  the forum board, pull chains on anything fishy, post one honest
  read. Reputation compounds by Tuesday increments.
- **TOEFL:** the shift (vignette 9's counter) — every shift is an
  immersion hour with wages.

The pattern: every loop is *practice wearing a livelihood* — paid
or credited, witnessed, chronicle-legible, and it composes the same
systems the crisis moments do. This is the vocations-are-essential
design choice cashing out: the grind isn't beside the game; it is
the game, and it is their actual studying, situated.

## The magic grid: spending the liberty zone

The one place advancement is entirely ours to invent — and this
audience tells us how to spend it:

- **Schools rhyme with vocations.** The healer school flatters the
  clinical track; lightning flatters the electrician; transmutation
  the chemist; warding the safety trades; scrying the
  diagnostician. The fantasy tier is the vocation transfigured —
  never a different life, the same life with the volume up.
- **Edges feel like curriculum.** Prereqs, specializations,
  synergies — the grid's typed edges make studying-shaped progress
  the native motion, so the study.com student's habits *are* the
  optimal play pattern.
- **Frontier nodes are the research endgame** (vignette 8): the
  inquiry substrate makes discovery, priority, and publication real
  gameplay — the one wish (being first) that reality prices out of
  reach for almost everyone.
- **Magic is the spectacle budget.** The storm's lightning already
  runs on honest conduction; the schools are where the world gets
  to be *visibly* awesome. An adult's transcript never pays out in
  awe; this one should.
- **Magic is the author's fine-grained instrument** — the reward
  channel that stays honest because its laws are invented. The
  real disciplines *cannot* hand out advancement (competence is
  measured, never granted — the honesty firewall), so an author
  who wants to reward, push, or flavor a specific player at a
  specific moment needs a channel with small, legitimate
  granularity: an attunement conferred by an NPC who owes you; a
  rune-keyed door; an enchantment riding a weapon's materials-
  response profile (an ignition rider on the heat channel, a
  ward that adds one layer to the covering stack); a charm with
  one precise micro-effect; a geas as an author-imposed constraint
  (consent norms apply). Every one is engine-legitimate (Effect
  iff gated Api — invented law obeyed as strictly as chemistry)
  yet author-*placeable* — the "loot feeling" without loot
  dishonesty, working at the micro level of combat and gear
  design just as it works at the school level. Real disciplines
  answer "what am I becoming"; magic answers "what did tonight
  give me" — and both stay true.

## What the wishbook exposes (gaps, wish-ranked)

1. **The three extensibility bridges** (item-effect envelope, vetted
   brain catalog, scripted-behavior brain) — vignette 7 is impossible
   without them; the accretion texture underlies *every* vignette.
2. **The inquiry substrate** — vignette 8's engine; the magic grid's
   endgame is thin without it.
3. **Contracts/gig board through merge** — vignettes 2 and 6 lean on
   the work-contracts build (MR !149).
4. **Courts** — vignettes 4 and 5 end happily because disputes met
   ledgers; the unhappy versions need the adjudication consumer.
5. **Threshold ceremony** (from the moments entry) — vignette 9's
   beats deserve their Katie-grade landings.

## For the videos

Each vignette is a video-shaped experience (one moment, one wish,
systems nameable on screen) — and per the settled rule, *fully
designed* qualifies. The cohort-matched ones double as
audience-targeted cuts: vignette 1 for the health-track viewer,
2 for trades, 3 for teacher-cert — the same world, pitched by wish.
