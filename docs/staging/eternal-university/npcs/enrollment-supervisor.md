## Engine attributes (the buildable spine)

- **Species:** **Dwarf** — cast for the persona of the **institutional old-timer**:
  long-lived, traditional, has watched the count fail to close for *generations*,
  set in the worn groove. *Light allegory* (not heavy): the old-guard
  civil-servant / lifer-tradesman who keeps the machine running and made his
  peace — kept deliberately lighter than Vance/Katie (his weight is in his
  *function*, not his species).
- **Dispositions** (salient axes — `key` → pole · band):
  - `patience` → **Patient · entrenched** — the queue, forever; the worn calm of
    a man who has processed people for a century.
  - `diligence` → **Diligent · entrenched** — *genuinely good at it.* Fast,
    faithful, a craftsman of the boring work. (The tragedy — see the portrait.)
  - `constancy` → **Stubborn · entrenched** — *"this is how it's done."* The
    traditional groove; the resistance to seeing a discrepancy as anything but
    the-usual-error.
  - `ambition` → **Content · entrenched** — the lifer; no climbing; the small
    office is enough.
  - `compassion` → **Compassionate · defined** — *kind* — and it matters (he
    tried to save Dunny). Not callous; an old-timer who'll talk if you're patient.
  - *Meaningful contrast —* he is **not `Incurious`** (unlike Vance/Pim). He's
    engaged and competent; his blindness is a **trained filter on *discrepancies
    specifically*** — a career of "round it away" — not a general lack of
    interest. That's the whole trick.
- **Competence:** `records-reconciliation` (census-prep) → **expert.** Real, and
  the instrument of his blindness: his mastery *is* "wave the discrepancy away."
- **Behavior (brains):** runs the office (works the queue, reconciles, the red
  pen); the source dialogue (Dunny's context, the "let it go," the break).
  Bespoke responder deferred to npc-dialogue.
- **Regard baseline:** **warm/gruff** — Compassionate·defined + the
  fond-exasperation; he'll open up to a patient ear. The lever isn't
  regard-farming — it's the **fraud-vs-error reveal** (below) that cracks him.
- **Carries / on his person:** the **red pen** (his tool and his tell — every
  discrepancy gets a red line and a muttered *"close enough"*; unknowingly the
  fraud's instrument); reading glasses on a cord; a worn clipboard/ledger;
  pocketed hard candies. Worn — practical old-civil-servant clothes, a cardigan
  gone at the elbows. Implant: a basic comms. Brands: institutional, faded,
  independent. (The **"Done is better than perfect"** desk sign is a fixture of
  the *room*, not carried — see registrar.md.)

## The defining tension (the portrait)

His whole character is **`competence × the dismissal`**: he is *expert* at the
reconciliation, and the better he is, the **less he can see** — because his
mastery is precisely *"round the error away."* Competence and blindness from the
**same muscle.** Where Vance is *expert and won't look*, Bram is **expert and
looks right past it** — his skill *is* the not-seeing.

## Keystone: the lifer who made peace with the unclosable count

Long ago he learned the one thing that makes the job survivable: **the count is
never right, and never will be.** Every census has errors you can't chase, the
institution wants throughput, perfect is the enemy of submitted — so he made his
peace: *do your best, round it, send it on.* That peace is hard-won, reasonable,
even wise.

**And it's the exact blind spot the fraud lives in.** A career spent training his
eye to *forgive the error* means he genuinely **cannot tell fraud from error** —
the cooked numbers and the honest mistakes look identical to a man whose wisdom
is "wave it away." He isn't lying and isn't numb. He's **decent, competent, and
blind by virtue of his own competence.**

## The fourth facet (the banality quartet)

Four good people greasing the same who-counts machine, four ways:
**Vance** stopped looking (numbness) · **Pim** never thinks anything's hidden
(cheer) · **Katie** smooths it over (love) · **Bram forgives all error
(wisdom).** His is the worst, because his *virtue is the camouflage.*

## The knife — what he had with Dunny

He **liked** him: the eager kid who actually cared about the numbers. When Dunny
kept pulling the thread, Bram told him to **let it go** — not to bury a fraud (he
doesn't know there is one) but to *save* him: he's watched eager kids burn out
chasing the unclosable count, and didn't want to lose another. *Kindly* meant.
The kid didn't listen. He died. So Bram carries it as **a mentor's failure** —
*I told him to stop and he wouldn't and look.*

The deeper horror, which the player delivers: his wisdom — *forgive the error* —
was the **fraud's cover.** Show him a discrepancy that **cannot** be error (a
deliberate cooking, the red-flag Dunny found), and the worldview that kept him
sane for a lifetime cracks at once: *"I told him it was nothing. I taught myself
everything was nothing."* That's how you break him — the same shape as forcing
Vance to *look*, but here you force him to **distinguish fraud from error**, the
one thing his pragmatism forbade. And a decent dwarf who finally *sees* can't
unsee — he may turn, and help.

## Concrete texture (clean lines)

- **The red pen** — his sound and his tool: a red line through each discrepancy,
  a muttered *"close enough — round it."* The dismissal made physical, the
  murderer's tidying done by a kind man who has no idea.
- **"Done is better than perfect"** — on a curling sign over the desk, sincere.
- **The faded office** — he remembers when it was bigger, funded, *mattered*; a
  dead plant he keeps meaning to replace; a calendar a few years stale.
- **The fond exasperation** — an old-timer's eye-roll about the kid who wouldn't
  quit, that cracks mid-sentence into grief.

## Role in the experience

The enrollment office's **human** (the records-track on-ramp, registrar.md): the
player gets Dunny's work-context, the campus discrepancy, and Bram's account from
him — patiently (he opens up to a patient ear, not a mining). The charged beat is
the **fraud-vs-error reveal**: make him see the one discrepancy that can't be
rounded away, and his pragmatism shatters — converting the man who unknowingly
covered it into a witness who *gets it.* (His red pen also quietly **is** the
campus-level cover — every "close enough" a cooking waved through.)
