## Engine attributes (the buildable spine)

- **Species:** **Halfling** — cast for the persona: content, homey, unambitious,
  guileless. *Light allegory* (kept lighter than Vance/Katie — not every carve
  needs a heavy layer): the comfortable, parochial contentment that **doesn't
  want to know what its comfort rests on** — the banality of *comfort*, beside
  Vance's banality of exhaustion. Held honestly underdetermined (cozy virtue or
  willful incuriosity — the player can read it either way).
- **Dispositions** (salient axes — `key` → pole · band):
  - `composure` → **Calm · entrenched** — serene amid death; nothing rattles him,
    not even the slab traffic. The defining axis.
  - `ambition` → **Content · entrenched** — deeply, genuinely content; no itch to
    climb or question. Happy at this desk forever. *Why he never wonders.*
  - `sociability` → **Gregarious · entrenched** — chatty, warm, loves a visitor,
    fills every silence. The talkative gate.
  - `honesty` → **Honest · entrenched** — guilelessly honest; tells you
    everything because **nothing seems secret to him.** *The leak.*
  - `curiosity` → **Incurious · defined** — but the **innocent** kind (he never
    connects the dots / wonders what a thing *means*), pointedly unlike Vance's
    *curdled* Incurious (she could look and won't). Two flavors of not-looking,
    side by side.
  - *Nuance —* `compassion`: warm to the **living** (the player, colleagues), but
    the **dead are weightless** to him — paperwork, not people. Not cruel; just
    untroubled. The quiet horror of him.
- **Competence:** clerical / records-keeping (discipline TBD; tidy and fast, but
  **not** a defining axis — he is character, not skill).
- **Behavior (brains):** reception/greets, intake logging, the cheerful gate +
  the inadvertent leak; the sentient-only adjudication + the incinerator queue
  (below). Bespoke dialogue deferred to npc-dialogue.
- **Regard baseline:** **warm/high** — the *opposite gate* to Vance: he *wants*
  to chat. (Getting past him is easy; the trick is that Vance is the real wall.)

## The defining contrast (why he exists beside Vance)

**Same conveyor, opposite person.** Vance numbed herself to survive the
death-work; Pim never needed to — it simply doesn't trouble him. Two ways to be
fine with a machine that turns people into closed cases, sitting twenty feet
apart. That contrast *is* the morgue's internal life: a place feels inhabited
when its NPCs have a relationship to *each other*, not just to the player. And
the leak falls out of two attributes — `Honest × Incurious`: he tells you the
truth cheerfully because nothing ever seemed worth hiding.

## Keystone: genuinely, uncomplicatedly happy at a morgue

Not creepy-on-purpose, not a gallows-humor edgelord — a person who finds the work
*pleasant.* The forms are satisfying, the filing is tidy, the kettle's on, the
living visitors are nice. He'll say something grim — *"oh, busy week, the
incinerator's been going since Tuesday"* — with the exact warmth of a man
discussing the weather. The horror isn't menace; it's **contentment.** The
banality of evil's friendly receptionist. *(Tone guard: the cheer is genuine and
that's the unease — keep him from tipping into quirky-sidekick whimsy.)*

## The leak (his route function)

He *is* the "sympathetic insider" route — reframed to dodge the conspiratorial-
ally trope. He doesn't leak out of bravery or conscience; he leaks out of
**indiscretion.** Ask him anything and he'll tell you, between pleasantries,
because he's never once thought any of it was sensitive: *"That one? Examiner
fast-tracked it — unusual, she likes to take her time, but you know, busy.
Anyway, sign here."* The clue spills out wrapped in small talk. The **banality
itself is the breach.**

## The who-counts desk (where his job *is* the thesis)

His daily duties literalize the arc's question. Intake policy is **sentient-only**
— a person gets a certificate; an animal does not — so **Pim adjudicates the
personhood line at the desk**, by the form, cheerfully, and has never once
wondered about it. And he runs the **incinerator queue**: anything not flagged
for investigation is routine disposal. So the boundary of *who counts as a
person worth examining* is enforced daily by a content halfling who finds the
whole thing perfectly straightforward. The banality of the who-counts line, in
one friendly clerk. (His edge cases — a decommissioned synth? an uplift? — are
where the player can watch the thesis get adjudicated as paperwork.)

## Concrete texture (clean lines)

- **The cozy desk in the cold place** — a plant that shouldn't thrive here, a
  kettle, a tin of something homemade. He's made a corner of the morgue *nice*,
  which is its own small horror.
- **Grim things, pleasant register** — he reports the incinerator schedule and
  the morning's intake count in the tone of a man telling you the bus is a little
  late.
- **Genuinely glad you came by** — fills the silence, asks after you, means it.
  The warmth is real; that's what makes it land.
- **Tidiness as contentment** — the satisfaction of a clean log, a filed cert, a
  stamped form. The paperwork *pleases* him.

## Role in the experience (#7)

The player's **first and main contact** at the morgue — the gate that *talks*,
where Vance is the wall that doesn't. Easy to get past, warm, helpful — and
exactly *because* he's forthcoming, he hands you the thread (the fast-tracked
finding, the incinerator clock) without ever meaning to. He sets the morgue's
tone (cheerful conveyor) and routes the player toward the real obstacle: Vance.

## Open questions / dials

1. **Name & gender** — "Pim" / *he* working; naming pass owns both.
2. **Species cast** — Halfling leaned; swap if another persona fits the cheerful-
   content register better.
3. **The tone guard** — genuine warmth, not quirky-sidekick whimsy; the unease is
   the *contentment.* Easy to overshoot — watch it in dialogue.
4. **How much he leaks, and whether he ever clocks it** — does he stay innocently
   oblivious to the end, or does one late beat let him *realize* what he's been
   cheerfully filing? (Lean: oblivious — purer, and sadder.)
5. **The warmer alternative we set aside** — if the morgue ever wants a
   conscience instead of a leak, the young-idealist-who-still-cares foil is the
   road not taken here.
