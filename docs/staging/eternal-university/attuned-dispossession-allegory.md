## The thesis

Terminus is the platform's **headline act** — the frontier/multiverse is the
neutral stage where arriving authors tell their own stories, but the authored
core has to demonstrate *one* specific, hard, decodable allegory done well, or
the "safe stage for hard stories" promise has no example to point at. The shame
we're choosing to carry is **indigenous dispossession via forced assimilation**
— and the whole point is that it must **propose redemption through
participation, not lament.** The redemption and the shame share one address: the
player doesn't tour the wound, they *do the thing the platform is for* (see,
recognize, reckon, restore) on the people it's easiest not to do it for.

## Craft principles (the guardrails, from the conversation)

These are the discipline the allegory has to keep, hard-won across the thread:

- **Allegory ≠ theme.** An allegory needs a *whom* (a specific real referent)
  and a *device that does specific mechanical work* (Maus's animal taxonomy
  *is* the Nazi racial hierarchy; it isn't decoration). "Capture is bad" is
  theme. A district you can decode as *this specific machine* is allegory. Early
  drafts of this thread kept sanding the referent off and producing mood — the
  failure mode to watch.
- **True vs. false about where the evil lived — not abstract vs. specific.**
  Animal Farm is razor-specific and never anti-Russian; it aims at a *power*
  (the vanguard-become-masters), not a *people*. "Reskinned bigotry"
  ("constructs are evil") fails not for being vivid but for putting evil in a
  *birth-category*, which is the mechanism of real bigotry re-performed.
  **Locate the evil in the system/institution, never in a kind of person.** You
  may be as strong as Orwell as long as you are as *true* as Orwell.
- **Distortion must reveal, not conceal.** Abstraction is legible exactly when
  the distortion carries the meaning (Guernica); it goes dead when it's a fog
  you hide in. Test any move: does it make the real thing *more* legible or
  *less*?
- **Defamiliarization is the protection.** A fictional people carrying the
  structure lets players tell hard stories safely *and* denies would-be
  caricaturists a 1:1 real referent to cartoon. The belief/recognition engine
  already renders a stereotype as *the beholder's projection* — the mechanic,
  not the failure.
- **Mosaic, not portrait.** Build the allegory from *several* real
  dispossessions (Canada + Australia + …) so it points at the **universal
  structure** of indigenous dispossession rather than one nation's specific
  wound. Composite = universal = uncaricaturable. (The multiple veins converging
  on one structure is also the signal the work is *organic*, not bolted-on —
  see the "absorption" bridge below, where the real history already fused our
  two mechanisms.)
- **Safe means "can never become the harm it depicts,"** not "gentle." Truth
  secures that, not softness.

## The vehicle — the RETURNED attuned (not the ancients)

Canon settles that ancient and returned attuned are **not the same kind of
being**. The allegory rides the **returned** only:

- **Ancient attuned** — extinct field-species, "gone as a kind, forever." Died
  in the Fallow of habitat loss (the field drained). The **paradise-myth**,
  safely lost. *Cannot* carry a dispossession-by-institution story: they were
  extinct centuries before the clergy returned; the clergy never met them. Their
  loss was to entropy, not to any hand.
- **Returned attuned (`homo/sensitivus`)** — the **modern playable people.**
  Ordinary fallen mortals in whom the gift surfaces as a *trait*, a fresh
  emergence, **not a surviving bloodline**; "the gift without the grace." The
  clergy's whole arc — revere → learn-from → monopolize → dispossess — is with
  *these*. So the institution's **sin is against the returned**, specifically.
- **Both projections are canon and load-bearing.** The returned were *never* the
  legendary ancients reborn — just ordinary people the world *projected* the
  myth onto. So **both the reverence (pedestal) and the discarding
  (dispossession) are projections** — the belief-engine's doing, not their
  truth. This is the built-in anti-caricature engine: no noble-savage romance,
  no denigration; both are the beholder's myth. **Hold both.**

Because the returned are **playable**, a player can experience the dispossession
*from the inside* — redemption-through-participation at the most literal level.

## The mechanism — schooling (spine) + commoditization (backdrop)

**Decision: schooling is the spine; commoditization is the backdrop.** They are
two *different* real harms and must not be blurred:

- **Commoditization (backdrop — this is already canon).** The University learns
  the gift *from* the returned and mass-produces it as the **implant, for the
  non-attuned masses.** The implant is *not* for the sensitivus — they don't need
  it; that's the whole point of the species. Their dispossession is
  **comparative/obsolescence**: once they were the only ones who could touch the
  field, sacred and rare; after the Widening anyone can attune with a clinic
  visit, so their defining distinctiveness becomes a commodity and they are made
  *redundant*. (Correcting a dead-end from the thread: there is **no beat where a
  sensitivus receives an implant** — incoherent.)
- **Schooling / disciplinary erasure (spine — this is what we ADD).** A naturally
  attuned people attunes the *wild, natural, uncounted* way (raw field access,
  the ancients' way, off any sanctioned protocol). The University — whose entire
  nature is **legibility, the count** — decides wild attunement is improper,
  unmanaged, illegible, and **disciplines it into the institution's sanctioned,
  trainable, countable form.** Not "here's a prosthetic" but *"you attune wrong;
  we'll teach you to attune properly."* The loss is the natural self trained away
  and replaced with the standardized one (language beaten out — exactly the
  residential-school mechanism). Assimilation **is** being made legible: the
  conversion of uncounted-wild into counted-institutional, which ties straight
  into the honest-count spine.
- **"Absorption" is the bridge.** The Australian record already names both as one
  policy ("ultimate absorption," "forget there were ever any Aborigines") — which
  is nearly word-for-word canon's commoditization (the Widening dissolving the
  attuned into the universal field until no one remembers they were distinct). So
  spine and backdrop are the *same structure* the real history calls one thing.

## Integrated / unintegrated — a LEDGER STATUS, not blood

The single make-or-break decision. If "integrated vs. unintegrated" is a *kind*
of sensitivus (a fact in the blood/species), we re-implement status-Indian /
blood-quantum *as ground truth* — reskinned bigotry relocated, because the real
harm of those systems was disguising an *imposed political status* as a *fact of
nature*. So:

- **The division lives in the ledger, not the blood.** One people.
  "Integrated/unintegrated" is a **status the institution assigns and records**
  (at the Registrar — the same ledger that would hold the redlining covenant,
  see below). Depicting it as an *imposed, contestable classification* **is** the
  critique; depicting it as natural would be the endorsement.
  - **Integrated** — your natural gift was schooled into the sanctioned forms;
    you're legible, on the count, a functioning modern citizen who uses the
    network's protocols. (A sensitivus *taught to route the gift the proper way*
    — no implant needed or given.)
  - **Unintegrated** — you kept the wild natural way, off the sanctioned forms,
    off the count. The elders, the community the city routes around.
- **Char-gen IS the integrating event.** A playable sensitivus starts
  **integrated** because being a modern Terminus citizen *means* you were schooled
  into the sanctioned way — the wild way is what got trained out before the game
  began (true to being born into the already-assimilated world). The player's
  arc is the **reckoning**: discovering it's gone, meeting the unintegrated,
  learning what it was, choosing whether to reconnect. Not cosplaying the
  dispossessed — *reckoning* with a loss that's upstream of you.
- **The unintegrated are NPCs because they predate/refused the school.** Rich,
  hand-authored carves (the "NPCs are expensive" rule — dignity, not a faceless
  class). Restate the protective rule not as a prohibition but as a fact of the
  world: **the fully-unintegrated life is upstream of char-gen — you can only
  *approach* it through play, never *start* there.**

**Two hard guardrails:**

1. **Never a mechanical penalty for the dispossessed condition.** The instant
   "unintegrated" carries a stat/economic disadvantage, the game is *paying out*
   disadvantage for identity — forbidden by the bible ("the world may react to
   who you are; it may never pay you for it"). It is a social/legal/narrative
   condition, never a number. (If anything the unintegrated, closer to the wild
   natural way, are *richer* in presence — but do not let that tip into mystical
   superiority; hold both projections.)
2. **Hold both projections** (romanticize *and* discard); the unintegrated are
   people living differently that the city can't see straight — not saints, not
   victims-only.

## Locations in Terminus (the mechanism, walked through town)

The device is *playable* because it lands on machinery already being built:

- **Oldtown & the Chapel** — the original mission-settlement: the ideological
  engine, where "erasure is salvation" was once doctrine and where "teach, don't
  crown" is the *scar tissue* of having learned better. Ground zero and reformed
  remnant in one place.
- **The Registrar / enrollment (char-gen intake)** — the naming/assessment desk;
  the place that assigns your official identity is where the wild gift is
  measured, filed, and normed. Holds the integrated/unintegrated status.
- **The dorms / residential halls** — the on-the-nose one: cheerful freshman
  dorms descend from the residential *halls* where attuned children were housed
  and remade. "Built on the ruin," made local.
- **The Sanctuary** — the aether-nexus at the dead heart: the sacred site that
  was the attuned's and became the *institution's* core. The taken holy place.
- **A burial ground the city routes around** — the unmarked graves, the
  contested count made physical. The "part of town we'd rather not talk about,"
  as a specific place with a specific fight over who's in it and how many.
- **The Museum** — the counter-institution and the redemption engine; already
  *is* the honest count, kept by the clergy-line that "carried the true account
  unbroken." Where players **participate in the reckoning** (reconstruct the
  blurred count) rather than tour grief.

**The reckoning apparatus is already the spine of the game.** The real thing is
a **Truth and Reconciliation** Commission; the founding is the **Recognition**;
the rite is the **honest count.** Point it at the institution's own history and
the redemption mechanics already exist. The clergy are a **reformed
perpetrator**, not an innocent bystander; the reckoning is *ongoing* (museum,
count, forums) with players inside it.

## Real-world referents (the mosaic) — verified, for grounding

Canada supplies the **schooling spine**; Australia fills the **ledger/census**
slots the game's own engines already have. Together they cover the machine.

**Canada — the schooling spine.**
- **Indian residential schools** (~1830s–1997), church-run (Catholic, Anglican,
  United, Presbyterian) in partnership with the state; ~150,000 children
  forcibly removed; explicit assimilationist purpose the **Truth and
  Reconciliation Commission (2015)** named **"cultural genocide."** ~4,100
  confirmed child deaths on the record, est. 6,000+.
- **The contested count (handle honestly).** The 2021 Kamloops "215 remains"
  claim outran the evidence: those were ground-penetrating-radar *anomalies*, not
  exhumed bodies; the First Nation itself shifted to "anomalies" by 2024, no
  remains have been forensically confirmed, and the "mass grave" wording drew
  legitimate correction. **But** the underlying atrocity is TRC-documented and
  *predates* the radar entirely — "it was just ordinary graveyards / overblown"
  is the opposite overshoot. The truth is an uncomfortable middle. *This
  meta-story — real horror → number that outran verification → backlash that
  weaponized the bad number → people tune out — is itself the honest-count-vs-Mara
  machine, and makes the allegory richer (the fight over the count is part of the
  subject).*

**Australia — the ledger/census veins (mostly new).**
- **Stolen Generations** (1910–1970; *Bringing Them Home*, 1997; 10–33% of First
  Nations children removed) — same schooling/assimilation spine on a second
  continent (this is what makes the structure read as *universal*).
- **Terra nullius** — dispossession by a legal fiction of *emptiness* (no polity,
  no treaty), held until **Mabo** (June 1992). Erasure-via-the-ledger; maps onto
  the title/covenant machinery.
- **The uncounted (the killer vein).** **Section 127**: "in reckoning the numbers
  of the people… aboriginal natives shall not be counted," repealed by the **1967
  referendum.** Precise, myth-busting detail (it was *not* a "flora and fauna
  act"): pre-1967 censuses counted Aboriginal people by race specifically to
  identify "full-bloods" and **subtract them from the official population.** They
  were counted *in order to be removed from the count.* For a game whose
  redemptive rite is the honest count, this is the most resonant fact available.
- **The "Protector"** — the Chief Protector of Aborigines (A.O. Neville, WA
  1915–1940) as legal guardian of Aboriginal children, superseding parents:
  benevolence-as-control, made a job title. Maps onto the clergy/University-as-
  guardian.
- **"Breed out the colour" / biological absorption** (darkest vein, handle with
  gravity) — Neville's goal to "merge them into our white community and
  eventually forget that there were ever any Aborigines"; the 1937 conference's
  "ultimate absorption." This is the **bridge** between our spine and backdrop
  (see Mechanism above).
- **Dreaming / songlines** (the *dignity* vein — research firsthand, do not have
  Claude characterize it; sacred and varies across hundreds of nations) — land
  known through presence and story rather than the colonial map; maps onto the
  unintegrated's wild, natural, uncounted way, giving them a living culture
  rather than only a wound.
- **Reckoning veins** — **Mabo / Native Title (1992–93)** = legal restoration of
  erased title (→ Lands & Works striking the covenant; reparation as a
  *mechanic*); **National Apology (2008, "Sorry")** = the public reckoning ritual
  (→ the Recognition / the honest count turned on the institution).
