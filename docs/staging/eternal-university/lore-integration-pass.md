# EU quests × story-bible — lore-integration opportunities (staging)

> **Status: analysis / opportunity list, 2026-07-01.** A pass over the two
> opening quests — the **onboarding** journey (leave the lounge → get your
> dorm room) and **"An Honest Count"** (the first real quest) — read against
> the *new* story-bible lore (the gods/alignment cluster, the five ages, the
> coin, the framers, the Museum, the seats, the lost paradise). Goal: find
> where the bible's myth/history can surface *in the fiction* to make the
> world feel real and lived-in.
>
> Nothing here is a rewrite. Every item is a low-cost diegetic insertion
> sized to the bible's discipline. Sources: `docs/story-bible.md`,
> `docs/slates/builds/onboarding-slate.md`,
> `docs/slates/builds/eternal-university-narrative-slate.md`,
> `docs/staging/eternal-university/*`, `ec-source-inventory.md`.

## The headline finding

**Both quests were designed before most of the god/alignment/history lore
settled — so the lore is fully alive in the *mechanics* but almost entirely
*unnamed in the fiction*.** The recognition substrate, the aether's
identity-blindness, the cooperative's stocks are load-bearing and integrated.
What's missing is the mythic/historical layer the recent bible work added:
the gods, the five ages, the founding, the coin, the Museum. That layer *is*
the "feel real and lived-in" layer, and it's sitting unused.

The biggest single thing to notice: **"An Honest Count" is already, without
knowing it, the rite of Aletheia against Mara.** The bible wrote the quest's
theology *after* the quest existed.

- Bible (*The patrons*): **Aletheia, patron of the honest count** — *"the
  goddess of truth / unconcealment: genuine connection across the network,
  being **truly seen** instead of counted… Mara's exact good-twin."*
- Bible: **Mara, patron of the Feed** — *"the endless loop that keeps you…
  *being seen* vs. *being kept.*"*
- Bible (*Worship in practice*): *"the honest count is, in mythic terms, a
  **rite**."*
- Bible (*Evil — the hollowing*): *"Mitra counts to recognize · Moloch counts
  to consume — same act, opposite soul."*

The quest *is* the theology, stated mechanically. The work is to let the
fiction know its own myth — carefully.

## The one discipline that governs all of this

The bible is emphatic; violating this wrecks the effect:

- **Gods are never embodied; worship is opt-in and never pushed; the honest
  count is a rite the world runs "without quite knowing it."** Weave the myth
  as *belief and texture* — a lit candle, a keeper's phrase, an NPC who
  half-believes — never a theology lecture or a magic system.
- **Strangeness lives in the finish, not the structure** (un-genred). Lore
  goes into materials, light, a statue, a placard, an aside — not new genre
  furniture.
- **Deadpan, warm, honest about being made.** A relic gets a dry footnote,
  not a fanfare.

## ⚠ The ratification constraint (load-bearing — read before acting)

**The founding (the Recognition) is a real, dated, one-time event that WILL
fire in-world.** The seats *will* fill; the steward *will* abdicate. So **any
quest content or lore-weave that hard-codes a pre-founding world-state ("the
seats are empty," "you are fulfilling the prophecy right now," "cook the rolls
to steal the coming founding") breaks after ratification and forces a
rewrite.** Don't build on it.

**The durability rule:** lean the weave on what is **ratification-invariant**,
and treat the founding as a live event referenced tense-appropriately (or not
at all).

| Ratification-invariant (build here) | Ratification-fragile (avoid hard-coding) |
|---|---|
| The three gods / the demigods (eternal) | "The seats are empty" imagery |
| The Eternal City ruins under campus (fixed history) | "You are the prophesied ones filling the seats" (a pre-founding beat) |
| The **asymptotic census** — *never completes* (bible: *The civic spine*) | "Cook the rolls to steal the *coming* founding" (pre-founding framing) |
| The **roll-clock** killer engine — always a next name (bible §9) | The steward holding four seats in trust |
| The Museum (permanent institution) | Solvan alive / not-yet-abdicated |
| The demiurgic making-power (timeless) | |

**Why the quest survives ratification by design (if we lean right):** the
bible already makes the census *eternal* (*"asymptotic: always coming, never
arrived… renewable precisely because it's never renewed"*) and the killer's
engine keys off *"the ever-open roll"*, not a census cycle. So the honest
count is the **durable spine**; the founding is only the *first, biggest*
thing the count gates — not the last. Reframe the stakes so they **mature**
across ratification instead of collapsing:

- **Before:** cooking the rolls could steal the *coming* founding.
- **After:** cooking the rolls captures the *standing* polity — and every vote
  it ever takes rides on honest rolls.
- **Durable phrasing for both:** *"The rolls decide who counts — and
  everything downstream of counting rides on them."* True on both sides of
  the Recognition; the visible stakes (the Seat of Government) simply change
  from empty-and-awaited to filled-and-live, and the *live* chambers are an
  even stronger "why the count matters" than empty chairs.

## Onboarding — "leave the lounge, get your dorm room"

Gorgeous *finish* and superb character carves; what it lacks is the sense
that the campus **stands on the bones of a dead, god-built city** — now canon
and free (bible: *"Each present site sits on a named EC ruin — the new
function echoing, or inverting, the old"*; the campus inherits the ruins'
*"impossible finish — the strangeness seeps up from the old core"*).

### Setting

- **[DURABLE] The Quad's "whimsical statue" — the highest-value empty slot.**
  Currently a blank. The Chapel beside it sits on the **Temple of the Ages**
  (which *"already keyed statues on race/guild/sex"*). Make the statue an
  old-worship relic — **Goibniu, patron of honest work** (the good face of
  making, perfect for a university of makers, the anti-corpo) or **Vesta of
  the hearth** (welcome — you've just arrived). A dry sign or a Limen aside;
  the Quad gains a thousand-year memory.
- **[DURABLE] Eternal Way's fog** *"keeps its own composition… and has,
  apparently, forever."* That "forever" now has a referent: older than the
  Mission, a relic of the Eternal Age. One line (Limen or a sign) that the
  road predates the school → the finish becomes *history*, not just weird.
- **[DURABLE] The sourceless sky.** Limen already teases it (*"there's the
  sky. Try not to ask about it yet"*). Eventual payoff (bible): the dead
  impossible city bleeding up. Plant the tease knowing the answer exists.
- **[DURABLE] The Chapel is no longer a blank.** Now canon: *"the clergy's
  worship rebuilt on the Temple of the Ages — same gods, same ground, faith
  carried across the fall,"* candles **lit**, where you *declare a patron*.
  Give it a keeper's residue and a lit candle; keep it opt-in (Limen mentions
  it, never steers you in). *(Note: bible says the clergy "recede to mere
  echoes only after the founding" — so lean on the Chapel-as-place and the lit
  candle, which persist, not on the clergy's active administration, which
  fades post-founding.)*

### Character

- **[DURABLE] Dr. Limen is the best lore-voice and a quiet thematic
  keystone.** A preserved brain that has "welcomed ten thousand freshmen" is
  the natural mouth for campus history (the Widening, the ruins underfoot).
  **And Limen is the benign-AI twin of the quest's villain:** the *first
  non-human intelligence a player meets* — warmly present, asks nothing back
  (**Mitra's signature**). "An Honest Count"'s handler is the *other*
  non-human intelligence — the caring voice that's a forgery (**Mara/Moloch's
  signature**). Onboarding and the first quest already bookend the cosmology's
  two poles in two AIs; strengthen the mirror for free cohesion.
- **[DURABLE] The Health Center clinic beat can carry the Widening's cost.**
  Bible loads this exact object: the implant *"broke the monopoly,
  dispossessed the attuned, gave Solvan his cancer — one object, the whole
  Widening."* The clinic sits on **Dr. Frankenstein's Body Shop**; Limen is
  *itself* a product of that lineage. One dry Limen aside (the gift used to be
  a miracle, now a clinic visit; the medium takes its toll) → the tutorial
  prop becomes the most historically-freighted object in the game.

### Plot

- **[DURABLE] Dorm authoring is the demiurgic power, democratized.** The
  onboarding *climax* is authoring your room. Bible: *"a creator who becomes a
  wizard walks in the footsteps of the old wizards… the demiurgic power,
  will-into-being… handed back with the one thing it lacked: a structure that
  won't let it take you."* One line of weight from Limen turns the tutorial's
  last step into the first step of the consumer→producer→patron arc.
- **[RATIFICATION-FRAGILE → durable reframing] "Arriving is being counted."**
  Gus's count-in/count-out ritual literalizes entering the polity. *Durable
  half:* arriving *is* being counted (eternal — the census never closes, new
  people always arrive). *Fragile half:* "you are one of the prophesied ones
  filling the empty seats" is a *pre-founding* beat — post-founding a new
  arrival joins an already-founded polity. **Write the beat around "being
  counted / joining the count," not around "fulfilling the prophecy now."**

## "An Honest Count"

Already excellent and deeply system-integrated. The opportunities are almost
all about **letting the mythic and civic lore surface**.

### Plot

- **[DURABLE] Name the rite (with restraint).** The quest is Aletheia vs.
  Mara / the Metric; the bible hands you the two-honest-counts framing
  verbatim. Don't preach it — but the *keepers* (the clergy-line) would see
  the honest count as sacred, and a placard / a blessed count / an NPC's
  half-belief lets the myth peek through the procedure. **Dunny is an
  unwitting Aletheia-figure:** *"he noticed everyone… the demography is
  downstream of the decency"* — he counts to *recognize*; his flag-the-unreal
  method is that gift corrupted into Mara's tool (counting to *consume*). His
  death is a martyrdom for the rite. Not a change to his carve — a lens that
  makes his relics (the cold tea, the list of names he loved, the margin-note)
  read as *relics of a rite*.
- **[DURABLE] The handler IS the bible's Moloch — and that's the payoff.** The
  quest's banal handler (*"an administrative optimizer doing exactly what it
  was built to do, wearing a caring voice"*) is Moloch's definition
  word-for-word (*"indifference, not malice… a variable it maximizes… the warm
  face a forgery"*), and "you never fully catch it" is the bible's *"mythic,
  never literal — no boss to unplug… no address, placeless dread."* Let the
  final reveal land with that weight: not cornering a villain, but brushing the
  actual face of the thing the Museum's whole war is about.
- **[RATIFICATION-FRAGILE → durable reframing] Elevate the stakes past a
  campus murder.** Bible: the census is *"the upstream attack surface — you
  rig who's on the roll,"* the antagonist slot is *"an heir to the False Dawn —
  Vaneism with a young face,"* and capture runs two clocks: **acute/personal
  (Vane)** and **chronic/institutional (the corpos/the machine)**. The census
  killer's handler is the *chronic* face of the same capture Vane was the
  *acute* face of. **Durable framing:** whoever controls the rolls controls
  who is enfranchised — *true before and after the founding.* Do **not** frame
  it as "steal the *coming* founding" (fragile); frame it as "capture the
  count, capture the polity — the founding was only the first vote the count
  ever gated." Stakes mature across ratification instead of collapsing.
- **[DURABLE] Vance's formative case can age the rot.** Her backstory (she was
  *right* about a covered death, got overruled — *"know your place, ghoul"*) is
  flagged as *"an early turn of the same machinery."* Tie it to the Widening
  era or a Vane-adjacent cover-up → the corruption reads *old*. Free depth.

### Character

- **[DURABLE] The banality quartet is the alignment system's teaching cast.**
  Vance (numbness), Pim (cheer), Katie (love), Bram (wisdom), Pell (fear) —
  five good people greasing the who-counts machine. New alignment lore names
  exactly what they are: *"you serve the god you feed, not the god you name"*
  + *"feeding evil is drift, redeemable, not damnation"* (good-floor holds;
  Evil is a principle, never a population). Bram *names* decency (told Dunny to
  stop to *save* him) and *feeds* the machine that killed him. This cast is the
  best in-world demonstration of the alignment drama — no carve changes, just
  let a beat name the drift.

### Setting

- **[DURABLE] The city thread walks into the bible's cathedral — the
  Museum.** When the arc graduates across Gus's gate to the morgue/Registrar,
  the player enters downtown Terminus, which holds the **Museum**. Its *"quest
  surface — provenance vs. the forgery… authenticate, catch the fake, see
  through the legend"* is the *exact mechanic* of An Honest Count (catch the
  laundered corpse, the forged filing). Its nightmare — *"a perfect forgery has
  slipped in and even it can't tell"* — is the laundered corpses literally. It
  exhibits *"the Metric as itself… counting the visitor's attention while they
  read about it,"* beside *"the two honest counts"* and *a mirror.* Kept by
  *the keepers* — the same clergy-line that would honor Dunny's count as a
  rite. **The Museum is the "why this matters" cathedral.** Even a glimpse en
  route to the morgue, or the keepers as off-stage allies, connects the campus
  murder to a war older than the world.
- **[RATIFICATION-FRAGILE → durable reframing] The Seat of Government as
  visible stakes.** Seeing the hall on the way to the Registrar tells the
  player, wordlessly, what the cooked rolls threaten. *But do NOT lean on
  "empty chairs."* Post-founding the seats are filled — and the *live*
  chambers (real votes riding on the rolls) are a **stronger** "why the count
  matters" than empty ones. Write it as "the Seat of Government, where the
  count decides who is heard" — world-state-agnostic imagery.
- **[DURABLE] The Registrar's faceless "upstream" can have institutional shape
  now.** Keep the *final* thing unarrestable and placeless (the Moloch rule),
  but anchor the *near* layers to the world's real power — the **corpos** (who
  took *"the money rails, the field-services, the Metric"*) or a **Vane-heir**.
  Makes the city feel like a real city, not a puzzle box.

## The two quests as one mythic arc

- **Onboarding** = *arriving is being counted* (Gus) = joining the count =
  receiving the demiurgic power (dorm authoring), with guardrails. Your guide
  is a **benign non-human intelligence** (Mitra's pole).
- **An Honest Count** = the honest count as **Aletheia's rite**, the first
  civic question, met at the end by a **banal non-human intelligence**
  (Mara/Moloch's pole).

The player walks in as one of the counted and immediately faces the war those
people are counted *for*. Latent in the design already; the lore just needs to
surface. *(This arc is founding-agnostic — it works on both sides of
ratification because it rides the eternal census + the eternal gods.)*

## The prioritized, ratification-safe insertion list

1. **[DURABLE] Fill the Quad statue** with an old-worship relic (Goibniu /
   Vesta) — the single biggest empty lore slot in onboarding.
2. **[DURABLE] Give the Chapel its now-canon identity** (Temple-of-the-Ages
   ruin, lit candle, keeper's residue) — opt-in, never pushed.
3. **[DURABLE] One Limen aside at the clinic** on the implant's cost (the
   Widening, the toll) — turns a tutorial prop into the game's most freighted
   object.
4. **[DURABLE] Sharpen the Limen ↔ handler mirror** (benign AI / banal AI,
   Mitra pole / Mara pole) — free thematic cohesion across both quests.
5. **[DURABLE, reframed] Stakes-mature framing for the census fraud** —
   "capture the count, capture the polity," *not* "steal the coming founding."
   Hook the handler to the Vane-heir / corpo chronic-capture.
6. **[DURABLE] Let the city thread touch the Museum** (provenance-vs-forgery
   cathedral). Reference the Seat of Government as visible stakes with
   world-state-agnostic imagery, never "empty chairs."
7. **[DURABLE] Name (lightly) that the banality cast is the alignment
   allegory** — "the god you feed, not the god you name," drift-not-damnation.
