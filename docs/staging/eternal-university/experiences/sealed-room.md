## The experience in one line

The player's **first hard access puzzle** — and a setpiece with **two gates**,
where the interesting one isn't the lock. Getting *in* teaches "obstacles have
properties, find a route"; understanding *what you found* is the real wall.

## Where it sits (the gentle intro; the authored-once setpiece)

This is the **access-literacy tutorial.** Unlike the morgue (a city institution,
a hostile stranger, hard), the sealed room is *on campus*, gated by a **known,
winnable NPC** (Katie), with **gentle stakes.** The player learns the core immsim
literacy here; the morgue tests it harder. And unlike the morgue's **renewable**
conveyor (roll-clock-stocked, recurring), this is the **authored-once** setpiece
(§14) — Dunny's specific room, singular, climactic, never re-sprung.

## The two gates (the key structure)

1. **The physical gate — the tutorial-lock.** Easy, multi-route, low-stakes. Its
   *job* is to teach route-finding, not to stop anyone.
2. **The comprehension gate — the real lock.** The work inside is **illegible
   without context** (§17.G, Obra Dinn: *understanding* is the lock, not the
   door). A player who breaks in early gets a sad, messy room and incomprehensible
   doodles — a *hook, not a reveal.* The revelation lands only with the demography
   context (from reconciling the Dunnys, #3, and the professor). **Knowledge-
   gated, not visit-gated:** it reads *whenever* you have the context — a return
   trip, or the first entry if you come late already knowing.

## The player's path (the beats)

1. **Try the door, hit the lock.** Locked, taped, sealed — the first hard
   obstacle. Gentle stakes, a known NPC behind it.
2. **The affordance narrates.** Who opens a sealed room? The proctors taped it,
   but **Katie holds the master keys** — and the player already knows her (the
   on-ramp touchpoint). The breadcrumb points at a relationship.
3. **Properties → routes** (see below). The clean route fires **Katie's whole
   carve.**
4. **Inside, the negative-space lands first.** Before any "clue": the **cold
   tea**, the unmade bed, the abandoned coursework — and the half-furnished
   wrongness (Dunny's side intact, **Wren's side stripped bare**). Two kinds of
   absence. You feel the dead kid before you find anything.
5. **The work — and it means nothing (yet).** Notebooks: counting-notation,
   number-columns, lists of names. Knowledge-gated — it reads as an obsessive
   kid's doodles. The room hands you a **thread** (*what was he doing? what are
   these names?*), not a solution.
6. **The clock and the gap.** Wren's stripped half shows **the clearing has
   already begun** (the proctors are erasing the room on a schedule — urgency,
   erasure-by-routine again). And there's a **gap** — a torn page, a reference to
   something not here. The room is *deliberately incomplete*; one piece left in
   Wren's boxes.
7. **Leave with threads, not an answer.** You exit holding the emotional weight
   (motivation), the **illegible work** (a comprehension thread), the **missing
   piece** (→ Wren), and the **clock** (the room won't last). The room is a node
   in a web, never the gate.

## The routes (immsim — properties, not solutions)

Author the properties; the routes fall out. `Locked + Katie-keyed + windowed +
being-cleared-on-a-clock + Wren-used-to-live-there.`

- **Convince Katie (social — the signposted primary).** The gentle intro's
  teaching route: *earn the person.* Invoke **Dunny-as-a-person** (he was one of
  the few who treated her as a person, not staff) and her boss-mask slips; she
  breaks her own rule, at real cost, and lets you in. Her code-switch and her
  knife pay off exactly here.
- **Break in (force — the window).** Fast, **loud.** The floor notices, the
  proctors get wind, suspicion seeds (the §11 panic), and **the clearing
  accelerates.** The expensive route.
- **Time the cleaners (opportunity).** The room's being cleared on a schedule;
  slip in when it's open and unattended.
- **Go around via Wren's boxes (lateral).** You don't even need the room for the
  one piece that matters — the escaped fragment rode out in Wren's boxes (reached
  by earning Wren's trust, patiently — see Wren's carve). Bypasses the room
  entirely for that thread.

The truth is **over-determined** (§17.G): the room holds the bulk of the work,
Wren's boxes hold the escaped fragment, and neither alone is the key — so no
single route is a hard gate.

## The teaching scaffold (this is the tutorial)

Per the first-quest teaching mandate (§3): the locked door is **never a dead
end** — Katie *narrates the affordance* (who can open it, and what it'd take), the
breadcrumb is always **the next question** (what was he doing? where's the
missing piece?), and a stuck player always has a thread (ask Katie, find Wren,
get the context). What the player is really learning is **immersive-sim
literacy** — read an obstacle's properties, find a route — on a forgiving,
on-campus, known-NPC version before the morgue makes it cold and hard.

## The shared setpiece (multiplayer)

It's an authored-once setpiece in a shared world, so the no-consumption rule
(§17.F) governs: **one player can't use up the room.** The **clearing clock is
flavored pressure, not literal loss-for-all** (it reads as urgency; it doesn't
actually erase the room out from under other players). Comprehension is
**per-player** (your own context unlocks the work); the findings feed the
**shared argument-map** on the Quad.

## Open questions / dials

1. **The comprehension gate** — confirm knowledge-gated (legible *whenever* you
   hold the demography context), not a clumsy scripted two-visit. Lean:
   knowledge-gated.
2. **The clearing clock** — real-but-not-punishing ambient pressure (Wren's gone
   half proves it's real) vs. a hard timer. Lean: pressure, not a timer — and the
   multiplayer no-consumption rule pushes the same way.
3. **The signposted-primary route** — Katie social (lean), since this is the
   gentle intro and she's the known NPC; the rest discoverable.
4. **The missing fragment** — what exactly the escaped piece is, calibrated so
   room + Wren + the other nodes over-determine the truth without any one being
   *the* key.
5. **The real kill-site thread** — the room/body audit says he was moved from
   somewhere cold; whether that location is ever found (a thread to the
   laundering's cold facilities) or stays haze is still open (see §14 / the
   crime-scene discussion).
