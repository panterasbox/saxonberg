The subject is the spine

A Subject is one persisted record carrying identity and audience. It is not a board — a board is one of the four surfaces a subject can light up, à la carte, at most one of each.

So the tabs are not navigation. Measure 14 has an argument board, a popularity board and a live chat channel. Switching between them changes the rendering, not the room — one audience, one membership, one invite list.

That is what makes chat and forum stop being separate products. They were always two faces of the same thing; the subject is where that became true in the data.

Grain is the other axis. A venue is board-grain with a flat-global handle. A topic is a promoted thread — board-scoped as parent/name, inheriting the parent's audience, and free to light up only the one surface it needed.

Which is why the rail nests them rather than listing them flat.

Audience binds once, at the subject. Open, bound to an existing guild:/mql:/contacts: ref, or curated with a managed group minted alongside. Shown next to the title because it governs every surface at once.

Argument boards are ordered by structure. Posts attach by typed edge — supports, objects-to, responds-to — and the lens derives one fact: whether an objection has anything answering it. It never reads votes. So the unanswered claim rises, not the popular one.

Switchable

OFFICIAL a mixin · last revised by Aldric, 3 days ago

A mixin for anything with two stable states you can put it into by hand. A lamp, a valve, a lever, a breaker. It says nothing about what the states mean — a switched-on lamp is lit because LightSource is also composed, not because Switchable knows about light.

What it affords
turn onmanipulation · when off
turn offmanipulation · when on

Composes cleanly with LightSource, Powered and Fixture. Do not use it for things that toggle as a consequence rather than by hand — a fire is not switchable, it is Combustible.

Seen in play

You met this on the guttering lamp in the Lounge. Every mixin page lists where you have actually encountered it — the palette is learned by recognising things you have already touched.

Authority

In a game the players author and govern, the wiki has a question an ordinary wiki does not: is this page canon?

So every page carries its standing — OFFICIAL for pages the Make chamber has adopted, and a plainer mark for player-contributed notes. Both are useful; conflating them is not.

Editing is a prompt, not a page. wiki edit switchable opens the compose prompt in the Monaco editor, seeded with the current body — the same slot any long-form answer uses.

Search is honestly missing. The docs name a search port; nothing implements it. Until it exists the sidebar is the only way in, so the tree has to be good — and the field says so rather than pretending.
