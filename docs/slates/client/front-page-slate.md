## 1. What this instance is **[DECIDED — the owner]**

The owner: *"mud.panterasbox.com is the platform for Pantera's Box, the game-experiencing community
that I personally founded and now runs the Compact until I say otherwise. If Saxonberg fails it's not
the end for Pantera's Box … for me it's about the community and the tools that I leverage to
cultivate it. Saxonberg and all its many knobs and levers is that tool."* The three names are in
[positioning.md § Part 0a](../../positioning.md). The page promotes **Pantera's Box** because the
financing comes through it, and **Saxonberg** because finishing it to 1.0 is the community's one
focus — ⭐ *"I just don't want to bury the Saxonberg brand; it's as important as the PB brand, maybe
more so at this point."* It is **not** a replacement for www.panterasbox.com, which stays a
brochure about the project and the organization.

## 2. Who owns the front page — the Compact **[DECIDED — the owner]**

- **The page is outside the fiction** — a stranger sees it before entering the world. The Saxonberg
  Post is an institution **inside** the fiction. The community's front door belongs to the
  institution that speaks for the community: **the Compact** (today the founder as operator, until
  the handover).
- ⭐⭐ **Two lanes, two pens.** The Compact owns the page and its own lane; **the Post owns its lane,
  with final cut on what is in it.** The Compact decides that the Post's lane exists — as a newsstand
  decides which papers to stock — never what the paper says. Removing the lane is a public, recorded
  act. The community speaks for itself and the press stays independent.

## 3. The page, from the top **[DECIDED in shape — the owner]**

| band | what | why |
|---|---|---|
| **host bar** — thin, across the very top | the Pantera's Box hex seal: *"a Pantera's Box community"*, **linking to panterasbox.com** | the domain already says Pantera's Box; the bar confirms whose instance this is — the frame, like a publisher's name above a title — and ties the brochure to the place you take part |
| ⭐ **the hero** | **the Saxonberg seal and wordmark, big** · the headline · **sign in** · **try it as a guest** · the live count | signing in is entering Saxonberg, and Saxonberg is what is being built — what a stranger came to see. Close to today's hero |
| ⭐ **what we're building — Saxonberg 1.0** | what is actually done toward 1.0, **read from the record** (the roadmap, the launch worklist, real counts) | the one focus; *every claim on this screen has a source* — no adjectives |
| ⭐ **the Compact** | the three chambers (Make / Fund / Play) as today, presented as **how the Pantera's Box community governs Saxonberg**, with **the handover dial**: what is still the founder's and what has passed to the polity (the lever census as the inventory); bills on the floor once a legislature exists | where Pantera's Box is explained — and something no other project could show |
| **news from the world** | **the Post's lane**, its headlines, edited by the Post; today's press room becomes it; city papers join later | the press, under its own pen (§ 2) |
| **the show** | Bobalu Smallberries on Twitch, YouTube and Kick — *live now* or *next show* | the founder's voice lives here, not in the masthead |
| **support the work** | the Fund chamber made concrete — how to support and what support earns | ⚠ **after the lawyer's read**: how minted standing is described publicly is the founding offering's classification risk; until then only what is already settled publicly |
| **footer** | panterasbox.com · the source (AGPL) · Discord · the public record · privacy | |

- **The marks are a pair, not rivals**: the brand README notes both encode one argument — the
  Borromean rings and the hex each divide into exactly three (Make / Fund / Play). Placement follows
  its one rule: *red never touches blue.*
- **What stays from today:** the full-bleed bands, sign-in above the fold on a phone (a reflow, not a
  second composition), the honest live count (a number, a real zero, or nothing), and *every claim
  has a source* — no fallback strings.
