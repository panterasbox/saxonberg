# Atlas slate — browsing what a map knows, without moving

**Status:** a seed, 2026-11-14, from the navigation pane conversation
([map-slate § The card's sizes…](./map-slate.md)). **Size:** a client
build over the knowledge model, after the navigation build.

## The idea **[DECIDED in shape — the owner]**

The navigation card shows where you are and where you can go. **Browsing
your map records** — any zone or Locality you know, without moving your
body — is its own card, the **atlas**:

- **It reads map documents, whoever's they are.** Your own map is one
  source; **a library's copy of known territory** is another (a published
  map document). The owner: *"the library would want it anywhere probably,
  and from there it's easy to duplicate for the individual."* One card, a
  different document behind it.
- **Moving between zones without moving** happens here: pick a zone, see it
  as the document knows it, follow a way out into the next zone.
- **Any mode, any size.** At the large size it is also where a course is
  plotted on a chart.
- The nav card hands off to it: its one-hop lookahead tooltips end in
  *open in the atlas*.

## Open

1. **How you move from zone to zone in the atlas** — the interaction (the
   ways out as links; a graph of zones you know; the illustrated maps as
   the overview — [map-slate § The illustrated map](./map-slate.md)).
2. **Whose documents it may open** — yours; a library's copies; a map
   someone hands you; a published map (the `published` channel).
3. **Comparing two maps** — yours against the library's, where they
   disagree.
4. **The large size and the Expanse chart** — course plotting on the
   reckoning, never the true position.
