# Room capacity slate — how many bodies a place physically holds

> **Status: UNBUILT** — salvaged from crowded-rooms-slate's last open question at its /requirements, 2026-11-17 (first raised at the size-and-carrying finalize, 2026-11-16, from size-slate § 6.6)
> **Left:** whether a place's body capacity derives from its size and the bodies' envelopes · what happens at the ceiling (refused, pressed, spilled to the next place) · lifeboats, lifts and carriages as the first consumers
> **Size:** a build

Bodies now have an envelope and a place has a size ([size.md](../../subsystems/size.md)),
so *how many people a room, a lifeboat or a lift holds* is derivable from
the model. That is a physical fact about the world.

⛔ **It is never a performance cap.** The crowded-rooms build ruled
(requirements, lens 7): the engine never refuses a body entry for the
server's sake. A full lift is full because bodies do not fit, and the
refusal names the bodies, never the load.
