## 2. The common shape **[LEAN — to test against every museum above]**

What the gathered museums already share:

- **Exhibits are things in the world**, with provenance — a chain of custody the record can verify.
- ⭐ **A label is a claim**, signed by whoever wrote it, contestable — the finds slate's rule, and the
  argument map's. A museum is an interpretation, never the world's own voice.
- **Forgery is play** — the record never certifies in-fiction documents or objects
  ([record-integrity-slate](../platform/record-integrity-slate.md) § *The interface*), so a forged exhibit
  is caught by people reading provenance, never by the engine.
- **A curator is a seat** — a person's, by the curator-seat idea; an NPC curator is the floor.
- **Teaching, never a shortcut** — the Rejection warning: a museum that hands you what the world makes you
  earn fails lens 1.
- **Whose museum** — the polity's to author (Saxonberg's doctrine), an institution's (the TPA's), or a
  player's (the curator).

## 3. Two museums from the campus design

### The technology museum — the ladder, walked through

The ladder from Muscle to Future as **one building, a room per rung**, each with its prime mover working.
The reference is Disney's *Carousel of Progress*, a rotating theater through the eras of household
technology. It is a museum, not the campus's structure — the campus itself is zones by rung
([eternal-university-slate](./eternal-university-slate.md) § 2026-11-18). **[OPEN — where it stands: on
campus, in Saxonberg, or both.]**

### The realm museum — a preview of the starting realm

A museum of the realm's **published places**, each with its rung, its trades and its landmarks, **read
from live data** — so it fills as the content builds add places and never needs the realm designed first.
⚠ **Only what is public** (paths are spoilers): undiscovered places are fogged, one click to reveal, never
prevented.
