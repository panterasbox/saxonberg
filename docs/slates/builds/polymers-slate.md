## 1. ⭐⭐ A pack is an industry; rubber was half of one **[DECIDED]**

What `trade-rubber` ships is mostly a **process** — gather a long-chain material,
**cure or set** it, **mould** it — and the process is not specific to rubber. The
[destructive-distillation slate](./destructive-distillation-slate.md) already planned
*"the `Law` extension from crosslinking to the **polymer family**"* for a plastic that
had no pack to land in. The honest industry is **polymers**; rubber stays a material
inside it.

⭐ **Polymer is the lens-1 concept**: vulcanization, the thermoset and casein hardening
are all **crosslinking** — chains tied to each other — which ships as kernel law. One
law, many materials.

**The rename** — `/trade/rubber` → `/trade/polymers` — is a deliberate build-side
sweep: the namespace, a dropped dev database (no migrations), `pnpm install`. Never a
master edit.

## 2. ⭐⭐ The natural polymers **[DECIDED]**

| material | gathered from | sets by | what it is for |
|---|---|---|---|
| **rubber** | latex (ships) | vulcanizing — sulfur crosslinking (ships) | seals, insulation, tires |
| **gutta-percha** | the latex of a tree that sets hard | cooling | ⭐ **it insulated the first undersea telegraph cables** (the Channel, 1850–51) — the post and telegraph cluster's consumer |
| **shellac** | a resin secreted by the lac insect | evaporation | varnish ([paint-slate](./paint-slate.md)), records, ⭐ **sealing wax** — the signet's wax ([gems-and-precious-metals-slate](./gems-and-precious-metals-slate.md) § 5) |
| **casein plastic** | milk protein ([dairying](../../subsystems/dairying.md)) | hardened in formaldehyde | ⭐ **buttons** — a named gap |

Each is rows on shipped mechanisms: a tap or a harvest, a set process (the
[moulding slate](./moulding-slate.md)'s list — cooling, evaporation, crosslinking), and
the mould.

## 3. The lens pass

1. **Pedagogy** — ⭐ the polymer as one idea; crosslinking as the set that makes a
   chain material elastic, hard or permanent.
2. **Expression** — a moulded object's form is the author's mould.
3. **Immersion** — the rubber works, the cable ship laying gutta-percha.
   **Participation** — the tapper, the moulder, the works.
4. **Values** — plantation latex and its labor (sugar's question, and the
   unfree-labor slate's register, again).
5. **Continuity** — natural polymers → synthetics ([plastics](./plastics-slate.md)); the
   same cure and mould.
6. **Economy** — insulation for the grid and the telegraph; buttons; varnish; seals.
7. **Governance** — plantation regulation; later, the plastics questions.
