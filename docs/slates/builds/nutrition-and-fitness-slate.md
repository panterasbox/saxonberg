## Part 1 — The place of food (settled)

Food is not one thing; it is three timescales, and the game already runs
the first.

| clock | what it answers | state |
|---|---|---|
| **hours** — satiation / hydration → endurance recovery | *eat before the march* | shipped ([metabolism.md](../../subsystems/metabolism.md)) |
| **months** — body composition | *what your life has written on your body* | half: one stock (`flesh` = fat), banded by eye |
| **years / expedition** — micronutrients | *did you provision* | punted 2026-06; neither ledger nor consequence shipped |

And the economy: eating is the one demand nobody has to be given, which
makes the food basket the base of the whole Anno/Victoria economy. The
grain chain's `eats` brain is that for one good — a purse choosing a
loaf.

### The hours clock is FINISHED, and must not grow

⭐⭐ *Never invent a need to create a market.* Hunger is a timer and the
timer already runs; making it sharper, or making meals a daily chore,
imports real life's tedium and teaches nothing. People eat for every
reason but hunger, and the engine does not measure appetite, mood,
company or habit — so it must not pretend to. **The acute clock stays
exactly as it is.**

### The months clock is where every lesson lives

What you ate and what you did, over a season, shows on you — and other
people see it before you do. That is the whole gamification of food and
fitness, and the standing rule is [measurement.md](../../measurement.md):
**no gauge; the mirror shows you.** There is no fitness number, no
body-fat readout, no "health" bar. There is a body that a room
describes (`presence`: *a heavyset man*, *a wiry woman*), clothes that
were cut to a figure you no longer have, and a hill you can now climb
without stopping.

⭐ The months clock only teaches if there is a **choice** — a menu whose
items differ in what they write on you. That is why the foodstuff gaps
(Part 7) matter on this lens and not just the economy one.

### The years clock is provisioning, not diet

Scurvy bites on a voyage, a siege, a deep expedition — a rare,
expedition-grade consequence of a provisioning decision, never daily
management. The 2026-06 timescale reasoning stands; what changed is that
the ledger can now be *derived* (Part 6).

## Part 2 — The body: two stocks, one height, derived reads (settled)

### One stock or two? Two — lens 1 chose

`flesh` is fat: months-scale, deposits on satiation surplus, wastes on
deficit ([metabolism.md](../../subsystems/metabolism.md) § the flesh
partition). A stevedore and an alderman at the same mass and stature
have the same BMI and are not the same body — and **BMI misreading the
muscular is the one thing BMI is famous for**. An engine with one stock
cannot teach the canonical lesson about the number it computes. So:

- **`flesh`** — fat. Grows from surplus. Unchanged.
- **`lean`** — muscle. Grows from **exertion + protein** (Part 3).
  Wastes without either. The protein pool that has been *"inert,
  delivering nowhere until healing is driven"* since June finally has a
  consumer.

Both are `Reserve`s on `Creature`, tier-1 stored state. A body that
predates them (or is not alive) is a no-op, the shipped `flesh` rule.

### Stature — SUPERSEDED: lineage's, not this build's

*(As captured: stature becomes a per-person field set at char-gen.)*
The survey found `race.md`/textiles sealed stature at species level
(*"individual variance arrives through `getMass()` alone"*) and
[lineage-slate](./lineage-slate.md) owns it as an endowment axis. BMI is
mass / stature² and mass already varies per person; buoyancy reads
fat/lean; the tailor's girth already moves with mass. Stature buys only
*"a tall man"* — lineage's. **Dropped here**; BMI reads the species
stature until lineage deals a personal one. ⚠ Revisit with lineage in
char-gen.

### The reads are derived and stored nowhere

All tier-2, by the physiology slate's rule — *nothing in tier 2 is
stored*:

- **BMI** = `getMass()` / stature². Computed, never a gauge; a
  physician's `assess` may say the band in words, the vitals card may
  not show a number.
- **Body density** = the fat/lean split over mass. ⭐ **First mechanical
  consumer: buoyancy** — fat floats, lean sinks — the ascent term in the
  underwater design. This is what the *"I think we need BMI on vitals"*
  ask was actually asking for, and BMI alone cannot give it.
- **The portrait** — `bodyConditionBand()` already bands `flesh` by eye;
  it gains a second axis off `lean` so *gaunt · wiry · in good flesh ·
  heavyset · fat* is one vocabulary over two stocks. The `presence`
  form reads it. **This is the mirror.**
- **Fit** — textiles' `girthIndex` moves with the body. A coat cut in
  spring sits wrong by autumn, and `measure figure` already says so
  (*"you have moved since last time"*). No new wiring: the interop is
  free the day girth reads the person.

⚠ Deliberately CANNOT express: appetite, body image, the psychology of
eating. The engine measures mass, stature and two stocks. Everything a
person feels about them is theirs.

## Part 3 — Exertion: one event, many producers (settled)

> **Every source of exertion is an event with a duration and an effort;
> the body does not know or care where it came from.**

An `exertion` is `{ durationS, effort }` — effort in real units the
engine already has (a power over a duration is joules, and `Quantity<U>`
carries both). It feeds three things and only three: the `lean` stock
(with protein, Part 2), the `wind` Discipline's transcript (Part 4), and
**the body's internal heat load** (below).

### ⭐⭐ Exertion is heat — and the seam is already being cut

The injury build (build-4, `design/harm-survey`, unmerged at capture)
adds `heatLoadJ` + `absorbHeatLoad(joules)` to `ThermalRegulation`:
*"heat the body is carrying that did not come from the weather"*, with
the note that its first consumer is magic (a frost caster absorbs what
the heat pump moved) but *"the field is not magic's: it is the seam
exertion wants next."* So it is: muscle is ~20–25 % efficient, and the
rest of every joule of effort is heat the body must shed. An `exertion`
deposits `effortJ × (1 − η)` through that one call and nothing else —
**no second thermal model**.

What falls out, with no wiring of its own:

- Shedding is sweating and costs **hydration** on the shipped
  `HEAT_SPEND_PER_DEGREE` scale — so hard work costs water *through the
  thermal path*, one scale, not a separate exertion drain.
- **Worn insulation damps shedding** — clo sits in series with the
  body's own resistance (`SHED_BODY_CLO`), so the coat that holds warmth
  in is the coat that stops work-heat getting out. Haul in a parka and
  you overheat; strip and you don't. ⭐ This is the garment ↔ thermal
  interop, and build-4 made the two insulation models agree (a layer's
  thermal block reads the garment's real `getClo()`).
- Past the **wet-bulb ceiling** or with no water left, nothing sheds:
  the labourer in a steam-filled cellar has nowhere to put the heat.
- Unshed load is a real core offset (`ΔT = Q / m·c`) and hyperthermia
  now spawns at **setpoint + 2.5 K** — heat stroke on the haul is a
  consequence the model reaches honestly, and the physician's `assess`
  reads it.

⚠ The efficiency `η` and the producers' effort figures are the dials
that decide whether ordinary work in ordinary clothes cooks people: a
400 W nude shed ceiling against a labourer's ~500 W of heat means a
working body in a coat *must* sweat harder or strip. Plausible, and
exactly what the drive has to prove rather than the suite.

### The in-game producers — all of them already exist

- **Engagements** ([activity.md](../../subsystems/activity.md)) — a quern
  that holds your `hands` for the whole grind, hauling, hewing, the
  plough. An engagement with a duration and a labour term IS an
  exertion; the producers emit it at completion.
- **Locomotion** — a `run` pace over ground, a climb, a swim. The
  traverse ripple already knows the distance and the mode.
- **Combat** — a session's exchanges.
- **The gym** — a `sports` practice, a lift, a bout; the gym is the
  place whose fixtures afford exertion *for its own sake*.

⭐ Your observation, and it is the design's centre of gravity: **most
minted vocations are blue-collar. If you are engaged with the game, your
character is already earning the signal.** Fitness is a consequence of a
life lived. Nobody needs a gym to be fit; the gym is for the person who
wants more than their trade gives them, or whose trade is a desk.

### What exertion does NOT feed

Standing. Ever. *You can't farm a god* — exertion is throughput, and
standing that accrues from throughput is the named failure of lens 4.
Where the body's excellence CAN be recognized is **sport** (Part 4).

## Part 4 — Reach: the `wind` Discipline (settled)

### Why a Discipline at all

The fitness apps fail because the reward is a badge — *a reward for
time*. For fitness to have gameplay, the reward must be a thing you can
now **do**, spent on choices. The engine already has the mechanism: a
Discipline's **band-gated conferrals** — *at or above this band, these
verbs become afforded*. That is reach, literally.

`DisciplineChannel` already has `conditioning` — *"the body adapts;
bounded and bidirectional"* — with one row, `alcohol-tolerance`, that
nothing reads. **This build makes the channel true.**

- **`wind`** — a `conditioning` Discipline. Its transcript is exertion.
  Its bands confer reach: the sustained `run`, the swim across, the
  climb without the rest, the double shift, the long haul on foot.
  Exactly which conferral sits at which band is content, authored on the
  row like every other Discipline.
- The **stocks are what the body IS; `wind` is what it can DO** —
  physiology's own split. A strong body with no wind, and a runner with
  no strength, are both expressible, which is the point of two things.

### Reach composes with FUNCTION — two axes, one read

The injury build ships the physiology slate's function half:
`BodyCapacity` (`consciousness · locomotion · manipulation · circulation
· respiration · clearance`) read in four `FUNCTION_BANDS` (`full ·
impaired · failing · lost`), *function is the minimum along the path*.
That is a different axis from conditioning and must stay one:

- **Function** is what injury takes away — derived over the parts,
  tier 2, worst-band-wins.
- **Conditioning** is what training adds — derived over the transcript,
  half-life fold.

**Reach reads both.** The sustained `run` a `wind` band confers is still
gated by `locomotion` at `full`; the swim across needs `respiration`.
The hauler with a broken leg has the wind and not the leg. ⚠ Naming:
build-4 reserves *capability* (`Archetype.CapabilitySlot`) and
*capacity* (`SlotSpec.capacity`, `BodyCapacity`) — this slate says
**reach** and nothing else for what conditioning confers.

### Conditioning decays — as a fold rule, not a ledger change

The Transcript is append-only and competence is a **derive-on-read fold**
over it ([advancement.md](../../subsystems/advancement.md)). Skill and
knowledge fold every entry; a `conditioning` Discipline folds with a
**half-life** — recent evidence counts, old evidence fades — the same
shape [participation.md](../../subsystems/participation.md) already runs
for engagement. Nothing is mutated, nothing is stored; lay off and the
band falls. ⭐ `alcohol-tolerance` becomes honest for free, and the
channel's description stops being a lie it has told since June.

### Who says so — nobody, and then the polity

Fitness is **private**. The mirror shows you; no rank, no leaderboard,
no standing. Where others say so is **sport**: a race, a match, a lift,
a bout — the `sports` Discipline that already exists, judged by peers or
a polity, conferring standing the ordinary way. *Fitness is what you
are; sport is where others say so.* The split is also what bounds
cheating (Part 5): faked exertion buys reach, never standing.

## Part 5 — The real-world bridge (settled as a seam; NOT built here)

The gamification-mirror thesis wrote the rule for home stewardship:
*build the loop against in-game input, keep an abstract input seam, the
real-world sensor is a later adapter, reward-only.* The body is the
thesis's **second instance, and a cleaner one** — the unit is already
real. A stationary bike reports watts, cadence, duration. Real exertion
→ in-game exertion is a **unit conversion**, not a metaphor, and it is
the *same event* a quern emits. One seam, two producers; the bridge is
never a second mechanism.

### The reflection is not a bike — it is a journey

Thirty real minutes at 150 W is a number of kilometres **on the game's
own roads, at the game's own pace**, and at the end of it you are
somewhere — a place with people in it. The substrate exists:
[logistics.md](../../subsystems/logistics.md)'s `Journey` is a sustained
engagement whose beat is one leg; locomotion has pace modes; a vehicle is
a room that moves. The workout has a **destination, and the destination
has a bar** — the thing no fitness app can offer, and the thing that
makes it a game.

And it generalizes with no new mechanism: a rower is a boat on the river
(the watershed is built), a treadmill is the road on foot, a
stair-climber is the shaft ladder at the mine, a bike is the road on a
bike. The device is an adapter to `exertion`; the *fiction* is chosen
from what the world already models. ⭐ **Lens 5 holds: one event, and
the vehicle is the dynamics.**

### Reward-only, stated precisely

> **The in-game sim is honest in both directions; real-world input only
> ever ADDS.**

A character who hauls all season gets lean and a lounge regular does
not — that is fiction, not surveillance. But nothing real ever
withdraws: skip the gym and your character is exactly who they were.
The game is **complete with no bike in anyone's house** (opt-in
intensity); the bridge is a second source for a signal the in-game life
already produces, for the person who wants their own body in the loop.

### What this build ships of it: the seam

`exertion` as one event with one shape, emitted by the in-game producers
and consumed by the two readers. An adapter that turns a device's
session into an `exertion` + a `Journey` is **its own slate** — it needs
a device API, an account link, and the resilience-posture treatment
(friction + daylight; detect evasion, not malice). None of that is here.
What is here is that when it comes, it is an adapter and not a rewrite.

## Part 6 — Micronutrients: the punt has expired (settled)

The 2026-06 decision was *"full ledger, curated consequences (scurvy)"*.
Neither half shipped: nutrients are four routed tags (`carb` `sugar`
`fat` `protein`, plus `water`) and a `nutrientAmounts` map on a
`Material`. What changed since is the grain chain: **`ComposedMixin`**
means a food knows its parts, and a loaf's `nutrientAmounts` on the
material row is explicitly the *fallback for a loaf that knows nothing
about what went into it*. Micronutrients are now **derivable rather than
declared**:

- **The bran fraction IS the whole-vs-white story.** A 0.72 extraction
  and a wholemeal loaf are one material with different parts; the fibre
  and the B-vitamins ride the bran. The chain already computes the thing
  that decides it — nothing is authored per loaf.
- **The citrus roster is already planted**, and nobody eats it. Scurvy's
  cure exists as bar garnish. Part 7 makes fruit food.
- **A small kind vocabulary on materials**, carried through composition
  and `BulkPayload` like the macros: a handful of kinds real data
  supports (vitamin C · iron · the B group · calcium · fibre — the
  content decides the exact list, the engine only carries it).
- **Curated deficiency `Reserve`s**, few, driven by the metabolism
  reconcile: **scurvy** the canonical (no vitamin C → a progressing
  condition, resolved by fruit), anaemia the second if iron ships. A
  deficiency is a reserve that fills from intake and drains on the
  in-session clock at an expedition timescale — the 2026-06 dial.
- **Legibility, not a scold.** The `NutritionLabel` render is the
  inspectable face; it gains the kinds. Understand the food, provision
  the voyage, watch it pay off. The same move as the energy meter.

⚠ Still deliberately absent: kcal (energy reserves stay `%`),
per-individual metabolic rates, the fuller-stomach coupling — the rest
of the metabolism tail is untouched by this slate.

## Part 7 — The menu: food as a choice (settled in principle)

The months clock teaches nothing without alternatives. The edible
roster today, read honestly: **the fruit exists for the bar** (lemon ·
lime · orange · grapefruit · cherry · grape · cranberry · juniper · mint
· olive — nobody eats an orange); grain → bread ✓; roots → cooking ✓;
corpse → butcher → cuts ✓; egg · milk · fat ✓; salt · sugar · syrup ✓.

The gaps, ranked by *a producer that should be producing* (the dirty-
reason test the wire suite already codifies):

| gap | state | where it lands |
|---|---|---|
| **dairy** — `milk` is a row with NO consumer; the `eats` basket names cheese | the byre produces it, nothing takes it | ⭐ **the next ranching phase, after the pets build ships** — not here |
| **fruit as food** | rows exist, one recipe (`stewed-orchard-fruit`) | cheapest gap: rows, no code — the first micronutrient consumer, ships with Part 6 |
| **honey** | *Apis mellifera* is a species row; no material | rides dairy's ranching phase |
| **legumes / greens** | none | farming Stage B |
| **poultry / pork** | species + eggs ship; meat only via `stew-meat` | the butcher's per-species yield tables (designed) |
| **fish** | designed, unmerged | fishing build |
| **slices** — a loaf is one portion, all-or-nothing | the whole kitchen family | cooking-slate's tail; **the top kitchen item** |

⚠ This slate does not build a food pack. It builds the *reason the menu
matters*; the packs land on their own chains.

## Part 9 — Build shape & sequencing

Kernel-led; first consumer is **the body every existing character
already has** — the miner, the baker, the `eats` cast — which is what
makes it not premature: the producers exist and are running.

- **W0 — the body.** Per-individual stature (char-gen + dossier +
  `Species` fallback); the `lean` reserve; the portrait vocabulary over
  two stocks; BMI and body-density reads; the tailor's girth moves to
  the person. Acceptance: `measure figure` on two characters of one
  species at different masses records different girths; a `look` at a
  wasted body and a fleshy one reads differently with no authored line.
- **W1 — exertion.** The `exertion` event; the engagement, locomotion
  and combat producers emit it; `lean` grows from exertion + protein
  and wastes without. Acceptance: a season of hauling and a season in
  the lounge produce two different portraits from one starting body.
- **W2 — reach.** The `wind` Discipline row + conferrals; the
  time-weighted conditioning fold; `alcohol-tolerance` folds the same
  way. Acceptance: a `run` a fresh character cannot sustain, a hauler
  can; stop hauling and the band falls.
- **W3 — micronutrients.** The kind vocabulary on materials, carried
  through `ComposedMixin` and `BulkPayload`; the fruit rows become food;
  scurvy as a reserve + condition; the label gains the kinds.
  Acceptance: a wholemeal and a white loaf from one wheat nourish
  measurably differently; a long haul on bread alone ends in scurvy, and
  an orange ends the scurvy.
- **Deferred seams:** the device adapter + the `Journey` binding (its
  own slate, after the seam has been driven in-game); anaemia and the
  rest of the deficiency roster (content against the shipped kind
  vocabulary); the gym as a *place* (a locality archetype whose fixtures
  afford exertion for its own sake — ⚠ combat's "gym" is the balance
  bench, `test:gym`, not a room; no precedent exists).

### Risks

- **Build-4 is unmerged.** `heatLoadJ` / `absorbHeatLoad` and
  `BodyCapacity` are read off `design/harm-survey` at capture; the
  requirements pass re-reads them off master. If that build lands first
  (it should — it is the substrate this one composes on), W1's heat
  consumer and W2's function gate are one call each. If it does not,
  the heat consumer waits; nothing else here depends on it.
- **The heat dial.** η and the producers' effort figures decide whether
  a shift in a coat is sweat or heat stroke (Part 3). Drive it.

### Open questions

None load-bearing — reach closed the fork. Two for the requirements
conversation: **the exact `wind` conferral ladder** (which verbs, which
bands — content, but the first three should be chosen with the drive in
mind), and **whether `lean` reads protein from the existing inert pool or
the pool is retired into it** (engineering; the plan's).
