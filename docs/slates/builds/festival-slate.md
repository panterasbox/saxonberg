## 0. Trade is the mechanism, the holiday is the expression

The pack ships what festivals are made of. A holiday is somebody pointing at
it: today a proprietor holding a fair on their own ground, later a polity
recognizing a day. **No holidays ship.** This is the same split as every trade
pack: the mechanism is ours, the expression is the locality's.

## 1. ⭐⭐⭐ The show — measured classes and judged classes **[DECIDED — lens 1]**

Entries are goods or animals, entered in **classes** printed in a published
**schedule** (*"Cheese, hard, aged over six months"*; *"Ewe, any breed, shearling"*).

- **Measured classes** — heaviest marrow, longest fleece, the biggest cheese.
  An instrument decides; no judge is needed.
- **Judged classes** — best cheese, best ale, best ewe. A **person** reads
  grade, and their competence decides how *finely* they can tell entries apart
  ([instrumentation.md](../../subsystems/instrumentation.md): *competence
  resolves detail, never access*). A novice judge sees two cheeses as "fine" and
  "fine" and must separate them on something else; a master separates them.

The distinction taught is **quantity against quality**. ⭐ **Every shipped chain
feeds the show with no trade code** — grade is already on cheese, ale, cloth,
bread, wine, honey, and the herdbook already holds the animals.

**Lens 7.** The judge judges goods, but a ribbon is standing earned by a
person: the **criterion** is the schedule, the **appeal** is the show
committee, and the ribbon is a **deed** in the chronicle. A bent judge is
possible because the judge is a person — the polity doing something we did not
want, at the smallest stakes there are.

## 2. ⭐⭐ The showground buds for the day **[DECIDED]**

No permanent rooms that sit empty 359 days a year. A **showground kind** —
village fete, county show, hiring fair — buds its places on authored common
ground for the event's window and is struck after: the same budding machinery
as the construction site ([construction-slate](./construction-slate.md) § 2)
and holding's keyed instances.

| showground kind | its places |
|---|---|
| village fete | the green · the tea tent · the produce tent · the maypole ring |
| county show | the show ring (animals paraded) · the produce and craft tents · the stockyard pens · the trade stands · the beer tent |
| hiring fair | the market square · the hiring line · the stalls |

## 3. A festival is a row; declaring it is the landholder's act **[DECIDED]**

A festival row names **what** (its showground kind, its schedule of classes, its
program), **where** (the ground), and **when** — a date, or ⭐ a **natural anchor**
(*"when the orchard blooms"*), because a natural season is derived from the
simulation and never authored as a date. **Whoever holds the ground declares
it**: a proprietor, the co-op, a town committee. When a polity exists, its
holiday points at a festival row.

## 5. Who comes

- **The cast** — NPCs read a festival as a task with its own urgency, so on the
  day the whole cast is in one room ([settlement-model](../../settlement-model.md)
  § 11: *"how a player learns who lives somewhere … the cheapest possible
  ceremony"*).
- **Players** — the festival is on the news ticker ([press.md](../../subsystems/press.md));
  where players meet each other, not one at a time.

## 6. The economy

- **Stall rents** to whoever holds the ground; **entry fees**; **the prize
  purse**, which a brand may sponsor.
- **A winner sells for more** — the ribbon is legible to buyers that afternoon.
- ⭐ **The showmen** — booth operators who travel the fair circuit: the first
  business that moves. The settlement-mobility cluster starts here.
- **Later builds hook in** — the hiring fair (seasonal labor) and the horse
  fair (the horse trade), when those builds exist.

## 7. The first venue — Bloom at Heart's Delight

The settlement model: *"Bloom is already begging to be one — three weeks when
the valley is a postcard, Rovere's is full, and people come up from the city
just to look."* The valley's dairy, orchard, livestock and brewing give the show
classes on day one. Its anchor is natural: Bloom is when the orchard blooms.

**The drive, in one line:** you enter your cheese in the Bloom show; the judge
reads the class and you take second; the rosette is in your inventory and the
deed is in your chronicle; the winner's cheese sells for more at the stall that
afternoon.

## 8. Lens pass

1. **Pedagogy** — measured against judged; what a grade *is*; a judge's eye as
   competence. Decided § 1.
2. **Expression** — a festival is rows: a schedule, a showground kind, a
   program; any locality's own fair is content, no code.
3. **Immersion** — the whole cast in one room; the masquerade. **Participation**
   — the judge's seat; the bent judge.
4. **Values** — who may enter; whether a sponsor's purse buys influence over
   the classes.
5. **Continuity** — the same show across epochs: the classes change, the act
   of judging does not.
6. **Economy** — stall rents, entry fees, the purse, the premium on a winner,
   the traveling showmen. The demand was there first: every graded good wants
   a buyer who can tell.
7. **Governance** — the schedule as criterion, the committee as appeal, the
   ribbon as a deed; the holiday left to a polity.
