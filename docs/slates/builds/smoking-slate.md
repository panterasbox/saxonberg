# Smoking slate — tobacco, cannabis, and the smoke in the room

> **Status: UNBUILT** — designed at high level 2026-10-22 with the owner (*"I wanna design
> smoking, tobacco and cannabis and anything else adjacent"*). What ships: metabolism's
> toxin model — alcohol as the worked example: a dose, one banded `intoxicated` condition,
> an `alcohol-tolerance` stock with a half-life ([metabolism.md](../../subsystems/metabolism.md),
> [exertion.md](../../subsystems/exertion.md)); `ingest`, which only knows swallowing; fire;
> respiration's `inhale`; cleanliness's residues. The breathing model this rides is
> [breath-control-slate](./breath-control-slate.md).
> **Left:** inhalation as a route · nicotine and THC as substance rows · dependence (tolerance,
> craving, withdrawal, free recovery) · lungs through `wind` and the silent exposure stock ·
> smoke in the room and witness consent · the social rituals and venues · the tobacco and
> cannabis chains and the kit · governance (excise, prohibition, advertising, the
> constitutional case) · the verb collisions
> **Size:** a build

## 0. Why this one is loaded **[EVIDENCE]**

The project's own governance texts already name tobacco twice:

- the [draft constitution](../../compact/governance/draft-constitution.md)'s recorded
  weakness — in 1955 America smokers wanted cigarettes, workers needed the jobs and capital
  saw a bull market, so all three chambers agree and the harm passes: *"the one harm this
  structure cannot filter … Nothing currently addresses it"*;
- [measurement.md](../../measurement.md)'s archetype of the attention company — *"we impose
  nothing … Tobacco gave it."*

This is not just another trade; it is **the worked example of the project's own unsolved
problem**. This build would be the first content to **exercise** it — the slate says so and
does not claim to solve it.

## 1. ⭐⭐ Inhalation is a route, and the route teaches **[DECIDED]**

Same body, same dose model; the timing differs:

| route | onset | duration | the lesson |
|---|---|---|---|
| smoked | minutes | short | you can feel your way — stop when it is enough |
| ⭐ eaten (edibles, through `ingest`) | 1–2 hours | long | **the classic edible overdose: nothing happens, so you take more** — derivable from the route alone |
| chewed, snuffed | minutes, through mouth or nose | medium | |

## 2. Substances are rows on the existing model **[DECIDED]**

- **Nicotine** — a short half-life (about two hours); mild alertness, appetite down, calm
  under stress — the sibling of trait's *cope-drinking* stress spiral; strong dependence.
- **THC** — an intoxication band like alcohol's (perception, precision, time sense);
  appetite **up**, which reaches metabolism's hunger; relaxation; mild dependence.

## 3. ⭐⭐ Dependence — felt, never punishing **[LEAN]**

- **Tolerance** is a stock, like alcohol's.
- **Craving** is a [wellbeing](./wellbeing-slate.md) discomfort channel — *wanting is not
  liking* (Berridge), already in that slate's vocabulary.
- **Withdrawal** is real discomfort, always soft.
- **Recovery is free** — measurement's mean reversion: *cheap to look different this week,
  expensive to be different.*

Leaving dependence out would make tobacco pure upside — the dishonest version.

## 4. ⭐⭐ Harm — felt where it would be, silent where it is **[LEAN]**

- ⭐ **The lungs: smoking lowers the `wind` stock** — the smoker runs out of breath on the
  climb. Felt, deterministic, real.
- ⭐ **The hidden risk** — cancer and its kin as a **silent stock of accumulated exposure**
  no sense reports: the shape of spoilage's silent contamination, seeded and dose-driven,
  **never a dice roll** ([uncertainty.md](../../uncertainty.md)). Only a later-epoch
  instrument (the X-ray) reads it. The real lesson of smoking — **the harm you cannot feel
  until it is too late** — and the [radioactivity](./radioactivity-slate.md) slate's.

## 5. ⭐⭐⭐ Smoke is matter in the room **[DECIDED]**

Smoking indoors acts on everyone present: you smell it, it stains the walls (the lounge bar's
own description has the old cigarette burns), and it can start a fire (falling asleep with a
lit pipe — [fire.md](../../subsystems/fire.md)).

- **Secondhand smoke is breathed passively** ([breath-control](./breath-control-slate.md)
  § 6), so smoking indoors acts on the room — ⭐ **it needs the room's consent**, as the
  [expressive acts](./expressive-acts-slate.md) witness rule: *"mind if I smoke?"* becomes a
  real question.
- **No-smoking signs and smoking rooms** are authorable place norms — enable authors, never
  impose.
- **Blowing smoke in a face** is a directed act on the consent ledger (breath-control § 5).

## 6. The social layer — most of why people smoke **[DECIDED]**

- **Rituals** — offering a cigarette is a gift; lighting someone's is an intimate small act;
  ⭐ passing a joint is the gift loop going round the circle; the smoke break is a pause in a
  work shift; the after-dinner cigar is a status ritual.
- **Venues** — the smoking room, the cigar club, the coffeehouse, the hookah lounge, the den.
- ⭐ **The Cuban *lector*** — rolling floors paid someone to read newspapers and novels aloud
  to the rollers: a trade that brought literacy to a factory floor.

## 7. The chains **[DECIDED]**

| chain | steps | connects to |
|---|---|---|
| **tobacco** | grow → harvest leaves → cure (air, fire or flue) → ferment → age → cut or roll → the tobacconist | [farming](./farming-slate.md) (tobacco is a missing plantation crop); [maturation](../../subsystems/maturation.md) (the curing barn) |
| **cannabis** | grow → flower → dry → cure → hashish (resin) | ⭐ **the same plant as hemp fiber** ([cordage](../tails/cordage-slate.md)) — one field, two varieties |
| **the kit** | clay pipes ([pottery](./pottery-slate.md)) · briar · papers ([paper](./paper-and-printing-slate.md)) · ⭐ matches ([chemical works](./chemical-works-slate.md) — white phosphorus, *phossy jaw*, the 1888 London match-girls' strike) · ashtrays · lighters | |
| **money** | ⭐ colonial Virginia ran on **tobacco as currency**, with tobacco notes as warehouse receipts | [banking](../../subsystems/banking.md) — cash against the ledger |

**Through the epochs** (lens 5): pipe → cigar → hand-rolled cigarette → ⭐ the Bonsack rolling
machine (1881, which made cigarettes cheap — [machines](./machines-slate.md)) → the vape
(electricity). The act stays the same. Cannabis: smoked → eaten → tinctured → vaped.

## 8. ⭐⭐ Governance **[DECIDED as the polity's]**

- **Excise taxes and tax stamps** — measurement's three layers (the engine counts, the
  polity taxes); Pigou's tax on a harm.
- **Prohibition or regulation is the polity's choice** (title-level), with smuggling
  ([cover and concealment](./cover-and-concealment-slate.md),
  [deception and detection](./deception-and-detection-slate.md)) as its consequence; the 1937
  Act's sweep of industrial hemp is the cautionary lesson.
- **Advertising** — the attention-company link.
- ⭐⭐ **The constitutional case** — a realm where everyone smokes, the growers need the trade
  and capital profits is exactly what the three chambers cannot filter (§ 0). This build
  exercises it and records what happens; it does not pretend to fix it.

## 9. Verbs, and the collisions **[LEAN]**

- **`inhale [from X]`** and **`light`** (already an alias of `ignite`) carry smoking —
  [breath-control](./breath-control-slate.md) § 3.
- ⚠ **`smoke` is trade-cooking's** (smoke a ham). By the collision ladder, either unify —
  `smoke <thing>` dispatches on what the thing is — or let smoking ride `inhale`; the lean is
  `inhale` plus `light`.
- ⚠ **`snuff` is an alias of `douse`** (snuff a candle) and **`pack`** is the content-pack
  author verb — taking snuff and packing a pipe need other words, or a collision decision.

## 10. Scope **[LEAN]**

- **Plantation tobacco and unfree labor** — named as [sugar](./sugar-slate.md) named it,
