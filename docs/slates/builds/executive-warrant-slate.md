## The object

An **executive warrant** is the regulated form of the hole: the Prime
Minister (the seat, never a person) asks for authority over **one named
extent** — a parcel a committee holds — for **a stated reason**, for **a
bounded time**. While it stands, the holder of the seat passes the
committee's title checks for that extent and nothing else. It is the
[agency slate](./agency-slate.md)'s grant (*UseGrant-shaped · scoped ·
revocable*) with the executive as agent and the committee as the
principal that did not consent — which is exactly why it carries the
four regulations below that an ordinary agency does not need.

| property | the rule |
|---|---|
| **scoped** | one extent, by path; `can` resolves it beside the title, nearest-parcel-wins untouched for everybody else |
| **stated** | the reason is authored at the request and is part of the record |
| **expiring** | a game-time expiry is on the warrant; after it the hole closes by itself — no revoke needed for the ordinary case |
| **recorded** | the request, the grant and every act under it land in the governance record (the chronicle deed of the seat's holder, and the committee's channel) |
| **told** | the overridden committee is notified at grant and at expiry — its members can read what was done under the warrant |
| **reviewable** | the legislature or the courts may revoke it early, and that too is recorded; a warrant is a thing a bill or a judgment can name |

What it is **not**: a standing power, an all-extents power, or a title.
A warrant confers nothing a title confers beyond the extent and the
window; it never conveys code trust (the wizard axis is unaffected
either way — see the cleanup plan's D2).

## Why a seam and not this build

The cleanup's job was to delete what pretended to be this. The honest
replacement needs the agency grant first (the principal/agent split in
the execution context, the record shape carrying both identities — the
agency slate's *Left*), and a governance record the legislature can
act on. Until then the executive holds the platform's own extents and
sits on no committee, and anything a drive needs is seated honestly
(`committees:` on the test login — the cleanup plan's D13).
