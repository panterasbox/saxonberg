**Defects**
- TitleBuyController L233 then L247: money settles BEFORE `ParcelApi.subdivide`, which throws (`ParcelRegistry.ts` L325 floor-area ceiling, L349 land-use area band). A refused subdivide leaves the buyer charged with no title, contradicting the header's "a failed payment leaves no parcel". The fix is to check first or roll back.
- ButcherController L387-390 and L409: `exemplars[i]` is indexed by `species.getButcheryYield()`, while `dressed` comes from `Species.dressOut`, which DROPS lines (the `maxAgeDays` age gate and `kg < 0.05`). Once a line is dropped the indexes misalign, so `butcher X for <cut>` matches the wrong line's keywords and `stemOf` names the wrong cut.
- ButcherController: the `vessel` arg (`butcher.yaml`, with a default) is bound and never read (only L154 declares it). The blood catch is dead, and "absent means it spills" is never true either way. `blade` and `tools` also share the prepositions `[with, using]`.
- UnprovisionController L193-208: unlike Unlease (`ChattelApi.evictToStorage`, D9) it never moves the tenant's goods to storage before `dropUnit({revert})` + `deleteAllFor(unit)`. A dorm tenant's placed goods are probably lost. Verify against the furnishing estate slice.
- StallController L299-312: if the giver is not a Container, the counter's goods are never handed back and are destructed with the counter.
- SawController: the pit-saw path never calls `setSawing(true)`, so two sawyers can run one pit saw at once. The loser's `finishSaw` returns silently on `log.isDestroyed()` after the effort is spent, with no message.

**Hard cases**
- Cast/Recharge/Zap/Recite pass through engine prose (`refusal`, `reports`). Is a pre-worded refusal from a magic Api a rule-2 breach the replacement test should count against the controller, or against the Api only?
- Maintain picks HOUSE/HULL words by `instanceof Ship`. Is a per-host vocabulary presentation (it stays in the controller) or a protocol the host answers (`kitNoun()`)? The test can't say, because the words also pick the Discipline.
- Saw's water-mill path runs the act on the world clock with nobody engaged, while the pit path engages the actor. Is "who the act holds" one act with a host-supplied slot, or two acts?
- PreserveController's `recipeFor` decides "which recipe this act is" from the target. Is that a choice about asking (default-picking) or a fact of the world (the recipe the matter admits)?

**Rules (new)**
- (12) *A bound arg is never re-narrowed by class in the controller. If the view cannot express the kind, the gap is a view predicate, not an `instanceof`.* Evidence: Wind/Adjust re-narrow under `requires: [affords]`, plus Tally `instanceof CrossingLog`, Sever `instanceof LineAccess`, TitleBookBase `instanceof DeedDesk`.
- (13) *Twin controllers across packs mean a missing substrate act.* Provision≈Lease, Unprovision≈Unlease, Hall≈Letting: test = two packs carry the same sequence of kernel calls. Evidence: `ascentRefusal` duplicated verbatim in TitleBuy L336 and Lease L149.
- (14) *A controller that consumes money before a refusable world write has ordered a rule.* Test: any `settle`/`transfer` followed by a call that can throw. Evidence: TitleBuy L233→L247.
- (15) *A controller never reaches for template rows or duck-types its subject* (`Template.findByPath`, `getX?.()`, local `View` interfaces). Test: a cast to an inline interface means the method belongs on that class. Evidence: Unlease `floorplanOf`, Lease/Unlease `BuildingView`, Bore `getPositions?.()`.
