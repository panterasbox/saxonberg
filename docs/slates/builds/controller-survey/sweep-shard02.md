**Defects**
- TransfuseController 128-153 and 77-82: the completion runs `this.*` on a destructed controller (the corpse scar that DrillingActController 14-21 and GroundWorkController 147-155 document). On the engaged path the blood goes in, but the player gets no narration or credit, and no harm or revival is recorded.
- LineController 211 + `line.yaml` liner `default: "me:i"`, `requires: TangibleMixin`: the liner is never validated (`LINER_WORD` is dead code at 266), so `line` destroys whatever carried item binds first.
- MordantController 388-419: mordanting consumes no material and needs no bath; the mordant is a free string.
- BleedController 43-95: (a) no consent gate when the donor is another body; (b) the `with` phlebotomy tool is never required; (c) on a non-empty vessel it adds `UNIT_LITRES` with no capacity check.
- CharController 69-79: any `attention`-slot engagement short-circuits to "adjusting" this clamp. 64: the draught is written before the refusals. 155: a non-engaged actor completes the 3-day burn instantly.
- FellController 210 and 230 (and RiveController 126): endurance is spent before the engagement starts or the plan can refuse.
- Possible: LayController 24/62-68 fills a trap with fresh water whatever the reach. GlazeController 40 silently overwrites existing glazing. DonateController 75 says "your blood" for anyone's unit.

**Hard cases**
- PloughController `draughtAvailable`: who is pulling is a world fact, but no object owns "presence in the traces" (haulage owns hitching). The question for a rule: when the honest answer is "whatever is standing here", which object owns the reading?
- FellController 173-183: `fell oak` binds an oak LOG, and the controller discards the binding in favor of the raw word. Is overriding the binder with `raw` parsing, or a rule about what "fell" can mean?
- IceActController 50 and `bestInstrument` (Groze/Scribe/Muddle): when several tools are bound, is picking one a choice about asking (the controller's) or a rule (the plan's)?
- CharController and Bore: one verb whose act is decided by world state (lit vs unlit). Is "same words, different act by state" a `split` or a `protocol`?
- HouseFreightController `buysFor()[0]`: with several houses, is picking one a default about asking or a fact?

**Rules (new)**
- R12 *A controller writes nothing before its last refusal.* Any world write (cost, setter, stamp) before a refusal is a partial act. Evidence: Fell 210/230 `spend` before `engageStep`/`engagePlan`; Char 64 `setDraught` before the empty-clamp/no-fuel refusals.
- R13 *An arg the view binds and nothing reads is a lie to the player.* The player names the instrument and the game ignores it, so either the object checks it or the view drops it. Evidence: Bleed `with`, Transfuse `syringe` (44-47 "does not read it"), Prescribe `pad` (32-39), Test, Line `liner`.
- R14 *Acting on another body is a consent rule owned by that body.* `named ?? giver` makes "self or other" a controller default, and the permission lives nowhere. Evidence: Bleed/Set/Suture/Test/Transfuse 43-45-style; Bleed has no consent gate at all.
- R15 *A verb that is another verb plus a hand-off composes the two; it doesn't copy the first.* Evidence: ServeController 39-60 is MixController 27-48 plus a `move(drink, patron)`.
- R16 *A completion closure may close over values, never over `this`.* This is sharper than rule 3: the scar is documented in two bases and still shipped in Transfuse 80-81 and 138-152. A lint (`this.` inside an `onComplete`/`effect` arrow) would catch it.
