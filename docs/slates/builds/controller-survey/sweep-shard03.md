**Defects**
- Drift L210-212 and Shore L130: the cell's `holder` is `giver.getTemplatePath()`. Every player avatar shares one templatePath, so `MineWarren.carved[*].holder` records every player as the same person (the person-key antipattern; `lint:person-keys` misses it because it is not a `ParcelOwner`).
- Mill L185-205 / L446-455: the water-mill path never takes the grain. It stays in the player's hands for game-minutes, the charge was fixed at dispatch, and `finishGrind` skips the consume when the source is already destroyed but still mints the flour. Eat, sell or destroy the sack mid-grind and the flour is free.
- Assay L147-176/L371: the samples are never handed over. A queued or field batch destroys them wherever they are at completion (even after a sale), and the papers land in the dispatch room. L141/L284: `onShiftHere` exempts an employee of *any* business from the fee. L273 `staffedBy`: any on-shift employee in the room counts as this bench's assayer.
- Draft: no keeper gate (its sibling Move has `mayMove`). Anyone can draft a head out of any herd into their own room. Slaughter: no ownership or blade check, so anyone can kill another's stock. Breed L205-290: no sex or male check, so a bull "takes".
- Drain L239-280: `no-gas`/`not-sealed` are discovered only after the 30 s pump.
- Clay L33: the structured reason collapses to `'refused'`. Stake L141-143: says "not yours to record over" even when the overlapping block is the giver's own.

**Hard cases**
- Hew `pickFace`: picking the richest seam when no face is named. Is it a default about asking (presentation) or which-thing-the-act-is-about (rule)? It needs grade, a world fact the player may not have read. Question: may a default choose by a hidden value?
- MiningActController `groundPermits`: are the `0.5`/`4` thresholds only explaining (rule 9), or a second copy of Working's stability rule that could drift?
- HouseShop `stock`: a screen's signed-in principal decides which house's sheet is shown. That is permission, not presentation, and it sits in the controller.
- Pack controllers narrow by `instanceof PackClass` (Couch, Compose, Copy, Print, Clay, Unwind) because the view can `requires:` only a mixin. Can a view require a pack class, or is the narrowing doomed to live here?

**Rules (new)**
- R12 *A deferred act takes custody at dispatch.* If the effect lands after `execute` returns, the matter it consumes must move into the instrument (hopper, bin, bench) or be reserved then, or it can be spent twice. Evidence: Mill L189, Assay L147.
- R13 *A gate one sibling has, every sibling over the same record needs, so it lives on the record.* Evidence: Move L144 `mayMove` vs Draft/Breed/Slaughter (none).
- R14 *The "module function, because the controller is dead by then" comment is a detector.* Every completion closure in a controller file (`finishGrind`, `winOre`, `cutHeading`, `standTimber`, `finishAssay`, `crack`, `consolidate`) is the act's effect living in the wrong file. Seven files in this shard carry that warning. The object should return the plan, and its completion should run on the object.
