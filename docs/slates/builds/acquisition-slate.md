# Acquisition slate — capability onboarding, the credential itinerary, the update economy

**Captured 2026-07-28** from a deep-drill session inside the
demo-content requirements cycle (the Shallows-clinic pause →
"acquisition is the heart of onboarding"). This slate is the
doctrine + rulings; it **supersedes the onboarding slate's Health
Center / TPA-update beat** (stale since the credential-wallet
migration) and re-threads the first-login journey over the built
campus. Companion sessions running in parallel: city government
(the government quarter — forums venue, citizenship), guilds.

## The doctrine (three rules)

1. **The floor is reachability, not popularity.** Born-with =
   what *others* need you to have: attunement (implant at intake,
   or species-intrinsic — parity is architectural), **comms**
   (talk/emote/DM — the world must be able to reach you),
   **wallet-as-capacity** (institutions must be able to issue to
   you; it confers nothing empty). Plus a physical **TravelCard**
   in hand at intake. That is the entire floor.
2. **Everything else is sought — and the seeking is the
   discovery.** In a text world there is no icon grid; a born-with
   capability is invisible. Acquisition solves discoverability:
   the capability surfaces at the moment of felt need, the errand
   is the tutorial, the issuer's counter is the documentation.
   Ceremony that teaches is interface; only ceremony that teaches
   nothing is waste. Discovery is social by construction —
   capability use is witnessed (someone checks market prices off
   the air; you ask), word of mouth first, Limen's nudges and
   corpo signage on top.
3. **The sorting rule.** **Updates** are capabilities exercised
   from anywhere over the aether (comms, forums — real player-side
   software). **Credentials** are authorizations presented to
   machinery and institutions (travel, payment, keys,
   certificates, deputization) — the capability lives in the
   terminal/counter/door; you carry standing, not software. The
   TPA question dissolves here: teleport capability is the
   *terminal's*; the travel credential is pure authorization +
   registered-set data; no update exists or is needed.

## Rulings (2026-07-28, all user-decided)

- **Forums leaves the free bundle** — acquired at the government
  quarter (exact venue = the city-government session's). The
  loadout change: `installDefaultLoadout` stops injecting
  `ForumsUpdate`.
- **Hiring requires the payment credential** — "bring your
  account number": employment presents it at hire, making the
  bank visit need-fired in every player's week one.
- **No physical conferral certificate** — wallet record only
  (nobody would care; unit 11 of demo-content already record-only).
- **Dorm access digitizes now** — the key becomes a `dorm-key`
  wallet record Katie registers at the handover; `presentsKey`
  learns wallet-record presentation; no physical key object. The
  moments list already said it: "the door knows you now."
- **TravelCard stays physical at intake** (the terminal works from
  minute one; the card makes credentials tangible).
  **Digitization is the TPA bureau's act and is optional
  convenience** — deliberately NOT a journey stop; the first live
  demonstration of seek-when-you-care.
- **No NPC DMs shoved at newcomers** — the lounge landing stays
  human (the crowd points you on; bar staff cover quiet hours;
  signage/departures board carry mechanics). Limen's first
  contact is at campus entry, after Gus. Hard requirement on the
  Limen build: **replying to Limen's DM must work** (the
  model-backed brain answers), or the first private message
  teaches futility.

## The rosters

**Updates** (few, each a genuine aether service, arriving as its
service arrives): comms (floor) · forums (acquired, civic) ·
future candidates passing the anywhere-test: streaming receiver,
news/bulletin subscription, market-data client, document-storage
tiers, MQL query client. Priced where the service is real —
recurring via banking Terms. **Sink note:** only NPC-institution
fees actually sink money; player-run aether services circulate it
(both wanted; only the first fights inflation). Player-run aether
services are the CS-wishbook royalties fantasy growing into a
category.

**Credentials** (the itinerary — need-fired, order-free):

| travel | TPA — card at intake; bureau digitizes | leaving the lounge (minute one) |
| dorm-key | Housing (Katie, Duncan lobby) | journey's end — the first *issuance* the player witnesses |

**Hardware** (= healthcare; install/remove is surgery per the
vitals slate): **replace** (prosthetics — care venues: Health
Center, Terraces hospital, Goodkin's clinic) · **add/enhance**
(elective — the Aevex augment clinic, priced, a clean NPC sink) ·
manufacture: Aevex (the standard intake implant is civic issue
under Aevex contract — the one free piece, because reachability).
The procedure is real: appointment, table, brief honest recovery.
Designed space: the Gray's back-alley chop shop (discount, actual
risk) — future. Born-attuned parity: no implant beat; hardware
slots are body-plan facts; everything else identical.

## The first-login journey v2 (supersedes the onboarding slate's route)

1. **Intake** — implant issued (born-attuned: skipped), comms
   live, wallet empty, TravelCard in hand.
2. **The lounge** — human landing: people point you on; bar NPCs
   cover quiet hours; present the card at the terminal (first
   credential *use*).
3. **The crossing** — **Gus formally absorbs the greeter role**
   (no separate greeter carve; his sheet gains the first-login
   ritual variant). First stranger, first introduction, the log.
4. **The gate, north** — the sky-flip, earned on foot. Eternal
   Way → the Quad (statue, fountain, the wrong light). **Limen's
   first contact here**, post-Gus. **Silver Street visible and
   deliberately bypassed** — the registrar's hall stays sought
   (the doctrine applied to our own content).
5. **Limbo Lane → Duncan** — **Katie's handover is the
   acquisition lesson**: first credential issuance, the door
   knows you now. (The lesson the Health Center used to carry,
   relocated to the beat that owned its moment.)
6. **The room → customization** — the scoped-authoring climax,
   unchanged.

**The Health Center leaves the journey** (its TPA-update job is
extinct three ways: no update exists; digitization is the TPA's;
surgery isn't a walk-in demo). It enters life need-driven like
any clinic. **Week one is the itinerary, need-fired, no
checklist**: job → bank → first-wage beat; conferral → summons →
ceremony; forum curiosity → the quarter; subscriptions, augments,
the guild choice each in their moment. Onboarding dissolves;
Limen goes quiet.
