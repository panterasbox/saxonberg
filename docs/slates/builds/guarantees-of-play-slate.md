## 0. The ask **[DECIDED — the owner]**

Four protections collected through the 2026-10-28 conversation — the comms floor, confinement limits,
emergency care beyond banishment, and a cap on banishing from a crossroads — with the question *which
rights does the platform guarantee before the convention?* The owner agreed to all three answers:

1. **Guarantees of play are Tier B**, separate from the convention's civil rights.
2. **The list** (P1–P9, measurement.md).
3. **Enforced by code first**, the court keeping judgment cases.

## 1. Why Tier B, and why not rights **[DECIDED — the owner]**

The draft constitution's Art. IX leaves civil rights to the convention, *"for the rights a people hold
against their own polity are theirs to choose, not to receive."* These are not that. Each stops **a
local rule from taking away the ability to play your character** — stranding, silencing, spending
your play, denying rescue — which is a promise about what the product *is*. Promised by whoever ships
the code, checked by the AGPL right to fork, and liftable to constitutional rank by the convention.

## 2. What the build does **[LEAN]**

Every guarantee becomes a **wall in the substrate** — the declaration cannot express the violation,
so no court is needed for the common case. Each refusal names the guarantee, so a committee learns
the floor by meeting it.

| # | the wall |
|---|---|
| P1 passage | a presence banishment's extent excludes the way out to the nearest public route home; the banished may cross, never stop |
| P2 comms | an exclusion or a sentence can never name the aether; a gaol's cell keeps the carrier's reach |
| P3 play | a gaol term counts only online and only when the convict chose it from the menu; custody past the limit releases; only a seat's custody survives logout |
| P4 rescue | no exclusion gates `treat`, `carry` or the clinic for someone in the dying window |
| P5 characters | no local declaration may name an account |
| P8 labor | no register may name a player |
| P9 logout | nothing refuses a logout |

P6 (death) and P7 (a defendant for every NPC) are kept by their own builds.

## 3. The judgment cases **[LEAN]**

For the Compact court: whether a long presence banishment from a crossroads, passage intact, is still
too much · whether a gaol's conditions defeat P3 in spirit · whether an off-grid place is a person's
home (P2's *where you live*).

## 4. The lens pass

1. **Pedagogy** — the floor is legible: every refusal names it.
2. **Expression** — authors keep harsh content; only these few things are off the table.
3. **Immersion** — innocent passage and rescue are real law. **Participation** — nobody is removed
   from play by a local rule.
4. **Values** — the medium's scarce thing, a player's time and community, is protected.
5. **Continuity** — the guarantees hold in every epoch and every realm.
6. **Economy** — a banishment still costs a place's economy; it never costs the game.
7. **Governance** — the convention can raise any of these to constitutional rank, never lower them
   below Tier B without a fork.

## 5. Open
