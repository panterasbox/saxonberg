# Cooperative effort — several people, one device, and the synchrony matters

> **Status: UNBUILT** — and ⭐ it is the ONE genuinely new mechanism the
> vessel pass turned up, after a spatial primitive was proposed, refused,
> and found to be a shape rather than a mechanism.
> **Left:** the **pooled draw** (N × `exert` into one device) · what the
> device reads — **the sum**, and ⭐ **the synchrony** · the failure
> (*out of time*, not *too weak*) · who calls the stroke · and the
> `Drivable` widening from one driver to **N propellers**.
> **Size:** a tail — `exertion.md` ships the per-body half.

Designed 2026-10-09 out of
[navigable-water-slate](./navigable-water-slate.md) § 7a. ⭐ It arrived by
subtraction: an intra-space linear-position primitive ("the strip") was
proposed for a whaleboat's stations and **refused by the owner as a shape
with invented consumers** — and when that was cleared away, the thing
actually missing was not spatial at all.

---

## ⭐⭐⭐ The gap

`conveyance.md` ships `Mountable` / `Drivable` — **one driver and N
passengers.** A coach, a horse, a cart.

> **A rowed boat has N PROPELLERS, and being out of time is the failure.**

Nothing models that. And `exertion.md` already ships the per-body half:
one `exert({durationS, powerW})` event from the scheduler, the traverse or
the exchange, with five slow stocks and **every limit soft**. So the
missing piece is narrow and specific:

> ⭐⭐⭐ **N people exerting into ONE device, where the device reads the
> SUM and the SYNCHRONY.**

⚠ And the synchrony is the half that makes it a mechanism rather than
addition. Six people each producing 150 W is not 900 W of boat unless
they pull together; out of phase, they fight each other and the boat
yaws. **"Out of time" is a different failure from "too weak"**, and it is
the one that makes a crew feel like six people instead of one person with
a bigger number — which was the actual goal the spatial primitive was
never going to reach.

---

## The consumers, and none of them is invented

⭐ This is the test the refused primitive failed. CLAUDE.md promotes
substrate when *"a third pack **wanting** a mixin"* appears — ***"not
count them up later"***, and never by supplying the third yourself.

| consumer | what pools | where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐⭐ **the whaleboat's oars** | propulsion | whaling |
| ⭐⭐ **the capstan** — raising an anchor | torque | **any ship, whaling or not** |
| ⭐⭐ **the windlass** — what rotates the whale during cutting-in | torque | whaling, and it is the **second** consumer inside one trade |
| **the two-man saw** | a reciprocating cut | forestry / carpentry |
| **the hand-pumped fire engine** | a pressure head | the fire service |
| ⭐⭐ **the sport crew boat** | propulsion | [tourism-and-sport-slate](./tourism-and-sport-slate.md) — the only **cooperative non-combat play** in the design |

**Five contexts across a trade, a fixture, a craft, a service and a
sport** — and whaling alone needs two of them before it needs anything
else, which is the strongest signal in the list.

---

## ⭐⭐ What it is NOT

- ⛔ **Not a spatial model.** The refused "strip" tried to make a
  whaleboat interesting by giving positions an ordering. **The boat is
  interesting because six people are working one hull**, and that is
  effort, not geometry. (The full refusal, and the *"you just made
  triangles"* reasoning, is recorded in `navigable-water-slate` § 7a.)
- ⛔ **Not a party mechanic.** `party.md` and `combat-formations.md` are
  about *strategy over a threat graph*. This is about **physics into one
  object**, with no adversary.
- ⛔ **Not a minigame.** ⭐ The decision is *whether to pull harder*, and
  `exertion.md`'s doctrine already governs the answer: **every limit is
  soft, and work never collapses a body.** So the cost of pulling too
  hard is paid in the slow stocks, later.

## Open questions

1. ⭐⭐ **How is synchrony produced?** The honest candidates are a **called
   stroke** (somebody is the metronome — which is a *role*, and therefore
   content) versus an emergent phase that players feel. ⭐ The called
   stroke is better: it makes the coxswain / boatheader a real job, it is
   a *verb*, and it is how it actually worked.
2. **Does the device read power, or work?** A capstan cares about total
   work (the anchor comes up or it doesn't); a boat cares about power
   *now*. Probably both, declared by the device.
3. ⚠ **Where does the mixin live?** `Drivable`'s widening says
   `lib/conveyance`; the capstan and windlass are not conveyances at all.
   ⭐ If it is one mechanism for both, its composers have **no common pack
   ancestor**, which is CLAUDE.md's own test for the kernel — but that
   wants the second real composer in hand first, not assumed.
4. **Is a draught animal team the same thing?** `conveyance.md` has
   haulage (hitch/unhitch) and a four-horse team is N propellers with no
   players in it. ⭐ If yes, this has a sixth consumer and an NPC one.
