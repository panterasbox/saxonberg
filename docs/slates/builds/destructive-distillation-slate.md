## ⭐⭐⭐ Plastics is NOT blocked on oil

The instinct is *oil → plastics*. That is the **modern** path and it is
historically backwards. **Bakelite (1907) is phenol + formaldehyde, both
coal-tar products**, and it predates commercial petrochemical feedstock
by decades.

| vertical | feedstock | blocked on | state |
|---|---|---|---|
| **destructive distillation** | wood, then coal | **nothing** | ⭐ the collier already does it |
| **plastics** | **coal tar** | destructive distillation | coal ships |
| drilling + oil | crude | a new extraction shape | → [drilling-slate](./drilling-slate.md) |

> **The cheapest path into the industrial epoch runs through a trade that
> already ships, and it does not touch a drill.**


## ⭐⭐⭐ The on-ramp blocks the plastic and nothing else

⚠ **This slate contradicted itself for eleven days** and the contradiction
decided its position in the backlog, so it is resolved here rather than
left to the next reader:

| where | claim |
|---|---|
| the header | *"the epoch on-ramp, **which blocks this**"* |
| the table above | destructive distillation · blocked on · **nothing** |

**The table was right, the header was over-broad, and the slate's own
Decided #1 already said so** — *"it is the epoch's on-ramp in **content
terms**"*. An on-ramp is not downstream of the other on-ramp. The header
generalised one rung's dependency to all five.

### The resolution: apply the doctrine instead of quoting it

[inquiry-slate § the bootstrap](./inquiry-slate.md) states the test, and
it is a test about **epistemics, not about era**:

> **Medieval technology is knowledge-by-TRADITION; industrial technology
> is knowledge-by-LAW.** … The shipped baseline is pre-understood by
> construction — it works, and nobody has to know why.

Run each rung through it:

| rung | needs a `Law`? | why |
|---|---|---|
| **wood tar → pitch** | ⭐ **no** | Bronze Age. Nobody theorised pitch. |
| **coke + coal tar** | ⭐ **no** | Darby coked coal in 1709 by trying it. |
| **coal gas + the gasometer** | ⭐ **no** | Murdoch lit a factory in 1798; aromatic chemistry arrives 70 years later. |
| **the thermoset** | ⛔ **YES** | the crosslinking `Law`, extended — this slate's own words |
| aniline dyes | — | out of scope, Decided #5 |

> **Four of the five rungs are knowledge-by-tradition. The retort is a
> CRAFT advance, not a science one** — you close the vessel and condense
> what comes off, and it works whether or not anyone can say why.

⭐⭐⭐ **And the order is not merely permitted, it is the honest one.**
Coal tar is *the feedstock organic chemistry was theorised from*. Perkin
made aniline dye in 1856 by accident; Kekulé's ring came in 1865. So the
retort does not wait on inquiry — **the retort is what gives inquiry
something to be about.** Ship the material first and the theory second,
which is the sequence that actually happened, and the thermoset then
becomes the first thing a realm makes *because* it understood something.

⚠ **The one thing this must not be read as:** a licence to skip the
on-ramp whenever a rung looks old enough. The test is the epistemic one
above, asked per rung and answered in the table — not *"is this
pre-1900?"* The thermoset is gated and stays gated; it is the proof case
and giving it away would cost the build its point.

### Consequence

**Stage A — buildable today.** The retort as the clamp's capitalised
sibling, the condenser, wood tar → pitch, coke + coal tar, ⭐⭐ coal gas
with the `sealed` rung and the gasometer. No `Law`, no inquiry, no new
extraction shape. It disrupts the tapper and the dyer, upgrades the
smelter, and defines the gas commodity model that drilled gas inherits
(Decided 3b).

**Stage B — gated on inquiry.** The thermoset, and the `Law` extension
from crosslinking to the polymer family. Lands whenever the on-ramp does;
nothing in Stage A is shaped around waiting for it.

## ⭐⭐⭐ The collier is running destructive distillation and discarding the valuable half

`char` ships and its own help text names the craft: *"Airflow is the
decision. Too much air and the charge burns all the way through and you
open a clamp full of ash; too little and the middle never chars and you
draw half-burnt brands. Between them is charcoal."* Three game days of
watching, and you can lose the lot.

**That is destructive distillation.** Heating wood without enough air to
burn it drives off everything volatile and leaves carbon. The collier
keeps the carbon.

**Everything else goes up the chimney:** wood tar, pitch, pyroligneous
acid — and from that, methanol and acetone.

⭐ **The historical move is pit → RETORT**: close the vessel, condense
what comes off, and the byproducts become the point. That is the whole
build, and it is beautiful here for four reasons:

1. **It is an UPGRADE to a shipped trade, not a new one.** The collier
   becomes a chemical industry without changing jobs — the single
   cheapest way to introduce an epoch.
2. **Clamp vs retort is a CAPITAL decision.** The clamp is a pile of
   earth; the retort is built, and pays back only if somebody wants the
   byproducts. ⭐ That is the business ladder the settlement model
   already describes, arriving inside one trade.
3. ⭐⭐ **It disrupts the naval-stores tapper.** Wood tar from a retort
   undercuts pine tar from tapping ([rubber-slate](./rubber-slate.md) /
   [tapping-slate](./tapping-slate.md)). **This is the first time a new
   technology destroys an existing player vocation** — which is real
   industrial history, is extraordinary to teach honestly, and must be
   *deliberate, visible and survivable* rather than an accident somebody
   discovers.
4. **The draught mechanic already teaches the right thing.** The same
   dial that decides ash-vs-charcoal decides how much volatile you drive
   off, so the retort does not need a new decision — it needs a second
   *reading* of the decision already there.

## The chain

### Wood tar (the first rung)

Retort + wood → charcoal **and** wood tar **and** pyroligneous acid. Tar
boils down to **pitch**. Consumers exist today or nearly: caulking, rope
and cordage, waterproofing, torches, the incendiary side of
[fire.md](../../subsystems/fire.md), and preservative for anything
standing in weather.

### Coal tar (the second, and the one that matters)

Coal ships (`mineral/coal`). Coking coal in a retort gives **coke** (the
smelter's better fuel — ⭐ a direct upgrade to a shipped chain) **and
coal tar**, which fractionates into the aromatics the first plastics and
the first synthetic dyes are made of.

⚠ ⭐ **And that closes a loop already in the tree**: `trade-dyeing` ships
madder, woad, weld — *natural* dyes. Coal-tar aniline dyes are what
destroyed that industry in the 1860s. **A second disrupted vocation,
from the same retort**, and the dyer is already a player.

### ⭐⭐ Coal gas — the retort's THIRD product, and the first gas that is a GOOD

Coking coal in a retort gives coke **and** coal tar **and** **town gas**.
Gas lighting predates electricity by a century, and
[light.md](../../subsystems/light.md) ships a lamp model — so the first
mass consumer already exists.

⭐⭐⭐ **This is where the world's gas ECONOMY is defined**, not at a well.
Asked *"is gas always drilled?"* the answer is no on four counts (coal
gas · firedamp · seeps · marsh gas), and drilling is the **last** of them.
The commodity half of gas — storage, the market, the price — belongs to
**the first thing that sells gas**, and that is this retort. See
[drilling-slate § Sequencing](./drilling-slate.md), which records the
decision so it is not re-litigated.

⚠ **The gate is already declared and inert.** `bulk.md`'s closure scale is
`open < liquidTight < sealed`, and the doc says `sealed` and the
phase→required-level mapping are *"defined on the scale but **unexercised
until gas content lands**."*

> **`bulk.md`'s `sealed` rung IS the gas economy.** No vessel, no product —
> which makes containment the whole capital story, exactly as it was
> historically.

⭐ And it yields the **gasometer**: a `Bulkable` with `closure: sealed`, a
rising bell in a water seal, **visible across the city, its height telling
anyone who looks how much gas the town has left.** A public, honest,
entirely non-gauge readout of a shared resource — and the natural hand-off
to the [energy build](../tails/energy-slate.md) if one wants it.

### The first plastic

A thermoset from coal-tar phenol. ⭐ Mechanically it is **the
crosslinking `Law` again**, which is the whole argument for doing rubber
first:

> **Rubber and ebonite are two points on a curve. The first plastic is a
> third.** Chain length × crosslink density spans elastomer ↔
> thermoplastic ↔ thermoset, and a player who understands the curve can
> **predict a material they have never made.**

That is [inquiry](./inquiry-slate.md)'s loop at its highest form, on one
`Law` row extended rather than a new one.

⚠ **Where this gets expensive, and the line to hold:** *an abstraction is
legitimate while it still costs somebody the activity*
([uncertainty.md](../../uncertainty.md)). So **model the property space,
never the reaction chemistry.** You make a polymer by controlling
**conditions** — temperature, time, crosslinker fraction — not by naming
monomers. Real polymer chemistry is deep enough to swallow the build
whole and nothing is lost by stopping at the curve.

## ⭐ Sulfur has two waiting consumers, not one

[rubber-slate](./rubber-slate.md) needs sulfur and leans on a smelting
byproduct. It is not the only claimant: **`bulk/vitriol` already ships**
— oil of vitriol, density 1840, authored as the corrosion channel's
liquid agent and **purchasable at the Terminus general store with no
producer anywhere.**

Sulfuric acid is the first industrial chemical, and vitriol is made from
sulfur. ⭐ So sulfur arrives with **two** unmet demands already authored
into the world, which is the demand test passed twice over rather than
argued.

## Decided

1. **This slate ships before drilling.** It is the epoch's on-ramp in
   content terms — the first industrial technology a realm can reach with
   no new extraction shape.
1b. ⭐⭐⭐ **And it is NOT blocked on inquiry's on-ramp, except for the
   plastic.** Four of five rungs are knowledge-by-tradition; the thermoset
   is the only one that needs a `Law`. Resolved 2026-10-06 — see § *The
   on-ramp blocks the plastic and nothing else*, recorded so the header's
   over-broad claim is not reinstated.
2. **The retort is `CharcoalPit`'s capitalised sibling**, not a new
   trade. `trade-fuel` grows; nothing is carved out.
3. **Coke ships with coal tar**, because it is the same act and it
   upgrades the smelter — a shipped consumer, immediately.
3b. ⭐⭐⭐ **Coal gas is the THIRD product, and the gas COMMODITY model is
   defined here** — not at a gas well. Storage, the `sealed` closure
   rung and the gasometer are this slate's, and drilled gas inherits
   them. One gas economy, anchored on the first thing that sells gas.
4. ⭐ **The polymer family is ONE `Law`, extended** — never a second
   discoverable relationship per material.
5. ⛔ **Aniline dyes are named but NOT scoped here.** They belong to
   `trade-dyeing`'s own follow-on; this slate only records that the
   retort is their source and that the disruption is intended.

## Open

1. **Does the retort replace the clamp, or coexist?** Lean **coexist** —
   the clamp stays the poor collier's kit, and the choice between them is
   the capital lesson. A retort that strictly dominates deletes a shipped
   decision.
2. ✅ **How many coal-tar cuts** — the question stands, the **blocker is
   gone.** The cuts rung SHIPPED with the whiskey build (2026-10-04) as
   [fractionation.md](../../subsystems/fractionation.md): ordered spans
   over the volume drawn, each with a character, a grade band, a toxin
   dose and an `aromaticCarry`. A coal-tar column is a `FractionSchedule`
   row — ⭐ *"the second [consumer] … is a refinery column, and that is
   rows."* So this is now a content question (how many cuts are worth
   authoring), not a substrate one.
3. **Which plastic first.** A thermoset is the honest first (it is what
   the chemistry of the era gives) and it is also the one whose
   *irreversibility* teaches something — you cannot re-melt it, which is
   a property with real economic consequences.
4. **Whether pyroligneous acid is modelled at all**, or the retort yields
   tar and the rest is flavour. Lean: **tar only at v1** — methanol and
   acetone have no consumer, and the demand test refuses them.
5. ⚠ **The disruption's pacing.** Undercutting the tapper and the dyer is
   intended, but a vocation that dies in a week is a bug and one that
   dies over a season is a story. This is the polity's problem as much as
   the designer's — and it is exactly the kind of thing the courts and
   the guilds exist for.
