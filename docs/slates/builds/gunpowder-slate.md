## 0. The garrison is not a build yet — where its pieces went

The settlement model locks it: *"Lethal combat faces outward"*; danger inside a
settlement is economic and social, and *"the real predation in this world is
financial"* ([settlement-model](../../settlement-model.md) § 9). A garrison exists
to face something outside, and the starter census found no front: the wolf is the
only wild game. A garrison today would be a costume. Its pieces have homes now:

| piece | its home |
|---|---|
| the quartermaster (provisioning) | the cannery's customer ([cannery-and-bottling-slate](./cannery-and-bottling-slate.md) § 7) |
| the militia muster | policing — the hue and cry as the polity's posse |
| drill | the gym and sport — training that produces a body |
| the salute | diplomacy's ceremony, as a powder consumer (§ 5) |

**The frontier fort** — a garrison at the edge of the wilds, provisioned from
town — becomes a build when the taxonomy pass gives the wilds a bestiary.

## 1. ⭐⭐ Three ingredients, two already shipped **[DECIDED]**

| ingredient | share | what it does | source |
|---|---|---|---|
| saltpeter (potassium nitrate) | ~75% | ⭐ the **oxidizer** | the nitre bed (§ 2) |
| charcoal | ~15% | the fuel | `char`, trade-fuel ✅ |
| sulfur | ~10% | lowers the ignition point | caprock sulfur, the rubber build ✅ |

⭐ **Lens 1: fire needs air; powder does not.** It carries its own oxygen, which
is why it burns in a sealed barrel, a bore hole, or under water. Fire's ignition
balance ships ([fire.md](../../subsystems/fire.md)); powder is the first
combustible that brings its own oxidizer.

## 2. The nitre bed — joining potash **[DECIDED]**

- **The process:** manure, urine, earth and wood ash heaped and turned for
  months; leached with water **through potash**, which converts calcium nitrate to
  potassium nitrate; boiled; crystallized. A slow product on a clock, like the
  heap.
- ⭐ **The saltpeter man — lens 7.** Historically the Crown's saltpetre men held
  the right to **dig up your barn and dovecote floors** for nitre-rich earth: a
  sovereign's right of entry onto private ground, famously hated. A polity may
  grant it — with a criterion and an appeal — and it is the purest case of a
  polity doing something nobody wanted.

## 3. The mill — grain size is burn rate **[DECIDED]**

- **The process:** grind each ingredient; mix them **wet** (safer);
  **incorporate** under an edge-runner mill; **corn** (granulate) the cake;
  glaze; dry.
- ⭐ **Grain size controls burn rate through surface area** — fine for priming
  and pistols, coarse for blasting and cannon. Derivable, never a table.
- **Damp ruins it** — wetness ships, so wet powder is honestly useless and the
  magazine must stay dry.

## 4. ⭐ Danger, honestly **[DECIDED]**

- **The mill can explode** — hence buildings set apart behind blast mounds,
  **non-sparking** copper and wooden tools, and the magazine away from town.
- **An explosion is splash** ([ranged.md](../../subsystems/ranged.md)'s
  splash-as-relationship, consent gate included). An accidental blast is a
  **negligent** act on the [accountability](../../subsystems/accountability.md)
  ledger, not combat.
- ⭐ **The powder works is a LULU** — the settlement model's exit-driven town
  type, *pointed away from a prohibition*. Siting it is a zoning question,
  decided by density.

### The gun — designed in the ranged slate, summarized here

The [ranged slate](./ranged-slate.md) already designs the firearm honestly, and this
slate defers to it:

- **The mechanism is the curriculum** — cartridge decomposition (case, primer, charge,
  projectile; powder *deflagrates*, which the combustion substrate can say), and the
  **action families** as a ladder — matchlock → flintlock → caplock → cartridge →
  repeater → self-loader. ⭐ The **wheellock** between matchlock and flintlock is
  literally clockwork — a spring wound with a key ([horology](./horology-slate.md)).
- **Firing is easy; handling safely is the skill** — competence never gates whether a
  gun fires; **negligent discharge is a derived consequence, never a roll**.
- ⭐ **"Keep your powder dry"** — wetness ships, so damp powder misfires, a worn flint
  sparks poorly, rain kills the matchlock: reliability from parts and conditions,
  never rolled (ranged W4's *reliability against output*).
- ⭐ **Interchangeable parts came from guns** — the ranged slate's *hand-fitted vs
  pattern parts*: a hand-fitted flintlock's lock belongs to that gun; a pattern-made
  spring fits any of its pattern. The armory system (early 1800s) is the
  [machines slate](./machines-slate.md)'s *interchangeability as an achievement of
  precision*, born in this trade; **the gunsmith's** parts economy.
- **Registration is the chattel ledger** — serials are chattel IDs; a crafted,
  never-registered firearm is mechanically a ghost gun; localities legislate
  differently and people sort (Tiebout on gun law).
- ⭐ **The proof house** — every barrel test-fired with an overload charge and stamped if
  it survived (a British institution since the 1630s): the three-layer certification
  pattern — the engine measures, an institution certifies.

### Cannon, founding and fortification

- ⭐ **Bell-founders cast cannon** — the ceramics build's **foundry** (which casts bells)
  is the door; in wartime **church bells were melted for guns**, a real values case.
- **Cannon remade fortification** — tall castle walls gave way to low, angled star forts:
  the [masonry slate](./masonry-and-concrete-slate.md)'s thrust, at war.
- **Salvage** — an iron cannon raised from a wreck comes up a lump of rust, a bronze one
  comes up a cannon ([underwater](./underwater-slate.md) — decomposition by material).

### Law and land

- **The powder magazine** — isolation, *"a hazard to neighbors"*

- **Covenants** ([land-use-covenant](./land-use-covenant-slate.md)) — *"no powder within

- **Statutes against real fields** ([legal-code](./legal-code-slate.md)) — gun law

## 6. The epoch ladder

Black powder → nitric acid, nitroglycerin, dynamite (the acid works) → smokeless.
**Charge, tamp, fuse, fire** are the same commands at every rung (lens 5).

## 7. The venue

- **Rejection's mine is the first consumer**, with its magazine.
- **The powder mill is sited away from town**, on running water for its
  incorporating mill. Lean: **the Weeping Moor**, a day from Rejection — far
  enough to be a LULU, close enough to sell. **Open.**

**The drive, in one line:** you buy saltpeter from the nitre-bed keeper, sulfur
from the caprock and charcoal from the clamp; you corn a coarse grade at the mill
on the moor, carry it to Rejection's magazine, drill the face, charge and tamp
the hole, light a fuse cut for ninety seconds — and the drift advances a yard in
a shift.
