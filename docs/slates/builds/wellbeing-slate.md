## 0. The diagnosis (2026-10-18)

The owner: *"You need food and drink to stay alive — that's the baseline.
But on top of that, who cares? … at the end of the day who cares about any of
it if all it does is put off dying another day."*

**Eating is a solitary chore with a binary outcome.** Everything rich about
the supply chain — grade, the cook's skill, where the barley grew — lands on
no one who cares, because the world has **no differentiated demand**,
**no social weight** for food, and **a binary body**. ⛔ And the obvious
shortcut is refused: the MMO *"well fed: +10 stamina"* buff converts food into
a gauge, the move the measurement doctrine warns against.

## 1. ⭐⭐⭐ Taste — born, learned, and aging **[DECIDED]**

*"People don't opt into tastes, they're born with some of them — babies refuse
certain foods and it's not the same which — some of our tastes change over
time. But everyone has them and they're modelled nowhere."*

A **taste profile** on every body, from the real science:

- **born, seeded** (identity's seeded fields, as ABO blood type is) — ⭐
  **bitter sensitivity** is genetic (PROP/PTC supertasters); **cilantro tastes
  of soap** to carriers of one olfactory gene; individual likes and dislikes
  (Dwarf Fortress's seeded preferences, the closest game model);
- **aging** — **children prefer sweet** more strongly and it declines with age
  (the species' `ageCurve`);
- **learned** — **mere exposure**: repeated tasting raises liking, which is
  how coffee, beer and bitter greens are *acquired*;
- ⭐ **aversive** — **conditioned taste aversion** (the Garcia effect): **one
  bout of food poisoning gives a lasting aversion to that food** — derived from
  a real event in the character's history (spoilage and pathogens ship).

## 2. ⭐⭐⭐ Pleasure is not satisfaction **[DECIDED]**

*"When we eat any food we experience satisfaction. When we eat tasty food we
experience pleasure. Pleasure has no model in the platform right now."*

**Satisfaction** is the body's need met (metabolism). **Pleasure** is separate,
and the science gives it without an invented formula — the taste profile meets
the dish's **derived tastes and aromas** (the palate already derives them;
*no dish authors what it tastes like*), scaled by:

- ⭐ **alliesthesia** (Cabanac, 1971) — pleasure depends on **internal
  state**: food is delicious when hungry and cloying when full, cold water
  wonderful when overheated, a hot drink when cold. *Hunger is the best sauce*,
  literally — **pleasure is the body signalling usefulness**, derivable from
  metabolism and thermal state;
- ⭐ **sensory-specific satiety** — the fifth slice of the same cake is less
  pleasant while a *different* food still tastes good: **the real reason people
  seek variety**, making diet diversity matter with no rule;
- **hedonic adaptation** — pleasure habituates and **expectations rise**
  (Oxygen Not Included's morale against expectations, made real): the fine
  meal every night stops being fine;
- **"wanting" is not "liking"** (Berridge) — craving is distinct from
  pleasure, why you can want what you no longer enjoy (the alcohol tolerance
  stock's honest shadow).

## 3. ⭐⭐⭐ Felt, or it does not exist **[DECIDED]**

*"If you're talking about spitting out a line of prose when you eat shitty food,
no one's ever going to feel that… if you want pleasure to matter it needs to be
really felt, even if the feeling is the absence of suffering which you are used
to experiencing otherwise."*

**Discomfort is the baseline**, and real bodies are never neutral — the low hum
of the scattered physiology that already exists:

| discomfort | from |
|---|---|
| hunger, thirst | metabolism |
| cold, heat | thermal — core temperature against comfort |
| fatigue, soreness | exertion — the work you did; sleep pressure (§ 5) |
| pain | harm — wounds |
| ⭐ grime | cleanliness — the soiling seam |
| cravings | sensory-specific satiety; the slow stocks |

**Pleasures** — taste, warmth when cold, rest, a bath, company — **relieve**
discomforts and leave an **afterglow**: a dated event fading under hedonic
adaptation (RimWorld's thought against an expectation, the expectation rising
as you live well).

**Felt through five channels — soft limits that already ship:**

1. ⭐ **capacity** — exertion's sustainable power and pace floor: a miserable
   body tires sooner, a fed, warm, content one pushes further;
2. ⭐⭐ **learning** — advancement's gains scale with wellbeing; *eating well
   becomes how you get better*, not how you avoid dying — the channel players
   feel hardest;
3. **focus** — strong discomfort **interrupts long acts** (*"your stomach
   cramps; you lose the thread"*); you can always resume — the most felt, and
   the easiest to make annoying, so its thresholds need care;
4. **recovery** — wounds and sleep restore faster when comfortable;
5. **how others see you** — presentation reads a gaunt, filthy, miserable
   stranger as one; NPC regard and prices follow.

**For NPCs** pleasure lands fully — **demand** (they buy what they like and
return to the cook), **regard** (they remember who fed them well), **behaviour**
(a brain's urgency).

**The doctrine guards:**

- ⛔ **never control** — no mental breaks, no refusals (*the engine must not do
  the player's job*);
- ⛔ **never presence** — wellbeing **freezes offline**, as living-body clocks
  do; logging off costs nothing;
- **asymptotic** — misery is never lethal;
- ⭐ **the mirror shows it** — the BODY shelf row and `look` at yourself read it
  in words (*"cold, hungry and sore — and it's starting to tell"*): *the feed
  hides the measurement; the mirror shows you* — the player always knows why
  they are slowing down.

## 4. The four food directions **[DECIDED as directions]**

*"All of these are worth pursuing."*

1. ⭐ **Diet shapes the body** — nutrition made **diverse and regional** with
   the real deficiency diseases, each slow, legible and rescuable, never a
   death-check: **scurvy** (no fresh food — the sailor's disease and the
   maritime cargo of citrus), **beriberi** (polished rice), **pellagra** (maize
   without **nixtamalization** — a technique that saves you), **goitre**
   (iodine, inland — the sea's salt fixes it), night blindness, rickets. A realm
   eating only bread sickens slowly; **trade and cuisine become medicine**.
2. ⭐ **Food is social** — eating together builds **regard** between diners;
   hosting builds **renown** (reception); the **feast** is a civic event and a
   public-spending faucet; **guest right** is a norm a polity can enforce.
3. ⭐ **People want things** — every NPC's taste profile (§ 1) and cravings
   (the body's needs) make **grade and cuisine into demand**; a cook's
   reputation is earned in mouths.
4. **The calendar** — seasonal food and **the hungry gap** (late winter, stores
   running out) make preserving a community's act with consequences; ⚠ under
   Law 2 the clock starts at an act (preserving, or not), never presence.

Recommended order: **1 and 3 together** (the body's needs and people's wants
give every part of the chain a reason), then **2**, then **4**.

## 5. ⭐⭐ Sleep — the biggest relief **[DECIDED]**

The sleep slate holds the owner's design — **logout is sleep**; one **wake
reconcile** integrates the away time as a night spent where and how you lay
down; rest quality from the surface, how kept, what you wore and the room's
heat; pyjamas as consequences; absence never starves (needs plateau). This
adds **what a good night GIVES**, from **Borbély's two-process model**:

- **sleep pressure** builds while awake and discharges in sleep (felt
  tiredness); the **circadian** rhythm (the world clock) modulates it;
- ⭐ **well rested is low sleep pressure on waking**, from **duration × rest
  quality** — wellbeing's largest **relief** (fatigue and soreness gone) and
  strongest **afterglow**, fading as pressure builds through the day; felt
  through the five channels;
- ⭐⭐ **sleep consolidates learning** — skills practised in the day are
  consolidated in sleep (people perform better next morning without more
  practice), so **advancement's day banks fully after a good night** and less
  after a bad one;
- **sleeplessness is soft and capped** — pressure keeps rising, wellbeing sags
  (slower, poorer focus, yawning interruptions, a tired face), and it
  **plateaus** at *exhausted*; never lethal;
- ⭐ **sleep does not require logging out** — the sleep slate's *asleep logged in
  and logged out integrate the SAME night*; at the game clock's pace a full night
  passes in well under an hour, so a player who stays on sleeps in a bed
  in-game. **That keeps the incentive to log out weak** (the owner: *"they can't
  be too bad or we incentivize people logging out, which I'm not keen on"*) —
  logging out is the most convenient bed, never a requirement.

⚠ **A Compact law mandating logout** (the owner's aside: *"maybe once players
join the game and the Compact is running they'll pass a law mandating you log
out periodically"*) probably collides with an **entrenched** rule — *the one
thing that must never be the meter is the player's presence* reads as a tier-A
protection in measurement.md, amendable by nobody. A polity can encourage sleep
(a curfew in the fiction, an inn's prices); whether it can compel logout is to
be checked against the tiers when it arises, not decided here.

## 6. The lens pass (2026-10-18)

1. **Pedagogy** — alliesthesia, sensory-specific satiety, hedonic adaptation,
   taste genetics, the Garcia effect, wanting vs liking, deficiency diseases
   and nixtamalization, the two-process model, consolidation.
2. **Expression** — a cook's food is wanted or not by real people with real
   palates; cuisine becomes regional identity.
3. **Immersion** — the relief of a warm meal after a cold day is felt in what
   the body can do. **Participation** — the cook, the host, the innkeeper, the
   baker as a hub; ⭐ the feast as a civic act.
4. **Values** — ⭐ the agency fork decided: pleasure informs and drives the
   world, never controls the player; presence never the meter; misery never
   lethal. ⚠ Interruptions are the line between felt and annoying.
5. **Continuity** — none special; the same acts at every epoch, the foods
   changing.
6. **Economy** — ⭐ **differentiated demand at last**: grade, variety and
   cuisine become prices; inns sell good nights; the cocktails' and cooks'
   chains finally land on someone.
7. **Governance** — guest right; the feast as public spending; food-safety
   law; whether a polity may compel sleep (§ 5).

**Altitude** — *invariant*: never control, never presence, never lethal; the
mirror shows it. *Grain*: every weight on the five channels, the interruption
thresholds, adaptation's rate, sleep pressure's curve. *Title*: a realm's
cuisine.
