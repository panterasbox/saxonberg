## 1. ⚠ The error that produced this — and why wizardry is not the same error

An earlier pass put authoring work on
[contract.md](../../subsystems/contract.md)'s gig board: posted, taken,
completed, verifiable. ⚠ **Wrong — that is non-diegetic labour wearing
the fiction's apparatus**, which is the `compute-is-a-metaresource`
correction one level up.

⭐ **But wizardry is a different move, and the difference is precise:**

> **Compute-as-energy pretends a metaresource is diegetic. Wizardry
> admits the author stands OUTSIDE the fiction and gives that
> outsideness a name INSIDE it.**

⭐⭐ Which is why the MUD tradition's *"Bobalu conjures a magic pen"*
worked: **the fiction absorbed the act** rather than apologising for it
or pretending it was something else. It is not a costume for a mechanism
— it is **a role for a person who is genuinely half-outside.**

⭐ **So the work item stays non-diegetic** (a plain structured record —
see [labor-standing-slate](./labor-standing-slate.md)) **and the
PRACTITIONER is the diegetic part.** The fiction names the wizard, not
the paperwork.

## 2. ⭐⭐⭐ The authority argument — the finding that makes this cheap

> **User: "the game can decide how to calculate it however it wants, it
> needs no 'honesty' other than our own values when it comes to crafting
> content… this means we can actually be authoritative instead of the
> other disciplines which live and die on the honesty of our models."**

Every other Discipline measures something the world **simulates** —
smithing is only as honest as the metal model, fishing as the fishery,
medicine as the body. **Wizardry measures craft, and we are the authority
on craft here, because we wrote the lenses.**

> ⭐⭐⭐ **So the rubric can be a DOCUMENT rather than a simulation. And
> the document already exists:**
>
> - [design-lenses.md](../../design-lenses.md) — the seven, and the
>   altitude rule;
> - [eotl-craft.md](../../eotl-craft.md) — **fourteen verified
>   techniques** and **seven failure modes**, measured against 45,380
>   files;
> - *hollow content is number one* — the founder's own ranking of what
>   makes content bad;
> - [measurement.md](../../measurement.md) — what may be counted and what
>   a gauge is.
>
> ⭐⭐⭐ **We wrote the syllabus without knowing it.**

⭐ Which inverts the usual economics of a Discipline: the normal problem
is that the **measurement outruns the model**, and here **there is no
model to outrun.** Wizardry is the *cheapest* Discipline to make
rigorous, not the most expensive.

⚠ **And it is the first thing to build**, because it is free and it is
dispositive: **if a competence band cannot be defended from that
document, the Discipline is not real.** Everything below is downstream of
the rubric.

## 3. ⭐⭐⭐ The supply chain already exists — we had not named it

> **User: "is there a wizard 'supply chain'? Im not sure what its inputs
> and outputs are but it could be cool."**

⛔ **Not the four scarcities** — those stay uncostumed metaresources
(§ 7). The real chain:

| | |
|---|---|
| **input** | ⭐⭐ **LAND** — a parcel. Already scarce, titled, transferable, subdividable, with **three markets** |
| **intermediate goods** | ⭐⭐⭐ **templates** (you `extends:` somebody's torch — and the **mint ledger** already tracks who instantiated whose) · **packs** (`requires:` *is* the dependency graph) · **styles** (the gallery) · **mode cartridges** (slotted, crafted, traded) |
| **output** | **content** — places, things, people, laws |
| **demand** | ⭐ **72 locations against thousands needed** |

> ⭐⭐⭐ **The wizard supply chain was already designed across this whole
> thread. The missing thing was calling it one** — materials → templates
> → packs → localities, with styles and cartridges as intermediate goods
> and the mint ledger as its bill of lading.

⭐ And the demand test (`vocations.md`: *a vocation exists iff unmet
demand*) **passes harder than for any shipped trade.**

## 4. ⭐⭐⭐ `isWizard` is the APEX of the spectrum, and it is the one thing gated

> **User: "the 'wizard' level or tag or whatever we want to call it is the
> apex of wizardry. you asked if anything was gated, that's it. that's
> the thing that's gated. but it's all one spectrum, we're all wizards."**

⭐⭐ An earlier pass made the Discipline and the code-trust bit
**orthogonal** — competence measures skill, `isWizard` grants licence,
and never the twain. That is worse, because **it leaves the licence with
no ladder**: you have it or you do not, and there is no path.

### ⭐⭐⭐ And it does NOT violate the band rule — it is the rule working

`verb-conferral-retired` holds that **a band must never confer verbs**,
with the reason stated: ⭐⭐⭐ ***the refusal is the progression UI — if
something lifts it, the verb must EXIST so you can be told.***

> **`eval` already exists and already refuses with `isWizard`.** The
> refusal is already the progression UI. **What changes is only what
> lifts it** — from *an archwizard grants you a group* to *you reached
> the apex.*

⭐⭐ **And the band stays NECESSARY, NOT SUFFICIENT**, because
`isArchwizard` is already *the wizard-conferral axis*:

> **Measured competence makes you eligible; an admitting body admits
> you.** Which is how every licensed profession works — and it means **a
> band alone still confers nothing**, so the rule's letter holds, the
> ladder exists, and the one gate that matters keeps a human in it.

### ⭐⭐⭐ "We're all wizards" — a gradient, not a tier

⭐ The spectrum starts at zero for **everyone**, which is the only version
compatible with `everyone-is-an-author`'s ⚠⚠ *an "author tier" is a
CATEGORY ERROR*.

> **`isWizard` stops being staff and becomes the top of a ladder
> everybody is already on.** There is no author *tier*; there is an
> author *gradient*, and the apex is **a licence over the substrate**,
> not a class of person.

## 5. What is in-game and what leaves

| layer | in / out | why |
|---|---|---|
| **the practice** — acquire land, build, ship packs, consume others' templates | ⭐ **in** | it is an economy with real inputs and a real market (§ 3) |
| **the reputation** — who builds well | ⭐ **in** | it is already producer standing and renown |
| **the competence band** | ⭐⭐ **in** | and it confers nothing except eligibility for the apex |
| **the apex licence** (`isWizard`) | ⭐ **in, and gated** | § 4 |
| **the transcript** — portable applied hours | **out** | `antecedents-and-federation`: ⭐⭐ *skill travels* |

⭐⭐⭐ **And the two judges do not compete**, which was the objection that
nearly sank this:

> **Standing measures what the POLITY thinks of your work. Wizardry
> measures what the CRAFT says about it, against a rubric we wrote and
> can defend.** Two judges, two documents, no overlap in what they confer.

⭐⭐ **The band's useful payoff is author-to-author**: a band on a
template's author is a quality signal for somebody deciding whose work to
build on — ⚠ with the feedback firewall's guard: **disclosed by the
holder, never published by the system.** A self-read you may show, not a
leaderboard. (The *mirror shows you*; the feed does not rank you.)

## 6. ⭐⭐⭐ The lore is the best part, not a cost

> **User: "there's also the aspect of lore and all the backstory it
> introduces into the world."**

> *"To be a wizard is necessarily to sculpt reality."* — already a lore
> sentence.

And the cosmology it implies is **not a stretch, because the Compact
already implies it**: *law = code*, the polity governs its own substrate,
the chambers legislate the engine, and
[story-bible.md](../../story-bible.md)'s **Recognition** is the founding
event of a world aware of being made.

> ⭐⭐⭐ **The game already knows it is a game, constitutionally.** So the
> lore is not new fiction — **it is the prose nobody has written for a
> premise that is already load-bearing.**
>
> **Wizardry is not magic. It is authorship, and the world knows it.**

⚠ **One guard**, from `meta-vs-fiction-jargon` (*"the Compact" LOCKED*;
*the aether is just the internet*): **be honest about the meta rather
than mystifying it.** Name it, do not costume it — the same discipline
that keeps compute out of mana.

## 7. ⛔⛔ The two lines that must hold

1. ⛔⛔ **Wizardry's costs are NEVER paid in mana.** `arcane-science.md`
   has one postulate and a price list, and *"sculpting reality has a
   cost"* is already magic's thesis — which makes this **the most
   tempting blur in the entire design.** The Discipline is **named in the
   fiction and funded out of it**; the four scarcities stay
   metaresources.
2. ⚠ **The band gates nothing but eligibility for the apex.** The moment
   it unlocks a verb, an extent or a vote, it becomes a second franchise
   competing with standing — the thing § 5 exists to prevent, and the
   thing `verb-conferral-retired` already settled for bands in general.

## 8a. ⭐⭐⭐ The syllabus, and why it is the first curriculum

**Added 2026-10-04.** The Discipline needs teaching, and the teaching
turns out to be the cheapest thing in the design — so the full treatment
moved to its own slate:
[wizardry-curriculum-slate.md](./wizardry-curriculum-slate.md).

The three findings that bear on *this* slate:

> ⭐⭐⭐ **The evaluator is the build.** `lint:family`, `pnpm test`, and
> *does the row boot*. Every other Discipline's assessment is only as
> honest as its model; wizardry's answer key is maintained correct for
> unrelated reasons and **updates itself when the engine changes** — which
> is § 2's authority argument arriving as an engineering property rather
> than a philosophical one.

⚠ **But the rubric is NOT the syllabus, and conflating them was an error
worth recording.** The lenses + `eotl-craft` (§ 2) are what a **crit
scores**; they are not what a **course teaches**. The course teaches the
architecture — the capability vocabulary, affordance, persistence, the Api
surface — and the lenses are upper division, where lens 4 says there is no
derivable right answer. ⭐ Both documents stay load-bearing; they are
load-bearing at *different altitudes*.

⭐⭐ **And the band's input problem (§ 10.2) has a measured candidate.**
`eotl-craft`'s strongest empirical finding is that **detail density is the
only reliable completeness signal in 45,000 files** — *in every case the
unfinished room is the one with an empty or absent detail map* — with the
explicit instruction that the right use is **a mirror, not a gate**. That
is a real, honest, derivable input. ⚠ Whether the *course's* instrument and
the *Discipline's* instrument may be the same number is open, because that
is where the two judges (craft vs polity, § 5) would start to merge.

## 9. The lens pass

**⭐⭐⭐ 1 — pedagogy.** The strongest limb. It exercises modelling a
system, writing prose, composing from a vocabulary, estimating resource
cost, reading a schema — **all genuinely teachable.**

> **Wizardry is the one Discipline whose practicum is the platform
> itself.** You learn to build by building, and the measurement is your
> built work's reception — which is `practicum-thesis`'s *product =
> APPLIED HOURS* cashed out.

⭐ Derivable? Yes, from ledgers this thread already designed: declared
intents, completed work, engagement.

**⭐⭐ 2 — expression.** Naming the practice legitimises it and gives
authors a visible arc for the thing they actually do. ⚠ Risk: measured
practice becomes optimised practice — **mitigated only if the band
derives from RECEPTION, never from output**, or it is the churn metric
again.

**⚠⚠ 3a — immersion.** The real risk: a meta-layer intruding. ⭐ The MUD
precedent says charm *when the fiction owns it*, and § 6 argues the
Compact already owns it. ⛔ **But § 7.1 is the load-bearing guard** —
mana is where this breaks.

**⭐ 3b — participation.** *The economy has a blacksmith-shaped hole* →
**the world has an author-shaped hole**, and naming the trade is how you
advertise the vacancy.

**⚠ 4 — values.** Measuring authorship confers status, and status near
standing is political. ⭐ *Being good at building = political weight* is
the producer chamber's **actual premise**, so it is consistent — but it
is technocracy, and the counterweights are structural and already there:
**three chambers, two-of-three**, plus § 5's rule that the band confers
no power.

**⭐⭐ 5 — continuity.** Unusually clean: wizardry is the one Discipline
**about the substrate** rather than about the world, so it is
**epoch-invariant by nature.** A medieval wizard and an industrial one
both sculpt reality.

**⭐⭐⭐ 6 — economy.** § 3. Real inputs, real intermediate goods, real
demand — and the demand test passes more convincingly than for any
shipped trade.

**⚠ 7 — governance.** A competence band **judges a person**, so name the
criterion (§ 2's rubric), the appeal (`chronicle`'s **deed vs claim**),
and the entrenchment tier. ⭐ All three have homes; none is written.
⭐⭐ And the apex is a **licence**, so its conferral is an office act with
an appeal — which is `governance.md`'s territory rather than a new
mechanism.

## 10. Open questions

1. ✅ **CLOSED — the rubric is written**:
   [wizardry-rubric.md](../../wizardry-rubric.md). ⭐⭐⭐ It turned out
   to be **mostly what is left once three existing rules are obeyed** —
   `measurement.md`'s valuation ban, `advancement.md`'s *difficulty is a
   world-measurement*, and A3's *derive, don't track*. ⛔ The first of
   those **forbids a single "wizardry band"** (a cross-domain composite is
   the banned object), so the Discipline is **a tree of seven
   single-domain leaves** — which is also what *prefer specializations*
   independently demanded.
2. ✅ **CLOSED — and it is not "differently weighted", it is a different
   POPULATION.** ⭐⭐⭐ **Standing reads CONSUMPTION; wizardry reads
   PRODUCTION.** The consumer says *"I liked it"*; the peer says *"I
   depended on it."* So the band reads the build, the artifact's own
   properties, and **peer reuse** (the mint ledger already tracks who
   instantiated whose) — and ⛔ **never a rating, a use counter or
   renown**, which would merge the judges and import the payola problem
   two firewalls were built to keep out. See
   [wizardry-rubric.md](../../wizardry-rubric.md) § 4.
3. ⚠ **Why was this rejected before?** The user recalls it being raised
   and declined. ⭐ The two objections I would guess — *it makes authoring
   special* and *it collides with `isWizard`* — are both answered (§ 4),
   **but if the real objection was something else it probably still
   stands**, and the original should be found rather than re-derived.
4. **Does the apex have rungs above it?** `isArchwizard` exists as the
   conferral axis. ⚠ Whether that is *a second apex* or *the admitting
   office* changes whether the spectrum has one gate or two.
5. ⭐ **Does the trade get businesses** (§ 8)? A commissioning house is
   the obvious shape, and it is where the non-diegetic work item and the
   diegetic trade meet — the seam most likely to get costumed by mistake.
