# Bathroom slate — presence without function

**Captured 2026-08-01** from a design conversation. The governing
observation is prior art's unanimous lesson, named here **the toilet
paradox**: in games, the *presence of the room* carries all the
immersion and the *function of waste* carries none of it (Fallout
drinks from its sinks, immersive sims hide in the stalls, Animal
Crossing furnishes toilets — nobody excretes; the one franchise that
simulates bladders is the one that feels like a tamagotchi).

## Doctrine

- **No waste. Standing decision, reaffirmed.** Characters do not
  defecate or urinate; omitting waste costs nothing, simulating it
  costs dignity (the uncanny is asymmetric). The revisit door is
  **closed but labeled**: if pedagogy ever demands the excretory
  system, [metabolism](../../subsystems/metabolism.md) already
  teaches intake honestly — that is where the curriculum value
  lives.
- **Bathrooms exist anyway**, because they earn their rooms four
  ways, each hooked to a shipped system (below).

## The four functions

1. **Water** — a tap is a bulk-substrate fixture (fill / pour /
   drink, all shipped). Honest cost: the tap draws on the venue's
   water **utility** (diegetic money, per the stewardship slate's
   utilities line) — hydration free-to-cheap for patrons, a real
   line on the building's books.
2. **Washing [DECIDED: yes, as state]** — wetness's indoor cousin.
   **Enabling, never a decay meter** (wanna, not hafta: washing
   opens doors; filth never nags). The seams that make it worth
   having: **medical hygiene** (the medic vertical — "wash your
   hands" being real; *nurses will like that*), scent/trace and the
   stealth systems (rinsing the gallery-flood shift off), food
   service.
3. **The mirror** — "how do others see me right now" is a real
   query the belief/recognition substrate can answer, pointed at
   yourself. Checking a disguise in a mirror is honest mechanics in
   perfect flavor.
4. **Privacy** — a small lockable room (Sealable/Lockable shipped):
   changing, stashing, hiding, the quiet conversation. The
   immersive-sim lesson: the bathroom is secretly a gameplay room.

## The LOD ladder [DECIDED]

| Rung | What ships | Where |
|---|---|---|
| **Detail** | a prose detail ("a washroom door behind the counter"), not enterable | **the default for 1–2 room venues** |
| **Fixture** | a washstand/tap in the room | venues where the function matters but a room doesn't (aid post, kitchen) |
| **Room** | a real restroom, cloned from the archetype set | gridded buildings |
| **Venue** | **the bathhouse** — a social institution and a real business (attendant, employment, services, regulars) | one per city says more than four hundred closets |

## The water-tech gradient [DECIDED]

**Municipal taps downtown; pumps and wells at the frontier.** City
venues meter water as a utility; frontier water is free but
effortful (the pump, the well, the bucket — vessels and bulk carry
it). The gradient makes the bathhouse *matter* more the further out
you are, and gives the frontier its honest texture.

## The bathroom debate — a designed obstacle course for the legislature

**(User, 2026-08-01: bathrooms should *force* "the bathroom
debate.")** The laboratory thesis pointed at its sharpest real-world
target — and the design forces the debate without pre-answering it:

- **Kernel neutrality is the forcing function.** The engine ships
  NO facility-access rule — nothing gendered, nothing
  species-gated, nothing hardcoded. Room access is what it already
  is: locality law over the existing access machinery. Any rule
  that exists must have been *made* — by someone, through process,
  on the record, appealable. The legislature inherits no answer;
  it can legislate one, delegate one, or own the silence.
- **Body diversity makes it material; the allegory doctrine makes
  it survivable.** Species and body plans make facility design an
  *honest* material question (fixtures built for one plan genuinely
  don't serve another) — and per the species-as-race-allegory
  doctrine (defamiliarized, anti-essentialist), the staged debate
  runs over fictional categories no real player IS. The structure
  of the argument — who was this built for, who decides, what does
  "separate" cost — is fully experienced; no real person's identity
  is the chip on the table.
- **The provocation is legacy content.** The world ships with
  inherited conventions nobody voted for: the old bathhouse's
  traditional segregated sides, facilities obviously built for one
  body plan, a new species with no convention at all. The debate
  starts because the world already contains unexamined answers —
  exactly like ours did.
- **The federalism layer is part of the course.** Municipal law
  (localities differ; Tiebout sorting) vs. a floor-level right (an
  amendment-library candidate)? *Which layer decides* is half the
  pedagogy.

**The rail (standing, from the prison slate's guardrail):** the
in-fiction debate is legitimate governance content; real-conduct
harassment of real players is meta, always, and never wears the
debate as a costume.

## Open questions (for the archetype session / requirements)

1. **Cleanliness representation** — what exactly the washing state
   is (a band? a trace/scent clear? per-body-part?) and which
   systems read it first (medical hygiene seems the launch
   consumer).
2. **Hot water** — a bath wants heated water, and the thermal
   substrate is shipped: the bathhouse furnace is a real furnace.
   Whether hot/cold matters mechanically (comfort? treatment?) or
   is archetype flavor.
3. **Bathhouse placement** — Terminus authors one (the incumbent
   city's texture); Saxonberg deliberately doesn't (the committee's
   call, per the unfurnished-capital doctrine).
4. **Mirror mechanics** — the exact read surface (self-directed
   recognition describe) and whether it's the mirror fixture's
   affordance or a general verb the mirror merely flavors.
5. **The legacy conventions to ship** — which inherited
   segregation conventions the launch content carries (the
   bathhouse sides; the one-body-plan facilities), chosen so the
   debate is inevitable but no configuration reads as our
   endorsement. The archetype session should design these
   deliberately.
