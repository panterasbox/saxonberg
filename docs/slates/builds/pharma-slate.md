## The thesis

> ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ **You cannot tell whether the medicine worked. You only know you
> got better.**

**Credence goods** are the economics category where quality cannot be
assessed **before purchase or after use** — medicine, car repair, legal
advice. They are where markets fail hardest, and they are **the reason
licensure, regulation, brands and professional reputation exist at all.**

| Kind | Assessable |
|---|---|
| **search good** | before buying — *a sword's weight* |
| **experience good** | after using — *a meal* |
| ⭐ **credence good** | **never** — *did the medicine work, or did you get better anyway?* |

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ And in this sim it is STRUCTURAL, not authored

**Because time is the free heal, everyone recovers eventually** (see
[physiology-slate](./physiology-slate.md) § Part 7). So **a worthless
remedy and a real one are both followed by recovery.**

> **Snake oil is not a content decision. It is a viable business the
> physics permits.**

That is the placebo problem, arriving out of a floor we adopted for
humane reasons. **Nothing has to be written to make quackery possible.**

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ Which makes pharma where the game teaches STATISTICS

The only defence against a credence good is **evidence at scale.**

> **One patient tells you nothing. A hundred tells you everything.**

**How medicine actually developed** — you need numbers to beat anecdote —
and the append-only records make it **computable in-world** rather than
asserted. It joins the actuarial thread ([insurance](./insurance-slate.md)),
the publishing thread ([inquiry](./inquiry-slate.md)) and the
investigative thread ([press](./press-slate.md)), **all pointed at the
same problem from different sides.**

## ⭐⭐⭐ It does not merely cross-cut systems — it creates demand for INSTITUTIONS

The reason to build this industry rather than another craft:

| Institution | Why pharma needs it |
|---|---|
| **licensure** | you cannot judge the practitioner either |
| **the record** | the only thing that beats anecdote |
| **brands** | the mark is the only assessable property |
| **the press** | somebody has to investigate the claim |
| **assay / ratings** | a paid substitute for judgement |
| **the courts** | for when the substitute fails |

**Every institutional mechanism this project has designed has a natural
first customer here.**

### ⭐⭐ Brands get their best use case

`BrandedMixin` and corpo marks exist but are thin. Here they are
load-bearing:

> **You cannot judge the pill, so you judge the maker.**

Precisely why pharmaceutical brands matter more than rope brands — **the
mark is the product's only assessable property.** A brand that is cheap
elsewhere becomes genuinely valuable, and **genuinely fragile.**

### ⭐⭐ Batches make RECALLS possible

Provenance ships (`authoring_events`, context-derived author). Applied to
production runs it yields **batch identity**, and therefore:

> **A bad batch, traceable to its maker, and the question of whether they
> admit it.**

A complete story with a moral choice in it, **generated by a data
structure we already have** — and it gives the accountability ledger a
**corporate-scale** case rather than only interpersonal ones.

## The supply chain

| Category | Source |
|---|---|
| **botanical** | herbs, barks, roots, fungi — **foraged or farmed** |
| **mineral** | salts, sulfur, zinc — ⭐ **lithium** |
| **animal** | fats, oils, beeswax |
| ⭐ **solvent** | **water, and ALCOHOL** |
| ⭐ **vessel** | **glass** |

⭐⭐⭐ **The last two are the ones nobody thinks about.** A tincture is an
**alcohol extraction**, so **the distiller is a pharma supplier** —
quietly connecting the drinks economy to medicine exactly as it connects
in reality. Glass is sand plus heat, i.e. **the furnace family that
already ships.**

> **The bottleneck usually is not the herb. It is the solvent and the
> container.**

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐ The closed-Material problem, resolved by the shipped architecture

**Real tension**: the Material library is a **curated closed set** and
recipe packs add **zero material rows** — but a pharmacopoeia needs
distinct actives.

> **The closed set is MATERIALS. The open set is TAGS.**

`Material.toxicity: ToxinTag[]`, `nutrientAmounts` as tag→mg, and
`ToxinBehavior` (absorption / clearance / potency / bands) authored **on
the Condition seed, not in a code table.**

> **A new drug is a new Condition seed plus a tag on an existing plant.**

**Zero new material rows**, the doctrine holds unmodified, and the
extension point is exactly where content should live.

### ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ The central drama: WILD → CULTIVATED

**Wild collection is cheap and finite.** A rare herb harvested faster than
it regrows is a textbook commons — and the standing doctrine already says
**common-pool → QUOTA.**

> **The wild herb is the game's first genuine commons.**

And the pressure resolves the way it did in reality:

> ⭐ **When wild supply cannot meet demand, somebody has to FARM it.**

**The actual history of nearly every medicinal plant**, a real economic
transition **players drive rather than are told about** — and usefully,
**farming needs to know nothing about medicine.** You grow a plant that
carries a tag. *(User, correctly: farming gets built the same way whether
herbs exist or not.)*

### Spoilage caps scale, and moves the hard problem

> **You cannot build inventory, so you cannot scale ahead of the market.**

Shelf life keeps production tracking demand — which stops the industry
running away, and means **distribution is the hard problem, not
production.** Perishable + valuable + regulated is **the worst freight
case in the game.**

> ⭐⭐⭐ **Refrigeration relocates pharma**: without it you make near the
> **patient**; with it you make near the **herb**.

**Von Thünen again, unprompted, in a second industry** — a good sign the
geography model is real.

## ⭐⭐ The illicit branch degrades itself

Controlled substances (see [physiology-slate](./physiology-slate.md) §
Part 7f) create smuggling, and the consequence is **structural rather
than authored**:

> **The black market cannot use the institutions that substitute for
> judgement.**

No brand, no assay, no recall, no traceable batch. So **illicit product is
more dangerous BY CONSTRUCTION** — and *prohibition degrades quality* is a
**genuine empirical finding players discover rather than get lectured
about.** One more legislature object from the same source as the others.

## Disposal closes the loop

Expired stock is **toxic waste** — so it enters the sanitation pipeline
with a real cost, and dumping it is a **nuisance under the zoning emission
model.** Small, but it means the chain **has an end** rather than trailing
off.

## The vocations, and one that is new

grower · **forager** · apothecary · dispenser · inspector · smuggler —
plus:

> ⭐⭐⭐ **THE ASSAYER — the vocation that exists ONLY because you cannot
> judge the product.**

Someone who tests potency and **sells the verdict**. The credence-good
thesis producing a job directly, and the **fourth information vocation**
after the appraiser, the auditor and the physician.

⭐ Worth noticing the pattern: **this game keeps generating information
vocations because it has real hidden state.** Most games cannot support
them, because nothing is genuinely unknown.

### ⭐⭐ The assayer's instrument

The [instrumentation slate](./instrumentation-slate.md)'s three gates are
**capability, competence, calibration** — and an assay device is exactly
that shape:

> **A miscalibrated assay is worse than none.**

It converts *"I do not know"* into *"I know, wrongly"* — and **for a
credence good, false confidence is the most expensive error available.**
The calibration gate's best justification anywhere in the game, and it
gives the assayer's own reputation something concrete to rest on.

## ⭐ The second variant: SCALE

Running the probe on the business — the second variant of an apothecary is
**a manufactory.**

Workshop makes **bespoke**; factory makes **commodity** — which is the
buff economy's own **commodity-floor / quality-market** split appearing as
two business sizes. And it hands the legislature another honest object:
**does a factory need different oversight than a herbalist?** *(It does,
in reality, for reasons a player could derive.)*

## Open questions (for requirements)

1. **How many actives, and how distinct?** *Leans: few, strongly
   characterised* — the recognizability gate. A pharmacopoeia nobody can
   hold in their head is a spreadsheet.
2. **Is potency a Grade, a blend ratio, or both?** Physiology § Part 7b
   leans **Grade becomes potency**; the ratio question is separate and
   probably the apothecary's actual craft.
3. **Does a batch persist as an identity**, or is it a stamp on each unit?
   Decides how a recall works mechanically.
4. **Who can assay?** If anyone competent can, the assayer is a
   convenience; if it needs a calibrated instrument, it is a vocation.
   *Leans instrument.*
5. **Does the record actually support in-world statistics?** The claim
   that a hundred patients settle it needs the query surface to exist —
   probably MQL over the accountability / chronicle ledgers, and worth
   verifying before the pedagogy is promised.
