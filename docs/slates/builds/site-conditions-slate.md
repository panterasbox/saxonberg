## 1. ⭐⭐⭐ Senses are parts, and perception reads them **[DECIDED — the owner]**

- **Eyes and ears become parts** that **serve** new capacities, `vision` and `hearing`, as legs serve
  locomotion. Two eyes by mean: one lost is impaired (*a hobble, not a halt*), both lost is lost.
- **The vision profile reads the vision capacity** — a damaged eye dims what you perceive through
  the shipped per-viewer modifier, and detail degrades through the shipped thresholds. Hearing the
  same.
- ⭐⭐ **A shade is a viewer with no sense organs, perceiving anyway** — its senses fixed at
  *impaired*. **One rule covers the dead, the blinded and the one-eyed**, and every room in the game
  renders faint for the dead with no content rewritten.
- ⚠ **A cap, not a shift.** A shade perceives at most about *dim* — a shift would make unlit places
  black and lose shades in them. Always dusk: enough to find the way, never to read faces or scout
  well. Names still work — what a thing is *called* consults no perception — so a shade can `tell` a
  friend by name.

## 3. ⭐⭐ Occupational conditions — Ramazzini's question **[DECIDED — the owner]**

Bernardino Ramazzini founded occupational medicine (*De Morbis Artificum*, 1700) by asking every
patient ***"what is your trade?"*** The trades already handle the materials that cause these, so each
condition **derives from what the trade handles** — never authored onto the trade.

| trade | condition | site |
|---|---|---|
| smith, glassblower | burns; ⭐ **glassblower's cataract** — years of furnace infrared | skin, eyes |
| smithy, mill, mine | hearing loss from noise | ears |
| miner | crush; damps; dust lung over years | limbs, lungs |
| potash, dyeing | lye burns — the eyes worst (the caustic channel is reserved) | eyes, skin |
| diver | ⭐ **the bends** — the underwater column has depth and time | joints, spine |
| beekeeper | a sting into **anaphylaxis** | airway |
| butcher | cuts; meat-borne infection (contamination ships) | hands |

## 5. Build order **[LEAN]**

1. **Senses as parts and capacities** — four results at once: the shade's faint perception, blinding,
   deafness, the one-eyed fighter.
2. **Two lungs** — the collapsed lung.
3. **The airway** — choking.

Those three cover most of what kills people in real field medicine.

## 6. The lens pass

1. **Pedagogy** — every condition is a real first-aid lesson, and every occupational one answers
   Ramazzini's question from what the trade handles.
2. **Expression** — a species' plan declares its senses; a cyclops or a bat is rows.
3. **Immersion** — the dead see at dusk; a blow to the eye costs sight. **Participation** — the
   occupational physician.
4. **Values** — trades carry their hazards honestly; nobody's work is safe by fiat.
5. **Continuity** — the same parts in every epoch; hazards change with the tools.
6. **Economy** — protective gear (goggles, ear guards, the veil) has a demand that was there first.
7. **Governance** — workplace safety as law a polity may write.
