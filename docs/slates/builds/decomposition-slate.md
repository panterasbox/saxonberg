## The question

Rust in the rain, rot in a damp cellar, a sun-bleached sail, an iron cannon
concreting on a reef, a wooden pier eaten by shipworm, a body on a bed of
silt. Each is **a material degrading at a rate set by its environment** —
water, salt, oxygen, temperature, light, organisms — and nothing asks it
except the corpse and the larder.

## The one seam underwater design needs now

> **A material's decay rate is a function of its environment, reconciled
> on read from the time spent in it.**

Its first consumer is [underwater-slate](./underwater-slate.md) § 10, whose
table (iron, wood, bronze, glass, cloth, mechanisms, sealed containers;
cold-fresh-anoxic vs warm-salt) is **input to this conversation, not a
decision**. Its first modifier is a sealant coating (underwater § 8).
