## 1. ⭐⭐⭐ The printed thing is the static rung of a display **[DECIDED — lens 5, the owner's correction]**

The first draft made paper "a document, frozen" as if paper were the document's
natural body. The owner: *"a document is pure information. paper is quickly
becoming a relic … sure paper could show you a Document, but so could your phone
or your implant."* Continuity's test is *does the new object answer the same
commands*: the capability is **showing a document**, and paper, a screen and an
implant are three rungs of it. [display.md](../../subsystems/display.md) already
holds *"a tablet, a wall TV, the terminal's board are one thing."* **A printed
thing composes on the display substrate as its static rung**; `read` answers the
same on all three. What changes per rung is the dynamics, and that is what is
modeled:

| | paper | screen | implant |
|---|---|---|---|
| liveness | frozen at printing | live, refreshable | live, personal |
| power | none; needs light | the grid | the aether |
| cost of a copy | labor and material | nothing | nothing |
| ⭐ revocable? | **never** — once printed it is out | the publisher can edit or withdraw it | the same |
| reach | carried | networked | networked, private |
| what kills it | fire, water | an outage | a dead zone |

### Why paper survives the information age — not nostalgia

1. ⭐ **Irrevocability.** A screen copy can be silently edited or withdrawn; a
   printed one cannot. That is why censors seize *presses* and why underground
   printing matters.
2. **Where the network is not** — the aether dead zones
   ([ships-and-harbors-slate](./ships-and-harbors-slate.md) § 21), a place with
   no grid, a ship at sea.
3. **The bearer instrument** — a banknote, a ticket, a deed: holding it *is* the
   claim.
4. ⭐⭐ **Paper as material, not information.** In the real information age
   writing paper fell while **packaging grew** — cartons, corrugated board,
   labels, bags, filters, tissue. Lens 5's lesson: across the epochs paper's
   demand **moves** from carrying information to being a material.

### `copy` is one act across the epochs

The scribe (by hand, one at a time, errors by competence) → the press (a forme,
a print run, misprints and proofing) → the typewriter and stencil → the
photocopier → the printer (a peripheral of the screen). The publisher chooses
the carrier, and lens 6 decides: print costs per copy; the ticker is nearly free
but needs every reader connected, and it is revocable. The Gazette's print run
against its ticker line is a real business choice.

## 2. ⭐⭐ The printed-things family **[DECIDED]**

The book is one member. A printed thing is a **claim** read by somebody:

| printed thing | what it claims | who reads it |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ the label | what is in the sealed thing, and who made it | the buyer, the inspector |
| the lot code | which batch | the recall |
| the shipping mark | where the case is going | the carter, the depot |
| the shop sign | what is sold here | the passerby |
| the ticket, the banknote | a claim held in the hand | whoever takes it |
| the page | a frozen version of a document | the reader |

⭐ **This build ships the static display rung** **[DECIDED 2026-11-02 — the
owner: paper first]**. The first draft gave the rung to the cannery
([cannery-and-bottling-slate](./cannery-and-bottling-slate.md) § 6a); the order
flipped, and lens 5 agrees — the page is the rung's natural first member, a can
label a later one. The Gazette (which prints on nothing today) is the first
demand; the **label** ships here as a printed thing any good can carry, and the
cannery, when it builds, is a customer of the mill and the jobbing printer
rather than the rung's author.

**Packaging tiers** run through one product: primary (the can, the bottle —
cannery), secondary (the carton or case — paper's board, or carpentry's crate),
tertiary (the pallet — freight).

## 3. Paper — from rags **[DECIDED — trades ship medieval]**

- **Fiber: linen and cotton rags** — closing textiles' end of life and giving the
  **rag-and-bone man** a buyer. Wood pulp is a later rung, through forestry.
- **The process:** ret the rags · **beat** them to pulp in a stamper on a water
  mill (milling ships) · **dip** the mould and deckle in the vat · **couch** each
  sheet onto felt · **press** the post (the screw press ships in winemaking) ·
  **dry** in the loft · **size**.
- ⭐ **Sizing teaches absorbency.** Gelatin size stops ink bleeding — the
  [rendering slate](../tails/rendering-slate.md)'s glue and gelatin get their
  first demand. **Unsized paper is blotting paper**, a product of its own.
- **The watermark** — wire sewn onto the mould (wire drawing from the cannery
  build's metal-forming rung); the anti-forgery feature of the banknote.
  ⚠ **Waits on metal-forming** now that paper goes first: this build ships the
  mould without one, and the watermark lands with wire drawing.
- **Grade** — sheet weight, evenness, color (iron browns rag paper, the same
  logic as glass), sizing.
- **Parchment and vellum** — skin limed, stretched and scraped, never tanned: a
  side branch of tanning, rows on a shipped trade.
- **Board** — the carton's corrugated or pasted board: paper as material.

## 4. Ink **[DECIDED]**

- ⭐ **Iron-gall writing ink** — oak galls + copperas + gum: **exactly the dyer's
  iron-tannin black**, which ships as a mordant chemistry. One chemistry, two
  trades.
- **Printing ink** — lampblack (soot, from the fuel chain) ground into boiled
  linseed oil, the paint build's drying oil.

## 5. Printing **[DECIDED]**

- **Type founding** — type metal (lead, tin, antimony) cast in a hand mould:
  moulding, shipped.
- **Composing** — the compositor sets type. ⭐ **Misprints derive from
  competence**: a novice's page carries errors, and **proofreading** is the act
  that finds them (*competence resolves detail*).
- **The press** — the forme locked up, inked and pulled; each pull is one
  physical copy of the frozen version.
- **Distribution** — the type goes back in the case: type is capital reused.
- **Printmaking on the same press** — woodcut (relief, forestry's shipped
  `carve`); engraving (a copper plate and burin on a rolling press — the
  metal-forming rung); etching waits on acids. The block or plate is a reusable
  original; the image is the author's own.
- **Bookbinding** — fold, sew the gatherings, boards, leather, glue.

## 7. Governance — licensing the press **[DECIDED — lens 7]**

A polity may register presses, censor them, or guarantee their freedom —
and it matters **because paper cannot be recalled**. The
[press](../../subsystems/press.md) subsystem's anonymous press room becomes
**the clandestine printer**: a press that answers to no license, with a criterion
and an appeal for whoever tries to shut it.
