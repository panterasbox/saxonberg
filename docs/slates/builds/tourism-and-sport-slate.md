# Tourism and sport — catering is a choice of whose taste you serve

> **Status: UNBUILT** — and ⭐ **mostly not a mechanism**: the traffic
> axis, the regular, the specialization gradient and renown-per-scope all
> ship or are doctrine. What is missing is **content and one authored
> field.**
> **Left:** the **district demand profile** (a district needs a *taste*) ·
> **renown at place scope** as the only honest fame signal · the
> **guidebook** as a fallible document · the guide/tout and the excursion
> · the **souvenir** over `provenance` · a sport's **diagnosed failure**
> · the **public log** as content · and ⛔ the refusals below.
> **Size:** a tail for the mechanism, a build for the resort that
> exercises it.

Designed 2026-10-09 out of the water-trade audit
([navigable-water-slate](./navigable-water-slate.md) § 5c, *tourism and
watersports*). ⚠ Opened with the wrong question — *what is sport* — and
the owner's correction is the frame:

> *"It's **football-as-content**. **Skiing-as-content.**"*

⭐ Which is `eotl-craft.md`'s own three-kinds split: a **System** is
remembered by *having done it*, and we have *"no doctrine, no tooling and
no vocabulary"* for it. See
[eotl-craft.md § Two sports areas read closely](../../eotl-craft.md) for
the two shipped precedents and what each one actually did.

## 1. ⭐⭐⭐ Tourists and regulars want opposite things from the same room

The owner's thesis, and it is the design:

> *"Some locations would have more tourists and some would have more
> regulars. You usually can't have both — you have to pick which you're
> going to cater to."*

- the **tourist** wants the place to be *as advertised* — legible,
  performing itself
- the **regular** wants it to be *as it was* — unchanged, and **not**
  performing

⭐⭐ **And the coupling is asymmetric**, which makes it a dynamic rather
than a preference: a tourist's presence **degrades** the regular's
experience (crowding, the place playing to strangers), while a regular's
presence **authenticates** the tourist's — *real locals drink here* —
until there are too many tourists, the regulars leave, and the tourist is
standing in a fake place. **Tourism destroys what it came for, and it is
self-limiting.**

### ⭐⭐ The axis is already named, and the mechanism already ships

- `content-craft.md` § 5: repetition is a property of **traffic**, in four
  kinds — *low · favorited · transit · **first contact***. ⭐ **"First
  contact" is the tourist and "favorited" is the regular.** The craft
  doctrine has the axis; tourism is what happens when an economy sits on
  it.
- `employment.md`'s **CALL** is `call: regulars | rota`, resolving
  *capability → **your regular** → the freest → rotation*. ⭐⭐ **The
  regular is already a first-class concept with code behind it.**
- `attendant.md`'s queue-and-lease gives a storefront **finite
  attention** — so a tourist-heavy venue *has a queue*, and a regular's
  advantage is **being known.**

**No new substrate. Tourism gives shipped machinery stakes.**

## 2. ⚠⚠ CORRECTED — catering is not a business TYPE

The first draft mapped business types to clienteles (the innkeeper wants
tourists, the baker wants regulars). ⛔ **Wrong**, and the owner's
correction carries more structure than the thing it corrected:

> *"Bakeries aren't automatically just for regulars. A bakery whose
> business is 90% selling sourdough loaves on Pier 39 is different than a
> bakery downtown selling all the staples plus some specialties. And the
> sourdough may be something the entire city consumes — a bakery selling
> bean cakes in Chinatown is different than one selling munchies in the
> Haight. **Catering to tourists is more about whose preferences you're
> going to prioritize.** It's not necessarily about the market, though
> obviously the market is shaped by consumer preferences."*

⭐⭐⭐ **Two independent axes, and tourist-versus-local is only one cut of
the second:**

| axis | |
|---|---|
| **range** | narrow (one thing, done best) ↔ broad (the staples plus some specialties) |
| **whose taste** | a **city** (sourdough) · a **community** (bean cakes) · a **subculture** (the Haight) · a trade · a class — and *visitors* as one option among these |

⭐ **The sourdough is the instructive case** because it is **both**: the
whole city eats it *and* it is what visitors buy. A city signature is one
product with two markets, and the pier shop and the downtown shop sell the
same loaf to different people.

⚠ Note what is *not* on the list: the bean-cake and Haight shops are
**narrow-range shops catering to regulars**, and neither has anything to
do with tourism. **Narrow range is not a tourist strategy.**

### ⭐⭐⭐ The structural home is already written

`settlement-model.md`'s specialization gradient: ***"a city is where the
general store fragments."***

**That is this.** The store fragments because **density makes a narrow
clientele a viable market** — in a village one bakery serves everyone
broadly and indifferently; in a city a bakery can live on bean cakes
alone, because enough people who want bean cakes live within walking
distance. So **catering is the content face of the fragmentation
mechanism**, which is also why *"zoning exists because of density, not
order."*

⭐⭐ **What is missing is that a DISTRICT needs a TASTE.** The sixteen
needs already say what a *town* must meet or import; a district is a
sub-unit with **its own weighting plus specialties.** So *"Chinatown wants
bean cakes"* is a **district-level demand row** — ⛔ **not an
individual-preference system**, which we do not need and should not build.

### ⭐⭐⭐ And the payoff is an authoring tool

> **You learn what a neighbourhood is by what its shops sell.** Nobody
> needs a sign. **The counter is the description.**

That is `content-craft.md`'s *cultural content is what players know that
the text never said*, achieved with **rows rather than prose** — and it
costs nothing. ⭐ It also already has an exemplar in our own notes:
Gnomelands' bakery, where **plain bread is the most expensive thing on
the board because it is *gnomish*** (`eotl-craft.md` § *The joke is four
integers*). **The sign is the characterization.**

## 3. ⭐⭐⭐ Tourism is content discovery wearing a diegetic costume

The frame that matters most for a platform whose thesis is that players
make the world. The owner's hope was *"so many people cranking out new
content all the time"* — and:

> **A brand-new area has zero traffic. Nobody knows it exists.**

**Tourism is the mechanism by which traffic finds authored content**,
which makes it one of the most important systems in the realm and not a
flavour trade.

⛔⛔ **And the constraint falls straight out: it must never become a
recommendation algorithm.** `measurement.md` is explicit that
**neutrality is the attention company's answer, not ours** — so no
trending list, no *popular this week*, no engagement ranking. Discovery
is **diegetic and authored by a person**:

- ⭐ **the guidebook** — a document somebody wrote, and ⭐⭐ **the same
  object as the fallible chart** ([navigable-water-slate](./navigable-water-slate.md)
  § 3b): out of date, wrong, or paid for, with nothing correcting it.
  **A guidebook that is wrong is content.**
- **the guide, or the tout** — pilotage for tourists; local knowledge
  whose value spoils
- **the coach or excursion** — a logistics route that exists *because*
  people want to go
- **word of mouth** — an NPC who mentions a place, which is where the
  LLM-NPC work pays

> ⭐⭐⭐ **Tourism is content discovery that a PERSON has to author** —
> strictly better than an algorithm for us, because the guidebook writer
> and the guide are **vocations**, and being recommended becomes something
> somebody did for you rather than something a system computed.

### The economy on top

- **The inn.** `vocations.md`'s own worked example, where the honest
  justification was *"NPC schedules make the inn the place that stays
  open."* ⭐ Tourism gives it a **second and better one: visitors have
  nowhere to sleep.**
- ⭐⭐ **The souvenir** — an object whose entire value is **where it came
  from**, which is `provenance.md`: the authorship ledger with a
  context-derived stamp. **A good that is worthless except that you were
  there.** Shipped machinery, perfect consumer.
- **The viewpoint** — a room whose only function is to be looked at from.
- **The resort** — § 5.

⛔ **Fame is renown at PLACE scope, never a score on prose.** *Decor is
never a gauge.* A place is famous because **people went and talked about
it**, which is the same mechanism as a person's reputation pointed at a
location. ⭐ An author's writing matters **through players, indirectly** —
which is the right answer for this project and not a compromise.

## 4. ⭐⭐ The town has to choose, and the choice has losers

**Lens 4.** Catering to tourists means legibility, signage, prices
strangers can pay, and the place performing itself; catering to regulars
means the opposite. **The innkeeper and the souvenir-seller want tourists;
the baker and the regulars do not.** A council fight with real sides where
nobody is wrong — and **lens 7's appeal, for a regular, is leaving.**

⭐⭐ And the cruel true one: **the regulars' presence is the product being
sold, and they are not paid for it.** What tourists come for is other
people being at home, and no mechanism can compensate that.

### ⭐⭐⭐ The tourist trap is a COLLAPSE OF FEEDBACK, not crowding

The mechanism, which the first draft had as a vibe:

Narrow range normally means **high expectation** — your market is small
and knows the difference, so `Grade` and renown bite hard. **A broad store
can be mediocre at everything; a one-product shop cannot.**

**Except when the one product is sold to tourists**, who **cannot tell the
difference, are not coming back, and were not going to anyway.**

> ⭐⭐⭐ **So a tourist-facing shop can be WORSE and survive — and that is
> the engine of tourist-trap decay.** The owner's rational choice degrades
> the product, and the only people who would notice have already left.
>
> **A town that chooses tourists is choosing to stop being corrected.**

### ⭐⭐ A new lens-5 shape: tourism is a CYCLE

Every other obsolescence arc found this session is one-way (the ferryman,
the iceman, the laundress, the ditch company). A resort's **loops**:

> discovered → fashionable → crowded → ruined → forgotten → **quaint**

So a place can come back, and **people who were there at different points
in the cycle have genuinely different relationships to it.** The first
lens-5 outcome that is not a loss.

## 5. Sport — and the two precedents are opposite strategies

⚠ **The mistake to not repeat:** this section first contained *a theory of
what sport is*, which is `eotl-craft.md`'s third column answering a
second-column question. **A System is not a definition; it is a thing you
build for months and remember having done.** The two shipped precedents
chose differently and both are valid:

| | strategy | cost | what you remember |
|---|---|---|---|
| **football** | a **venue and a cast**, with the sport's **vocabulary converted into shipped mechanics** | one good idea and 161 rooms | *the referee threw a flag at me and it did seventeen damage* |
| **skiing** | a **real simulation** of the one act the sport is about | months | **doing it** — plus the log of everyone who died |

Read closely in [eotl-craft.md](../../eotl-craft.md). The five
transferable things, none of which needs a sport *system*:

1. ⭐⭐⭐ **Convert the vocabulary; do not simulate the game.** The
   referee's penalty is combat with renamed numbers, and **the damage
   figure *is* the yardage.**
2. ⭐⭐⭐ **Diagnose the failure.** *Too steep* / *too bumpy* / *bad luck*
   is worth more than any difficulty curve — and it is already our
   doctrine (*competence resolves detail*; *the refusal is the progression
   UI*) with **no exemplar anywhere in our tree.**
3. ⭐⭐ **Two parameters against two stats**, so difficulty is a **shape**
   rather than a scalar. The right way to grade a ski run, a dive site or
   a river stretch.
4. ⭐⭐ **The log is content.** A persistent public record of failures with
   place and cause. We have the chronicle substrate and **nothing writes a
   list anybody would read.**
5. ⭐ **Position as a room name** makes every message localize itself for
   free — which is why the broadcast line works.

### What sport brings that we have none of

⭐⭐⭐ **Cooperative, non-combat play.** Nearly everything in the realm is
individual labour or adversarial. **A crew boat is eight people, one hull,
and a failure mode of being out of time** — `combat-formations`' party
shape pointed at cooperation instead of violence. A relay, a tug-of-war, a
team game: co-presence that is neither work nor a fight. **A real gap.**

### ⭐⭐ And it answers an open ask in the deck

[`27-time`](../../lenses/27-time.md) records the gym's unanswered
question: fitness is *"untestable except **relatively**, which is a third
ask for the gym pattern."*

> ⭐⭐⭐ **A race is a relative measurement.** You cannot measure fitness
> absolutely and you can certainly measure who got there first.

**Sport is the instrument the gym has been missing** — and the body half
ships: `sports` is a Discipline row, and `exertion.md` has the gym
archetype, `LoadDevice` and the five slow stocks. Lens 6's own gym example
says *"work produces goods and a body; a gym produces only the body."*
**Sport is the gym's social form.**

### Smaller, and all cheap

- **Spectating.** A match is the first **scheduled** thing people gather
  to watch — a beat for the press, a purpose for `display`'s
  `watch … on <screen>`, a subject for livestream, a **season** for the
  calendar.
- **Betting** ⚠ as a **contract**, not a gambling system: a wager on an
  outcome neither party controls is `contract.md`'s clauses over
  verifiable conditions, with escrow. ⛔ It goes wrong the moment a house
  takes a cut.
- ⭐⭐ **The campus is the historically right home.** Organised sport came
  *out of* universities — football, rowing and athletics were codified
  there — and a university is exactly where rules get **written down.**
- **Watersports** is where the halves meet: rowing, swimming, sailing,
  diving. ⭐ **A regatta is a navigation contest**, so it rides
  [navigable-water-slate](./navigable-water-slate.md) § 3b directly.

### ⭐⭐ And the governance reading, which is not decoration

A sport is **a contest with agreed rules and no stakes outside itself** —
the exact inverse of our economy, which has real stakes and no agreed
rules. [`33-rules`](../../lenses/33-rules.md) carries Parlett's taxonomy
and the **house rule → law → official → written** arrow, and says that
arrow *is what the Compact promoted to a mechanic.*

> ⭐⭐ **So a sport is the Compact at play scale**: players invent a game,
> argue the rules, amend them, appoint an official, and discover the
> official's authority is a real problem — **with nothing at stake but the
> game.** The safest practice ground for governance there is, on the same
> machinery.

⭐ And a resolution for the deck: [`37-fairness`](../../lenses/37-fairness.md)
records that across 116 Schell lenses *"'fair' only ever means 'even
contest'"*, treating it as evidence lens 7 is unprecedented. **Sport is
the one place in this game where "even contest" is the correct definition
of fair** — handicaps, divisions, cheating, the referee. Everywhere else
the fairness question is constitutional; here it is athletic, and the
contrast is worth stating rather than leaving as an oversight.

⚠ **Lens 6 is where sport is weakest.** A sport produces nothing, and the
honest answer is that it produces **an audience** — attention the press
and the streaming layer can monetize — while the athlete consumes time and
a body. Thin, but real, and better stated than dressed up.

## 6. The resort is the locality that exercises the most of this

Tourism × sport, which is **EotL's resort cabal and sports cabal in one
place** — and `eotl-craft.md` notes *"The Resort died of 'changes in the
core code.'"*

A resort is a place whose product is **being somewhere else**: staffed by
regulars, visited by first-contact traffic, with sport as the activity,
a season, a lodge, and a cycle (§ 4). ⭐⭐ **And skiing is the content that
consumes the entire cold stack at once** —
[climate-slate](./climate-slate.md)'s latitude and lapse rate, snow depth,
the ground-condition locomotion axis, avalanche off the watershed's
`snowpackMm`, thermal, textiles' `clo`, the lodge as a warming house, and
this slate's tourism. **It is the integration test for the whole winter
design**, and it would prove out six systems that currently have no
consumer between them.
