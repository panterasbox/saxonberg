# Reading — text on a surface, and who can read it

> **Status: UNBUILT** — a question raised 2026-11-10 during the
> controller-liquidity build (W18), waiting for its own design conversation.
> No design here yet: this slate holds the owner's question list and nothing
> else.
> **Left:** the whole conversation — languages, scrolls and spellbooks beside
> their ordinary twins, newspapers and pamphlets and printing, and what
> "reading a surface" is.
> **Size:** a tail, until the conversation sizes it

## The question

Controller-liquidity W8 put a `Readable` protocol under `read` — one act over
three hosts: a screen (`Display`), a maker's mark (`Marked`), and written marks
on paper (the sheet, the page, the book). The owner, 2026-11-10, kept it — for
now — pending a broader design: *"the idea of reading things on a surface
needs a broader conversation."*

What the owner named for that conversation:

- **Languages in a text game** — resisted before for mechanical reasons, now
  probably needed.
- **Magic scrolls and spellbooks** — how they work compared with their
  non-magic counterparts (a scroll beside a letter, a spellbook beside a
  book).
- **Newspapers and pamphlets** — and the whole printing design with them.
- **Reading things on a surface** — what `read` is, across a screen, a mark
  and a page.

Until that conversation: `Readable` stays as W8 shipped it.
