# Alignment & religion slate

> **Status: VERY preliminary — RPG-layer, deferred.** Captured because
> char-gen is the right time to think about identity. Alignment rides the
> **advancement / breadcrumb layer**, so the real design waits for that.
> *Everything here is tentative.* Actual demigods are **content**, to be
> designed later.

Surfaced while triaging char-gen's identity choices. Two entangled
threads: the **morality/alignment system** and **religion (worship +
deities)**. The design order is *alignment first, religion emerges from
it.*

## Alignment

**Keep the D&D 3×3 grid** (Lawful/Neutral/Chaotic × Good/Neutral/Evil) —
familiar, time-tested, meme-able — **but make it the *world's* coordinate
system, not the player's.** The morality axis maps to **narrative roles**,
and players occupy only one row:

- **Good row = players + allies** (protagonists). Players express only the
  **Law axis** (Lawful / Neutral / Chaotic Good). **Good is a floor, not a
  choice.**
- **Evil row = the *other*** — antagonists, cults, corrupted places/forces
  (content). Players are **never** evil.
- **Neutral row = the indifferent world** — nature, animals, cosmic
  forces, the wilderness itself.

Authors still place every NPC/faction/location on the full grid (the memes
survive); players just can't *be* the bottom rows.

**Alignment is derived/witnessed, not chosen.** It emerges from your
**deeds** (your [breadcrumbs](./breadcrumb-slate.md)) — the breadcrumb
model applied to morality. So there is **no alignment pick at char-gen**
(the claimed-lean idea was dropped — "don't care what they lean"). The
claimed-vs-witnessed tension still applies if a deity is involved (your
chosen god vs. what your trail shows).

**The good floor — what happens when a good player does a bad thing?** It
**never flips your alignment** (there's no Evil cell to fall into). Bad
acts land as **social/reputation consequences** (the world remembers —
recognition/social-graph layer), not an alignment event.

**Harm lives OFF the alignment grid.** The Law axis is *order ↔ freedom* (a
**stance**), not a harm-ledger — "a good killed a good → unlawful?" is a
category error. Community-health is a **mechanics problem, not a
morality-classification problem** (the **Deep Rock Galactic** lesson):

- conflict points **outward at the *other*** (= evil-as-antagonist; the
  same reason Eternal City's PvP kill-zone Heart was rejected);
- **interdependence** makes cooperation the winning play — the **aspiration
  archetypes are complementary roles** (Healer / Guardian / Builder /
  Seeker / Mentor / Founder = a DRG-style squad);
- **shared goals, reviving/help mechanics, camaraderie rituals**
  (ESP-emotes → a "Rock and Stone" salute culture).

So "no evil players" and "a DRG-quality community" are the *same design*:
everyone's on the good side, the enemy is out there, the mechanics reward
pulling together. (Conflict model = fundamentally **PvE-narrative** —
load-bearing for the eventual combat/quest/wilderness design.)

## Religion (worship + deities)

**Worship is *chosen*; alignment is *derived* — keep both, let them
diverge.** Choosing a god is an *affiliation/relationship* (like picking an
origin), not a stat, so it doesn't violate earned-not-chosen. The richness
is the **interplay**: a **deity is a reactive character** who watches your
deeds (breadcrumbs) and responds *in personality* to the harmony or tension
between its values and your **witnessed** alignment. Your **chosen god** =
declared aspiration; your **deeds** = reality; the gap *talks back*. This
makes worship a live relationship (props-real), and deities the
biggest-**personality** NPCs in the game (a forcing function for the NPC
stack — see [[user-loves-npcs]]).

### Pantheon structure — a tree, not a crowd

Avoid the flat *American Gods* sprawl. Two tiers, mapping the grid onto a
**hierarchy**:

- **3 high gods = the morality axis** (Good / Neutral / Evil). Distant
  cosmic forces — and *literally the three narrative camps*: Good (the
  protagonists' side), Evil (the single great adversary the cults serve),
  Neutral (the indifferent world / nature / balance).
- **Demigods = the Law axis + domains + personality**, scoped *under* each
  high god (order-saint & trickster-saint under Good; nature-spirits &
  balance-keepers under Neutral; tyrant & destroyer under Evil). These are
  the relatable, reactive NPCs people actually worship.

Division of labor: **high gods = abstract cosmic forces** (you don't chat
with them); **demigods = the personality canvas**. Fits how we build
hierarchies elsewhere — a demigod *serves/extends* a high god like a Biome
leaf under a root; content-authorable like the species/biome rosters.
Stays three at the top, scales to as much flavor as you author below.

### What's chosen at char-gen

- **Pick a patron *demigod*** — from the **Good and Neutral** demigods (the
  relatable, domain-specific saints); high-god allegiance is implied behind
  it. Or **"seeking / unaffiliated"** — the **default**; worship is
  **opt-in**, never forced (standalone/welcoming principle —
  [[game-stands-alone]]).
- The 3 high gods are backdrop, not picks. (Texture: a rare austere devotee
  worships a *high god* directly — abstract faith.)
- **Place:** declared at char-gen (claimed), with a **chapel** as
  the service to formalize/change it (place-vs-anywhere rule; the legacy
  *Temple of the Ages* lineage gives the gods a building).

### Can you pray to an evil deity?

- **Not at char-gen** — the creation roster is good/neutral demigods only
  (don't start anyone in darkness; don't make it read as "pick evil").
- **Yes, as an *earned* arc** — a *good person tangled with a dark god* is
  one of the richest character types the chosen-worship-vs-derived-alignment
  split unlocks (a bargain, a cult legacy, a temptation). It **never
  corrupts** (the good floor): the dark demigod is a permanent push-pull
  (demands, favor, social cost), not a fall. Stakes are
  relational/social/narrative, never an alignment-flip.

### Devotional flavor (lean light / emergent)

Starting standing (devout / new convert / lapsed / doubter / estranged) and
structure (patron / pantheon / eclectic) are claimed seeds — but a
doubter→devout arc is better *earned in play* than dialed at a kiosk, so
offer maybe the starting standing and let the rest deepen.
