## 8. The lens pass (2026-10-18)

1. **Pedagogy** — firing temperature → vitrification → porosity (the brick
   rule); leather-hard as a window; moisture mismatch cracking joints;
   bisque vs glaze; reduction colour; thermal shock; a glaze is a glass;
   expansion mismatch (crazing); acidity × lead (leaching); evaporative
   cooling in a porous jar.
2. **Expression** — the ordinary case is rows (forms, glazes, a second
   pottery); ⭐ a potter's hand shows in the pot (wall thickness, form, glaze);
   an impression of any pot can be slip-cast (moulding § 0a); kintsugi turns
   damage into expression.
3. **Immersion** — a beginner's heavy mug, crazed glaze, the smoking kiln, a
   sweating water jar. **Participation** — the potter, the kiln-burner, the
   china mender, the stallholder, the bargee; ⭐ *can the polity do something
   we didn't want?* — **ban lead glaze** (or fail to), with a measurable
   public-health effect.
4. **Values** — ⚠ lead glaze (cheap now, poison later, silently); kintsugi
   against replacement; deskilling when slip-casting undercuts the thrower.
5. **Continuity** — coil → wheel → slip cast → jigger; pit → updraft →
   tunnel kiln: the same `shape`, `carve`, `fit`, `dip`, `repair`.
6. **Economy** — ⭐ demand shipped first (21 stocked goods) and recurring
   (breakage); consumes clay, fireclay, ash, lead, salt, tin, fuel; produces
   ware by body and grade, sherds and grog; the 21 stock lines re-pointed.
7. **Governance** — lead-glaze bans; salt-glaze fumes as a nuisance; the
   china mender licensed or not; each a criterion and an appeal when
   designed.

**Altitude** — *invariant*: a body's properties derive from its clay and the
heat it reached; skill decides the pot deterministically. *Grain*: the
firing thresholds, the wall-thickness bands, the lead leach rate. *Title*:
the Delight works, the Terminus stall.
