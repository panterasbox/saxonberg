# Climate and water — requirements

**Kind:** platform
**Leads from:** [climate-slate](../slates/builds/climate-slate.md) ·
[navigable-water-slate](../slates/builds/navigable-water-slate.md)
§§ 4, 5b–5c
**First consumer, in this build:** a realm with a winter somewhere in it,
a sugar maple whose run finally opens, a main that freezes for an honest
reason, and a pump that empties a working.

⭐⭐⭐ **The thesis: this realm has TWO climates and they disagree by nine
Kelvin.**

The sky says the coldest it ever gets, anywhere, in any season, is about
276 K. The river says the catchment's winter is 272 K at sea level and
colder with altitude — and the river is the one that banks snow, melts a
freshet and freezes a water main. **Both are shipped. Both are read. A
main can freeze today while the street above it is at 285 K.**

> ⭐⭐ **So this is a UNIFICATION, not a feature** — and the model to
> unify onto is the river's, because it has the one term the sky lacks:
> **where you are.**

---

## What already exists

- **A sky temperature** — one baseline for the whole realm, with an
  annual and a daily swing and a three-hour lag, so the coldest hour is
  near dawn. ⚠ **One latitude means one climate**: two settlements at
  opposite ends of the map get the same winter on the same day.
- **A catchment temperature** — a seasonal mean at sea level plus a
  **lapse rate**, which is what makes altitude bank the snow and is why
  the same storm rains on the city and snows on the headwaters.
- ⭐ **The celestial model is already written for any latitude**, and it
  already answers the **polar** limits honestly — a whole rotation of
  day, or none at all. Nothing in the realm can currently ask it.
- **Elevation** is a zone field with an ancestor walk.
- **The watercourse** ships flow, the snowpack and the melt freshet, a
  derived navigability that changes with the season, rights as dated
  records, storage that holds the freshet back, contamination by kind,
  and a conveyance ladder whose supply states include **frozen**.
- ⭐ And water and power already meet at **one equation read in two
  directions** — what a falling river generates and what a rising one
  costs — with both efficiency figures already authored.

### ⚠ What is broken or absent

1. ⛔ **Nothing has ever frozen and nothing ever will**, so a drying
   stall authored on three profiles has never fired, a cold store has
   never been needed, and a freeze has no driver at all.
2. ⛔ **The two climates are already producing an incoherent world** in a
   shipped read, and nobody noticed because nobody stood beside a frozen
   main in shirtsleeves and said so.
3. ⚠ **Snow is decided by the weather's own grammar rather than by the
   temperature**, so it can snow at 290 K.
4. ⛔ **A pump does not exist**, and three separate designs defer it —
   each assuming it belonged to one of the others. **Everything below a
   water table is unreachable**, including a mine, a quarry pit and a
   flooded working.
5. ⛔ **The moon ships and the tide it drives does not** — named twice as
   a deferral, with the forcing function complete, pure and memoised,
   and nothing asking it anything.
6. ⚠ **A reach has no character of its own** — no gradient, no bed, no
   obstruction — so every stretch of every river reads the same, which
   is the gap that makes a gorge indistinguishable from a millpond.
7. ⚠ **A withdrawal is subtracted in full**, so nothing a farm diverts
   ever comes back.

## Goals

- ⭐⭐⭐ **One temperature, derived from where and when you are.** The
  sun's angle and the day's length at *this* latitude on *this* day
  decide the heat arriving; a lag and a damping decide how much of it the
  ground still holds — and ⭐ **the damping is what makes a coast mild
  and an interior brutal**, which is one number doing the work of a
  table.
- **A place can say where on the world it is**, inherited the way its
  height already is — so **an author writes one number and gets a
  winter**: snow, a short season, a dark December, husbandry limits and a
  frozen main, with nothing else to declare.
- ⭐⭐ **And the polar case becomes reachable** — a place where the sun
  does not rise for weeks, where the sky stops being a clock, and where
  a town's day runs on something other than daylight.
- **Altitude still bites**, on top of latitude and in the same
  expression, because a headwater is colder than the valley under it.
- **Snow falls because it is cold**, not because the weather grammar said
  so — and ⭐ **a warm winter means rain**, which arrives now instead of
  in May, so a pleasant winter floods in January and empties the river in
  August, and nobody in the valley experienced a drought.
- ⭐⭐ **A cold place reads cold in everything that already measures
  temperature** — drying stalls, a store earns its keep, an unheated room
  goes cold, an uninsulated body suffers, wool starts paying, moisture
  stops moving in the soil, and the maple's run finally opens on the
  freeze and thaw it actually needs.
- ⭐ **Water is liquid, solid, or on its way between**, so a main can
  freeze for the same reason the puddle outside it did, and the two
  agree.
- ⭐⭐ **The tide moves**, computed from the moon the realm already has —
  so the edge of the water is somewhere different in the morning, a
  pool fills and empties on a schedule a person can learn, and **getting
  cut off is a real way to drown in four feet of water.**
- ⭐⭐⭐ **A person can lift water.** A pump makes a source above you
  reachable and a flooded place workable, and it costs capital and then
  energy forever — which is the same equation the river already turns,
  read backwards. **Its consumers are a mine, a quarry pit, a field and
  a harbour, and all four are waiting.**
- ⭐ **A stretch of river is distinguishable from another stretch** — it
  has a fall, a bed and things in the way, so a gorge is not a millpond
  and a ford is not a pool.
- ⭐⭐ **Not everything taken is consumed.** What a field does not drink
  returns downstream — and therefore ⭐⭐⭐ **lining your ditch to stop it
  leaking INJURES the person below you**, because the leak was their
  supply. The engine can price both sides exactly and cannot say who is
  right.
- **A field can be watered from a pipe and not only from the sky**, so an
  irrigated farm stops suffering weather and starts suffering **law.**
- **A water declares what it is made of atmospherically** — a surface and
  a submerged reading — so the things that already ask the air what it is
  doing get an answer over water.

## Non-goals

- ⛔ **No ice trade.** No harvest, no ice house, no retail, no
  thickness. This build makes ice **possible** and sells none.
- ⛔ **No hemisphere split in this build** unless it falls out free. The
  season is day-of-year arithmetic today and widening it reaches the
  weather bias, the husbandry windows, a tap's opener and the catchment's
  own table. ⚠ **Decide it, write the decision down, and defer the
  work.**
- ⛔ **No zone or frame changes.** The maritime build owns the frame.
  **This build must not touch it**, and must not add a depth field.
- ⛔ **No locomotion on snow**, no skis, no sleds, no avalanche. Snow
  depth exists so somebody later can stand on it.
- ⛔ **No metabolic rebalance.** ⚠ An unfed body already dies by game-hour
  seven at 295 K; at 265 K it dies faster. **This build must not make
  the realm's coldest place somewhere a drive cannot survive** — so the
  cold goes where content wants it, not everywhere.
- ⛔ **No irrigation works as a trade** — no ditch company, no rider, no
  curtailment. The water side only.

## The drive

1. **Stand somewhere cold and somewhere warm on the same day**, and read
   both.
2. **Climb**, and get colder, with nothing else changing.
3. **Go far enough toward a pole** that the sun does not rise, and be
   told so by the sky.
4. **Watch something freeze** — a vessel of water left in the cold — and
   watch it thaw.
5. **Try to dry something in a frozen place** and be refused in the
   process's own words.
6. ⭐ **Open a maple** on a freeze-and-thaw window, which has never been
   possible.
7. **Read a water main as frozen**, and read the street it is under, and
   have the two **agree**.
8. **Find the tide somewhere different** than it was an hour ago, and
   reach something at low water you could not reach at high.
9. ⭐ **Pump a flooded working dry**, and then stop paying for it and
   watch it fill.
10. **Lift water to a place above its source**, and be billed for it.
11. **Walk two stretches of one river** and have them read differently —
    a fall, a bed, something in the way.
12. **Divert water onto a field**, and measure the river **below** the
    diversion, and find that **not all of it is gone.**
13. ⭐⭐ **Line the ditch**, measure again, and find the person below you
    worse off.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **One expression answers "how warm is it here"**, and the catchment
   and the sky give the **same answer** for the same place and time.
2. Temperature **derives** from the sun's geometry at a place's latitude
   and day, with a lag and a damping — and the damping is **authored per
   place**, so a coast and an interior differ.
3. **Latitude is resolved for a place** and inherited, and every existing
   reader is unchanged at the old value.
4. **The polar case is reachable** and the sky says so.
5. **Altitude still lowers temperature**, in the same expression.
6. **Snow is decided by temperature.** ⛔ It cannot snow at 290 K.
7. At least **six shipped mechanisms that have never fired now fire** —
   the drying stall, a freeze, a cold store, the husbandry cold limit,
   the insulation ladder, and the maple window.
8. A **frozen** supply state and the **ambient** at the same place
   **agree.**
9. ⭐ **The tide is computed from the moon**, is the same for every
   observer at a given time, and moves the water's edge.
10. A **pump** exists, is the river's own equation read backwards, costs
    capital and then energy, and **empties a flooded working.**
11. A **reach has authored character** that reads differently between two
    stretches.
12. ⭐⭐ A withdrawal has a **consumptive fraction**, the remainder
    **returns** downstream, and **raising efficiency measurably harms
    the user below.**
13. A bed can be watered **from a conduit** as well as from the sky.
14. ⛔ **No place a drive must survive became lethally cold.**
