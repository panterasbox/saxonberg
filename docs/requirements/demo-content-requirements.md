# Demo content — requirements

The content-authoring tract of the education-video effort: the small,
permanent world beats the five videos film against. Seeded by
[demo-slate.md](../slates/builds/demo-slate.md) (items 2–5, 7), with
briefs in [education-videos.md](../education-videos.md) (§ Pre-script
gap audit) and the ceremony doctrine in
[lenses/moments.md](../lenses/moments.md). Everything here is
world-content that earns its place independent of the videos — the
never-half-grown rule applies: each piece ships as a real, complete
fixture of the world, not a film set.

## Goals

- **The campus opens on its authored road network.** The v1
  stitch of the Eternal University campus per
  [staging/eternal-university/campus-map.md](../staging/eternal-university/campus-map.md)
  and the city plan
  ([staging/terminus-city.md](../staging/terminus-city.md)): the
  Quad as the Heart-geometry compass junction, Eternal Way as the
  south→north spine gate-to-gate, Silver Street east toward the
  Mere, Limbo Lane west to the Residential Quarter — wiring the
  already-built Duncan Hall onto its own campus. Road prose is
  pulled from the EC source (`docs/eternal/`) per the map's
  pull→polish→adapt method; road materials are real floors
  (adornments), so Silver rings and Limbo springs.
- **The registrar's hall functions, not just ceremonies.**
  Student Services stands on Silver Street; inside, the registrar
  (this build's one NPC carve) answers the **advisor read**
  (derive-on-read transcript navigation — the hall's headline
  function), keeps the Rolls (the deeds registry's public face),
  and conducts the conferral ceremony — thirty filmable seconds
  that mint the **conferral certificate** into the recipient's
  credential wallet, obeying the civic-registry three nevers. When a character's conferral
  fires, the ceremony beat is observable there: witnessed,
  chronicle-recorded, once per threshold, skippable, no fanfare.
- **The ceremony triggers on any conferral.** In-game-earned
  conferrals fire it today; the future external-issuer adapter
  becomes just another trigger. No coupling to the adapter.
- **The first-wage beat exists.** A character's first-ever wage
  posting lands with a small diegetic beat at the employer; one
  authored instance ships at Dave's Bar.
- **Katie's key handover is audited, polished, and digitized**
  against the ceremony rules (witnesses, chronicle receipt,
  once-only, diegetic) — and the dorm key migrates to a
  `dorm-key` credential record per the acquisition slate ("the
  door knows you now").
- **The Shallows infirmary exists** as a permanent town fixture:
  the clergy-kept walk-in clinic on the West Bank, cots, the
  stocked cabinet, the scheduled clergy restock. Deliberately
  unstaffed — the vacancy is true fiction and the medic role is
  the player's. The medic verb suite works end-to-end on any
  patient brought in.
- **The fountain exists in the Quad — scenery, not demo.** Good
  prose, real water (bulk: drinkable, fillable), an honest sound
  `character` for `listen` attribution; it stands with the Quad's
  whimsical statue, not in its place. It carries no
  physics-demonstration duty (decision below).
- **The crossing's lamppost is V4's look-then-analyze prop.** The
  avenue's prose already promises it ("come dusk it will find its
  own light") and the crossing build spec rosters it as
  planned-but-unbuilt Stuff — this build realizes it. A real
  fixture on the world's 120 V standard: `look` gives warm prose;
  `analyze electrical` (shipped rendering, no engine changes)
  gives potential and element resistance in real units, from which
  the viewer derives current and power — Ohm's law and P = V·I,
  the electrician track's actual working knowledge. Lit by night,
  dark by day, on existing scheduling seams. (It lives on the
  Terminus side of the wall because campus light deliberately
  "never matches the hour" — a dusk-faithful lamp belongs under an
  honest sky.)
- **HELD: the rehearsed authoring path** (the V5 runbook) waits
  on the scoped-authoring sandbox build — spec retained in
  content-spec unit 16; not part of this build's acceptance.

## Surface decisions

### Demo-world aging: deferred

The slate's "curated, deliberately aged world-state" is split: the
static content (this build) versus the aging (chronicle depth, title
chains, ledger texture, named regulars, seed/reset design). Aging is
deferred to its own pass. Reasoning: the beats are filmable and
world-improving on their own; the aging pass has real open design
work (honest history generation, reset mechanism) that shouldn't
gate the content. The hybrid model surfaced in ideation — authored
backstory via the claim/prologue channel, deed texture generated by
genuinely running scripted play — is recorded here as the leading
candidate for that pass, not a commitment.

### The aid post is a Terminus clinic, unstaffed, no patient loop (siting refined to the Shallows infirmary — unit 14)

The wishbook sites the aid post at the mining delving, which is
unbuilt. Rather than bolt a camp onto newbie-wilds for filming
convenience, the clinic ships where it permanently belongs — in
Terminus, a town obviously due a clinic. Two consequences accepted
explicitly:

- **No staged patients.** A behavior loop delivering
  mishap-injured NPCs was considered and declined — patient supply
  waits for an organic source (Ferrow's hazards, real players).
  Until then the clinic is a real, stocked, working facility whose
  medic verbs function on whoever is brought in.
- **V2's "months in the role" stage is only partially served** by
  this build — the room and stock exist; the standing rounds loop
  does not. The video-side gap stays open in
  [education-videos.md](../education-videos.md)'s audit.

### The campus slice rides the authored design, not an invented one

An earlier draft invented a gatehouse→courtyard campus from
scratch; it was thrown out when set against the already-authored
campus design (the road network, districts, and EC-derived road
prose in
[campus-map.md](../staging/eternal-university/campus-map.md), the
v1 roster in the eternal-university slate, and the city plan in
[terminus-city.md](../staging/terminus-city.md)). The settled
geometry lives in content-spec unit 1. Two notes of record:

- **The Quad takes the Heart's geometry deliberately.** The campus
  map rejected EC's Heart as a *kill-zone funnel*; the objection
  was the PvP funnel, not the compass-junction shape. The Quad as
  the central four-way junction (with the statue as the monument
  rhyme) is the homage, minus the kill-zone.
- **Opening the campus bends the EU slate's "build it all
  together" principle knowingly.** That principle serves the
  defer-your-choices onboarding model; it holds as long as
  char-gen/onboarding does not yet route players to campus
  services. This build opens the geography and the registrar only;
  the services drop (housing, Health Center, outfitter, the
  first-login journey) remains the EU build's.

### The conferral lands in the registrar's hall, Student Services

Student Services / Admin sits on **Silver Street**, among the
working halls that street was designed to carry (clinic, store,
chapel — EC's own roster) — the user's ruling: buildings live on
roads; the Quad stays a pure hub. An intermediate draft had the
building fronting the green; it was reversed. The ceremony's
threshold opens onto the ringing plates instead, which suits it.
The registrar's hall is the interior; the registrar (persona,
dialogue, disposition per the full NPC-dossier standard) is the
ceremony's voice, and this build's single new NPC carve. Per the
EU slate's own law: the strangeness is a finish, never a
structure — the registrar is a registrar.

### All three threshold beats ship

Conferral (net-new, first priority), first wage (net-new, small),
the key (audit/polish of Katie's existing handover). The wage and
key beats are cheap next to the conferral and complete the moments
lens's named threshold set.

### First-wage venue: Dave's Bar

The beat mechanism is generic — first-ever wage posting for the
character, at the employer, diegetically. The one authored instance
ships at Dave's Bar (existing business seed, existing staff to
voice it, and the bar is the standing integration target). Other
employers can adopt the beat later without new mechanism.

### V4's prop is the crossing's lamppost; the fountain carries no demo

The slate's "fountain with an honest acoustic profile and a
textbook-checkable fact" died on engine facts, verified in source:
the sound walk has **no distance falloff** (`EXIT_TAU = 1.0` —
open exits pass full amplitude; no intra-room distance exists), a
`SoundSourceMixin` emission is **dB + prose character only** (no
frequency), and **no numeric acoustic read surface exists**
(`listen` renders prose without numbers; the `analyze`/`measure`
family has no acoustic member). Making the fountain demonstrable
would have meant a `measure sound` verb plus a toggle-jet
contraption built to justify the prop — engine work hiding in a
content build, in service of our weakest shipped channel.

Decision: **the demonstration moves to the strongest shipped
channel with a real vocation attached.** The crossing's lamppost —
already promised in the avenue's prose and rostered as planned
Stuff in the crossing build spec — rides the world's 120 V
standard (the substation's convention). It stays on the Terminus
side of the wall: campus light deliberately never matches the
hour, so the dusk-faithful lamp belongs under the honest sky. The
electrician track's daily loop is literally "walk the row's
fixtures, `analyze` for wear" — so the beat is the professional's
ordinary glance, not an abstract lab demo: `look` → prose;
`analyze electrical` → potential (120 V) and element resistance in
Ω (shipped rendering); the viewer derives I = V/R and P = V·I and
finds the lamp is a familiar wattage. Values are authored so the
arithmetic lands on a round, reference-checkable figure. No new
verbs, no new physics.

The fountain stays as what a fountain honestly is: a fixture of
the Quad (standing with, not displacing, the whimsical statue —
the Quad's designed landmark) with real prose, real water (bulk —
drinkable, fillable, wets things), and a sound `character` so
`listen` attributes it truthfully. No dB claims beyond that, no
demo duty.

## Content spec

For a content build, the template-level decisions ARE the
requirements: every room, object, and beat below is documented to
authoring depth — class, mixins, field values, prose (or prose
direction + register), the numbers, and the reasoning — before
planning begins. Units are worked one at a time with the user;
un-worked units are listed as pending.

Unit checklist: **1** campus geometry & gates ✓ · **1b** avenue
updates ✓ · **2** Eternal Way (both arms + north-gate stub) ·
**3** the Quad · **4** Silver Street · **5** Limbo Lane + the
Duncan Hall stitch · **6** the fountain · **7** the statue ·
**8** the crossing's lamppost · **9** Student Services / the
registrar's hall + objects · **10** the registrar (NPC dossier,
own session) · **11** the conferral ceremony beat · **12**
first-wage beat at Dave's Bar · **13** key-handover audit ·
**14** clinic room · **15** clinic stock · **16** V5 runbook
**(HELD — waits on the scoped-authoring sandbox build)**.

### Unit 1 — campus geometry & the gates ✓

**Perimeter (from the city plan,
[terminus-city.md](../staging/terminus-city.md)):** the campus
sits at the city's north head, on the ruins, embedded in the
city — the civic wedge at its south, the **Marrow** river down
its west flank, the **Mere** down its east, meeting at the
Confluence at its back. So: **two road gates, two water
frontages.** South: the **main gate**, at the University Avenue
crossing. North: the **landward gate** onto the valley roads
(future content; one honest stub room beyond it). East and west:
river frontages — no road gates in v1; the rivers are present in
the road prose and end Silver Street and the residential side
honestly.

**The gate is the intersection, not a room.** The crossing's
north exit re-points through the existing gate door directly onto
Eternal Way's south arm — the wall's boundary is the door on that
exit. The gatehouse stub (`university-avenue/campus-gate.yaml`)
is **retired**; `campus-gate-door.yaml` survives as the main
gate's door, unlocked.

**The network (from
[campus-map.md](../staging/eternal-university/campus-map.md), EC
names kept):** hub-and-spoke with the **Quad as the Heart** — the
central compass junction, the deliberate homage to EC's Heart
("roads from each point on the compass lead off"; the monument →
our statue), minus the kill-zone. **Eternal Way** is the
south→north spine, main gate → Quad → north gate. **Silver
Street** is the east arm, working halls lining it, running toward
the Mere. **Limbo Lane** is the west arm, toward the Residential
Quarter, wiring the already-built **Duncan Hall** onto campus.
**Buildings live on roads, never on the green** — Student
Services (the registrar's hall within) sits on Silver Street
among the working halls.

**Gate policy: open.** The main gate unlocks; the campus is
public. Honest, zero new mechanism (the enrollment-gated
alternative required an enrollment-credential system hiding in a
content build); the fiction: the University has opened its gates
to the town — Gus's "not yet" has arrived. The **sky-flip**
begins at the wall: crossing the main gate trades Terminus's
ordinary sky for the campus's sourceless, wrongly-bright one.

**Namespace:** campus content lives at
`seeds/domain/eternal/campus/` — its own zone; cross-zone exits
authored explicitly on both sides at every seam (crossing↔Eternal
Way S, Limbo Lane↔Duncan Hall; the crossing↔arrival-gate
precedent). Address locality: `campus/…`. Road materials are real
floors — Adornments composing the material, per the campus map's
floor model (the substation's `flooded-floor` is the shipped
precedent).

**Terminals (sparse-trio rule):** Duncan Hall's lobby terminal
(#2) already exists or is verified in its build; the Quad as
optional terminal #3 — the modern echo of EC's Heart staircase up
to the lounge — is decided in unit 3, not here. No terminal at
the gates.

### Unit 2 — Eternal Way (both arms + the north-gate stub) ✓

**Structure:** two road rooms + one stub, all
`CartesianLocation` in the campus zone. `eternal-way-south`
(0,−1): exits south → the crossing (cross-zone, through the main
gate door) and north → the Quad. `eternal-way-north` (0,1):
exits south → the Quad and north → the north gate.
`valley-road` stub (0,2) beyond the north gate's own door seed
(open): honest dead end, the road climbing toward the domains,
the campus wall at your back, onward travel soft-walled (future
content — the retired campus-gate stub's role, relocated).

**Prose (south arm — the campus map's authored draft, endpoints
adapted to our compass):**

> Eternal Way runs wide and dead-straight from the gate into the
> green — the spine the whole campus hangs off. A pale mist lies
> a few inches above the pavement and refuses to lift; fan at it
> and it only stirs, sluggish, then settles back over whatever
> the road is made of. Nobody has ever gotten a clean look at
> the surface underfoot — the Way keeps its own composition to
> itself, and has, apparently, forever. Every so often something
> you can't see slides across your shins as you wade through it.
> Overhead, the light owns no source it will admit to: simply,
> evenly, *wrongly* bright. Behind you to the south the gate
> stands open on University Avenue's honest daylight; ahead, the
> mist thickens toward the Quad.

North arm: same fabric, endpoints swapped (mist thins toward the
Quad; the landward gate north, "weather that looks real" beyond
it).

**The floor:** an Adornment floor per the campus map's model —
with the map's bonus joke made real: the floor's material
*exists* but is concealment-gated under the mist, so
`analyze`/`look` honestly fail to resolve what the Way is made
of. (Whether an Adornment can host `Concealable` is a planner
verification; the stated fallback is fog-veiled prose without
the mechanical gate.)

**Details:** the mist (the fan-at-it joke), the pavement (the
refusal), the light (sourceless), the wall, gate-views both
ways, and the shins-thing as a **touch-slot** detail ("something
dry and quick crosses your shins and is gone") — per-sense
detail slots are shipped.

### Unit 3 — the Quad ✓

**Structure:** `CartesianLocation` at campus origin (0,0),
address `campus/quad`. **Exactly four exits — the compass
roads.** Buildings are kept off the green entirely (decision,
2026-07-28, revising the campus map's "buildings front it
directly"): every campus building lives on a road; the Quad
holds grass, walkways, the statue, the fountain, and nothing
else. Populates: the fountain (unit 6) and the statue (unit 7).
No resident NPCs (the greeter is the onboarding build's).

**The strange finish — two notes, deliberately no more:**
wrong-green grass and light that never matches the hour (the
map's TBD, committed). The paving stays plain — the roads own
the material weirdness; restraint keeps strangeness a finish.
The light note carries a free honest beat: the campus biome's
ambient never tracks the sun, so **`measure light` at midnight
equals noon** — the fabrication is measurable.

**Prose draft:**

> The Quad opens around you: the campus's heart, a broad green
> crossed by pale walkways, with roads running off at every
> point of the compass. The grass is a green that matches no
> season anyone can name — even at the edges, where grass ought
> to be thinking about dying. The light lies over it all at the
> same even brightness it always keeps, whatever the hour
> happens to be. At the center a stone fountain plays beside the
> statue. The campus keeps its buildings off the green: they
> stand along the roads, faces turned to their own streets, so
> the Quad holds nothing but grass, water, stone, and the wrong
> light. Eastward the plates of Silver Street throw back that
> light in a long streak; south, the mist on Eternal Way hides
> the road home; west, Limbo Lane glows its quiet pink toward
> the dorms.

**Details:** `grass` (the wrong green, close up), `light` (no
source it will admit to; the measure-light invitation),
`walkways` (plain pale stone, deliberately unremarkable), and
`plate` — a bronze survey plate set in a walkway edge noting the
campus stands **on the ruins** (the Sanctuary-below seam as
prose only; the modern echo of the Heart's manhole, no exit).

**No terminal in v1** (decision): the Terminus TPA terminal
empties onto the crossing two rooms south, so campus arrival is
a short walk that earns the sky-flip at the gate; a Quad
terminal would let players skip the build's best threshold.
Duncan's lobby covers the resident shortcut. Revisit if the walk
wears thin.

### Unit 4 — Silver Street ✓

**Structure:** `silver-street.yaml` at (1,0), address
`campus/silver-street`. Exits: west → the Quad; north → Student
Services (the building takes the northeast block; its south face
and door are on this street; interior at (1,1), unit 9). East is
a **soft end at the Mere** — the river present in prose, no quay
room in v1.

**Honest door states:** the street's designed roster (Health
Center, campus store, chapel) exists as tall shut fronts — the
fiction carrying the build-state truthfully: the University has
opened its gates before all its halls are staffed. Only Student
Services stands open. Each shut hall gets its own facade detail
(a future hook, not set-dressing).

**Prose draft** (the campus map's authored text, adapted; the
mirror gains one beat the wrong light earns):

> Silver Street runs east off the Quad, tiled wall to wall in a
> metal polished to a long mirror — silver, or close enough that
> nobody's bothered to argue. Every footfall rings off the
> plates, a clear low note that lifts and gets lost somewhere
> overhead, so the street is never quite silent, even empty;
> look down and the campus looks back at you, upside down in the
> wrong light. The working halls line it — the Health Center,
> the campus store, a chapel — shut for now behind their tall
> fronts, waiting on staff the University hasn't hired yet. Only
> Student Services keeps its doors open, brass-plated and lit,
> on the north side. East, past the last hall, the street runs
> out toward water: the Mere, glinting where the plates end.

**The floor:** the plates as the Adornment floor — real polished
metal as the material, so the signature is modeled ground, not
paint. The per-footstep *ring* stays prose in v1 (event-sound
plumbing is not this build's; noted as a future
narration-adapter candidate).

**Details:** `plates` (the reflection underfoot), `ring` (a
hearing-slot detail — the note that never quite dies),
`health-center` / `store` / `chapel` (shut facades),
`services` (the open brass-plated door), `mere` (the east water
view).

### Unit 5 — Limbo Lane + the Duncan Hall stitch ✓

**Structure:** `limbo-lane.yaml` at (−1,0), address
`campus/limbo-lane`. Exits: east → the Quad; **north → Duncan
Hall's steps** (the hall fronts the lane from the north side,
mirroring Student Services on Silver Street — buildings on
roads, consistently; cross-zone seam authored on both sides,
Duncan Hall keeping its own zone/Warren); west is a **soft end
toward the Marrow** — the residential quarter's growth
direction, honestly unfinished.

**Prose draft** (the campus map's authored text, endpoints
adapted):

> An odd pink material paves Limbo Lane — soft, rubbery, faintly
> aglow — and it springs underfoot: each step sinks the road and
> the road pushes back, so you bounce a little whether you mean
> to or not. It's the quiet way out of the core. Silver Street's
> ringing falls away behind you to the east, and the lane
> carries you, gently springing, past the limestone face of
> Duncan Hall on its north side. West, the pink runs on toward
> the Marrow and the rest of a residential quarter the
> University hasn't built yet.

**The floor:** the pink as the Adornment floor — real material,
with the spring as a **touch-slot** detail ("press it and it
presses back, patient, like it's done this before"). No bounce
*mechanics* in v1 — the material is modeled, the behavior stays
prose, stated honestly.

**Duncan Hall edits (the stitch):**

- The steps gain **south → Limbo Lane**; the lane's north exit
  is the reciprocal.
- The steps' stale second paragraph ("the quad opens up,
  sun-bleached…") rewrites to the true view: the pink lane at
  the foot of the steps, the core's ringing east, the wrong
  light overhead.
- **The steps join the campus light regime** — outdoors inside
  the wall now, so its biome swaps to the campus's
  sourceless-light one. Interiors unaffected.

**Details** (all compliant with the durable-facts vista rule):
`paving` (vision + the touch slot), `duncan` (the limestone face
and its geranium planter, seen from the road), `marrow` (the
west water view), `glow` (the pink's faint light — prose only;
under the campus's wrong light we don't stack a real light
source on it).

### Unit 6 — the fountain ✓

**Template:** `campus/fountain.yaml`, content class
`/domain/eternal/campus/Fountain` (path mirrors template),
composing: immovable fixture + `SoundSource`
(`emittedAmplitude: 55` dB — the reference-honest
garden-fountain figure; `character: "falling water"` — `listen`
attributes truthfully) + Bulkable water (~400 L: `drink`
hydrates through metabolism for real, `fill` charges a flask; a
municipal-refill behavior is a planner nicety, not a
requirement) + real stone material (`analyze response` reads
honest rock). **Not Named** — generic:
`shortDescription: a stone fountain`.

**Prose:**

> A round stone fountain, its basin broad enough to sit on, its
> single jet rising about a man's height and folding back into
> the water with a steady, unhurried plash. The stone is worn
> glassy at the rim where hands and weather have had years at
> it. The water is real — cold, clear, faintly mineral — which
> somehow makes the light lying on it look wronger.

**Details (per-sense):** `water` — vision (clear to the basin
floor), touch (genuinely cold), taste (clean, faintly mineral —
true: the drink is modeled); `basin` (the glassy rim, the
sitting invitation); `jet` (the rise and fall, the one steady
thing under the wrong light). Sitting on the rim stays prose in
v1 (no `Postured` surface — the few-objects rule holds; one-line
mixin addition later if it matters).

### Unit 7 — the statue ✓

**The design: a salvaged artifact, not a mascot.** (A whimsical
"Bob" anti-monument was proposed and rejected — the Quad's
iconography is a lore venue, and refusal-of-lore wasted it.) The
statue was raised whole from the core excavations at the
University's founding — the one piece of the dead city below
standing in daylight. Human or close to it, robed, one arm
lifted; **the hand broken away at the wrist, and with it
whatever it held**. The ambiguity is *evidence-shaped*, not a
refusal: who it was, what it held, and what the plinth says are
all answerable someday by disciplines already designed — the
plinth carries a single line in the **lost Eternal-age script**
(a standing decipherment target, authored now, method later —
the Ferrow vertical's campus sibling), and the missing object is
an ordered-forum-shaped question (a lamp, a tool, a coin, a
blade — each implying a different Eternal City).
Ratification-invariant by construction (fixed ruins-history
only). The bible's relic discipline holds: a dry footnote, not a
fanfare.

**Template:** `campus/statue.yaml`, immovable fixture, **not
Named** — its name is lost, which is the point;
`shortDescription: an ancient statue`. Material: real bronze of
an archaic alloy nobody pours anymore — `analyze` reads honest,
old metal.

**Prose:**

> A statue older than the University stands at the center of the
> green, raised whole from the excavations the campus is built
> over. The figure is human, or close to it — robed, head bare,
> one arm lifted; the hand is broken away at the wrist, and with
> it whatever it held. Rain, and longer ago something worse, has
> worn the face to a suggestion. Around the plinth runs a single
> line of characters in the old script of the city below, cut
> deeper than the weather could reach — still crisp, still
> unread. A brass plate at the base, in the University's own

### What enrollment means (recorded 2026-07-28)

**The world is the curriculum; the campus is the recognizer.**
Learning happens in the world (the advancement doctrine, the
wishbook's loops — practice wearing a livelihood); the University
witnesses and recognizes it. The intended player arc is
**vocational, fast** — days to a first job, not semesters — you
exit the campus residentially and daily-loop-wise quickly, and
never exit its recognition. Being **on the roll is a relationship,
not a phase**:

1. **The dorm claim** — the first-home on-ramp (already real).
2. **The seam binding** — enrollment authorizes the University to
   *receive your evidence*: binding an external platform account
   to the character so coursework lands as claims, verified
   assessment as deeds, and conferrals fire. The registrar is the
   in-fiction anchor of the future issuer adapter; the binding's
   fiction is an act of enrollment, done at her counter.
3. **Campus services access** — the deferred-choices desk (EU
   build).
4. **Standing** — to petition conferrals and be ceremonied.

Enrollment is indefinite and weightless; perpetual students are
fine and honest. On educational content: the licensed vertical is
the curriculum plan (the academic hall stays reserved as its
future in-world surface); the authored floor is vocational
schools standing in the world (the Practicum, the gym,
newbie-wilds' curriculum, player-taught seminars via the command
discipline) — academic content is precisely what licensing is
for.

### The University's role: LinkedIn with a college face (recorded 2026-07-28)

**Guilds own vocational engagement** (the guild slate's chartered
institutions — where a field is practiced, taught, employed). The
University deliberately is **not a guild** and offers what a guild
structurally can't:

1. **The commons before commitment** — the vestibule where every
   field is visible, anything can be tried, and no charter binds;
   its structural job is handing players onward well-matched. The
   guild choice is the real choice; the University is where you
   stand while making it.
2. **The neutral record between guilds** — transcript and
   certificates are University-kept precisely so they're *not*
   guild-kept: portable across guilds, employers, and disputes; no
   guild holds your standing hostage. The layer that makes guild
   claims comparable.
3. **Navigation** — in a world with no levels and no quest log,
   "what should I do next" is the scarcest good. The **advisor
   read** is the registrar's first functional service: ask about
   any discipline and she reads your real transcript against it
   (derive-on-read, no new state) and says what the next rung
   wants. This is also V1's "return" beat.

**Named honestly (user, 2026-07-28): this is the world's diegetic
professional-identity registry — LinkedIn with a college face.**
Profile (the Rolls + transcript), verified credentials (the
certificates, provenance-graded), the recruiter surface (the
directory + guild brokerage), career navigation (the advisor
read). The differentiator over the real thing: LinkedIn is a
*claims* registry — self-reported, endorsement-inflated; this is a
**deeds registry** — every line derived from evidence, unfakeable
by construction. The resume that can't lie is a space the platform
could genuinely compete in (the gamification-mirror /
boundless-reach thesis in work clothes; a strategy-doc note, not a
video claim).

**Civic registry, never a business (the three nevers):** never
priced (enrollment, certification, ceremony all free — charging
would be the Mara shape); never scarce or discretionary (minted
for anyone whose transcript carries it — the registrar reads, she
doesn't decide); never a gate we author (no core verb, door, or
content requires the paper — bands stay directly readable; the
certificate's value emerges from *other parties'* choice to trust
it: certified-only contract clauses, employer preferences,
player-run requirements). The CB and the parcel registry are the
in-world precedents: records of enormous economic value, sold by
no one. The Rolls are the title registry of competence.

**The choice surface — deferred to the guild reconciliation:**
declaring a course of study (mapping onto guild-chartered fields,
shaping brokered introductions, focusing the advisor read) is the
registration-shaped decision the University owes the player. Its
field vocabulary must reconcile with the parallel guild-slate
session; designed on record here, built at that seam, and the hall
is shaped expecting it.

**The roll lifecycle (designed on record; mechanisms mostly
deferred).** The Rolls never erase — entries close, they never
vanish (the chronicle's append-only culture, in ink):

- **Enrollment** — char-gen's `enroll` commit *is* matriculation;
  the open roll renders the enrolled honestly.
- **Graduation** — a **petitioned threshold conferral**, measured
  never granted: the player petitions; assessment is
  derive-on-read over the real transcript (the bar — bands,
  breadth, effectively a declared course of study — is the
  advancement/EU integration's design, not this build's). The
  ceremony is the conferral ceremony's senior sibling, same
  venue, same Rolls; the entry moves to the **alumni register**.
  Alumni keep playing. Residence note carried forward:
  graduation *nudges* the dorm-to-apartment ladder (a term's-end
  grace), never a cliff.
- **Withdrawal** — voluntary; the entry closes with a line.
- **Passage** — when the mortal-vessel design's true death
  lands, the passed move to the **memorial page**; the passage
  event writes the line.
- **Expulsion** — exists as fiction; mechanism deferred until
  courts/governance can carry it honestly. Nothing builds on it.

This build ships the Rolls' true structure (open roll, alumni
register, memorial page) rendered from real registries where
they exist and honestly thin where they don't; petition,
withdrawal, memorial automation, and expulsion are all deferred.

## Constraints

- **Ceremony doctrine** ([lenses/moments.md](../lenses/moments.md)):
  once per threshold per character, diegetic, skippable, no fanfare
  sting. Each beat must have witnesses possible and a chronicle
  receipt — a moment that leaves no trace can't be retold.
- **No new event bus.** The conferral/wage triggers are
  single-consumer seams: direct witness hooks or method calls per
  the standing feedback rule (no EventApi unless genuinely global).
- **One NPC carve.** The registrar is the only new NPC; NPC
  dossiers are expensive and done one at a time. The clinic is
  unstaffed by design; the wage beat uses Dave's existing staff.
- **Room spec three tiers** (standing content rule): atmosphere
  prose carries the bulk; few real interactive objects; NPCs listed
  explicitly. Room-by-room design lands one beat at a time.
- **Content placement rules** (CLAUDE.md): seeds under
  `seeds/domain/…` in the owning locality's namespace; any
  domain-local verbs follow the `domain/<sphere>/<locality>/cmd`
  pattern; nothing content-specific in the core trees.
- **Honesty discipline**: the fountain's emission is real signal in
  real units through the senses substrate; the ceremony beats state
  only what the ledgers actually record; no manufactured fanfare.
- **Vista details describe durable facts only** — water, stone,
  the fixed face of a building; never dynamic state (occupants,
  weather-dependent looks, time-dependent state), which rots
  silently. The crossing's clock tower remains the sanctioned
  bespoke live-read. The real abstraction is captured in
  [slates/tails/distance-perception-slate.md](../slates/tails/distance-perception-slate.md);
  this build does not attempt it.
- **Beat idempotence is a hard requirement**: "first wage" and
  "conferral ceremony held" must survive restarts/re-login without
  re-firing (the derive-or-record mechanism is the planner's
  choice; the observable rule is once-ever per character per
  threshold).
