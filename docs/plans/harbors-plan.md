## Plan-level decisions

**P1 — One model: `FloorplanWarren` lifted to the kernel; a ship owns a keyed `ShipWarren`.** `C/residence/src/idea/HoldingWarren.ts`'s floorplan mint, keyed rooms, `wake`/`captureAll`/`teardown`/`revert`, `layOut`, the shell clock, bands, `restoreShell` and `upkeepTerm` move to `S/lib/location/FloorplanWarren.ts` (abstract `Persistable(WarrenMember(Container(RoomWarren)))`). `HoldingWarren extends FloorplanWarren` keeps only what is residential: `addressBase`, the parcel keyway (`refreshKeyway`/`keyway`), `admitPlot`, `reconcile → institution`, `conditionCause`'s house prose becomes a `@hook upkeepWords()` the ship overrides. `Warren` is a class so a `Ship` cannot be one (the slate's open 1); the ship **owns** one: kernel `S/platform/idea/ShipWarren.ts extends FloorplanWarren` (instanceable, rows = ship kinds' floorplans), one instance per ship keyed by her durable key, `reconcile()` → the ship's dormancy. A Ship row names its floorplan forward (`warren: /stuff/idea/ShipWarren/<kind>`); an authored singleton (the *Hesper*) names none and her warren **adopts** the resident rooms under her `extent` as members (platform row `/platform/idea/ShipWarren/authored`, empty floorplan). `Ship.liveMembers()` reads the warren's members, never `Template.findDescendants`. Reasoning: holding.md's last section names ships as the second consumer and says the lift waits on them; the owner's rule *"whatever the rule, it works the same way as residences"* makes the clock part of the lift rather than a rhyme. ⚠ The rate key stays `residence.weather.daysToWorn` (the requirements: *"the same rate — the residence setting"*), read by the kernel base with the same 45 fallback; renaming it is a later sweep (§ Risks).

**P2 — A ship kind is `(scope = kind row, key = serial)`; her rooms `(room row, <serial>/<leaf>)`.** Kind rows `/stuff/idea/Ship/<kind>` (class `/platform/idea/Ship`, `warren:`, `hull {draftM, beamM, lengthM, depthM}` — `depthM` is new, the height of her hull from keel to deck, what she can take aboard before she goes down — `house: /stuff/idea/Business/ship-company`, heights, speed). Buying one clones the kind and `PersistableApi.restoreOrSeed(ship, serial)` with `serial = SecurityApi.uuid()` minted by the sale and **recorded on the register entry** (so the mint is `referenced`, not a probe). Her durable handle is `<kind row>#<serial>` (`getDurableHandle()`), which is what every record names her by. Her company is minted `asIdentityPath: /stuff/idea/Business/ship-company/<serial>` (the `DrillingOutfit` precedent; justified `referenced` by the register entry's `house`). No ship is ever stamped with an identity path: lineage stays the kind row (D17).

**P3 — Membership and "which craft am I aboard" go through the warren, with a handle.** `WarrenMember.getWarren()` on any ship room → `ShipWarren.ship()`; kernel readers that take a path (`ExpanseApi.craftAt`, `NavigationApi.structureAt`, `AshoreExit.warm`, `SeaWarren.shipOf`, `CourseController.craftOf`, `HailController.craftOf`) accept a **durable handle** (`row#key`): `ExpanseApi.craftAt(pathOrHandle)` resolves a handle to the live keyed room by `StuffApi.findByDurableHandle` (new, over the registry's keyed hosts) and asks its warren by shape (`shipOf` on a `ShipWarren`), falling back to the structure attachments for a row path. ## Risks & opens

1. **Settled vs sunk at a berth depends on the water's depth at the quay.** The Kestrel's margin depth at km 0.4–0.9 is derived by `RiverLine` (the channel's normal depth, margins shallower) and may exceed a smack's `hull.depthM` (1.8 m), in which case a neglected ship at the Town Quay would SINK, contradicting the requirements' *"settled onto the shallow harbor bottom"*. P20 gives a harbor quay an authored `berthDepthM` (a mud berth — the harbor's own fact until a dredger exists) that the settle/sink choice reads first. The build should read the derived depth at the quays' cites in A1 and author `berthDepthM` on the Town and Fish Quays at or under the smack's depth. **Decided here, flagged for the user: the harbor authors its berths' depths.**
2. **The clock's rate key.** The kernel base reads `residence.weather.daysToWorn` because the requirements say *the residence setting*. A kernel class naming a pack's key is a smell; the honest fix is a neutral key (`structure.weather.daysToWorn`) seeded by the platform with the residence row retired — a one-line rename sweep after this build, recorded in the ship subsystem doc.
3. **`FacedMixin` on `Boat`.** Faces.md composes it on `Good`; `Boat` is a `Vessel`. If the mixin's host type refuses, B5 keeps a `hullCoat` state on `Boat` read the same way (`holdsWater`) and the seam to `Faced` is recorded. Check at B5's first commit, not later.
4. **The settling window counts ACTS through `EventApi`.** If the command-dispatched event does not carry the giver (to be checked at B5 against `api/event.ts`), the window falls back to real seconds only and the requirements' *"sized in acts"* is recorded as unmet with the reason — raise it, do not quietly accept the backstop.
5. **`subjectTo` for a ship room.** `AddressApi.resolveFor(scope)` walks containment to a zone; a ship's zone (the kind's `CartesianZone`) has no address, so the chain is `[]` today — P16's branch runs only when the chain is empty, which is also true of an unaddressed cellar. The branch must ask *is the scope's root a ship* first, never *is the chain empty*.
6. **A sleeping minted ship at a public landing** is re-wired only through the register's `lies` (`lyingAt`); an unregistered (stateless) minted ship cannot exist this build (every yard sale registers), and authored ships load with their frame. Documented, not solved.
7. **`Frame.hailables` on the chart** finds the Terminus office through the bar node's `river`; a sea harbor behind a node is found through the node's destination. A harbor on a lake with neither is not reachable by `hail` this build.
