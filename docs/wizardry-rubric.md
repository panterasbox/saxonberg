# The wizardry rubric — what the craft measures, and how

> **Status: the Discipline's scored syllabus.** Written 2026-10-04,
> because [wizardry-slate](./slates/builds/wizardry-slate.md) § 10.1 said
> to write it first: *"it costs nothing, it is dispositive, and everything
> else is downstream."* ⭐⭐⭐ **It is dispositive in both directions — if
> a band cannot be defended from this document, the Discipline is not
> real.**
>
> **Nothing here is new machinery.** It is an
> [advancement.md](./subsystems/advancement.md) Discipline tree plus an
> `ActSignature` decomposition, obeying
> [measurement.md](./measurement.md)'s rules, with its criteria taken from
> [eotl-craft.md](./eotl-craft.md)'s measured techniques and failure
> modes. ⚠ The one thing it may never become is a second band model.

---

## 0. ⭐⭐⭐ The three rules that wrote this document

Everything below is downstream of three constraints that already exist,
and the rubric is mostly what is *left* once they are obeyed.

**1. The engine may read a measurement; it must never read a valuation.**
*(`measurement.md`)* The test: a **single-domain derived band is a
measurement; a cross-domain weighted composite is a valuation.**

> ⛔ **So there is no "wizardry band."** A single number combining prose,
> composition, siting and economics is precisely the banned object. ⭐
> Wizardry is a **tree of single-domain Disciplines**, and this follows
> independently from `advancement.md`'s *prefer specializations* rule —
> *specialize when the sim already tells the two practices apart.* It
> does: different instrument, different failure mode, different refusal.

**2. Difficulty is a world-measurement, not a tag.** *(`advancement.md`)*
*"You can't farm a hard attempt by relabeling."* So nothing in § 3 is
declared by the author — every difficulty is read off the artifact or the
world.

**3. Derive, don't track.** *(A3, amendable by nobody.)* Competence is a
BKT fold over Transcript rows, re-derived on every read.

> ⚠⚠ **Therefore the rubric does NOT assign bands, and this is the thing
> most likely to be got wrong by whoever builds it.** A rubric that says
> *"to reach expert you must…"* is a second band model competing with the
> estimator, and it violates A3. **The rubric's entire job is to define
> what counts as an ATTEMPT, how HARD it was, and whether it SUCCEEDED.**
> The band falls out. § 5's band descriptions are *readings*, never
> requirements.

## 1. The Discipline tree

`wizardry` is a **root nobody is banded in** — the `services` pattern, a
`specializes` parent that exists so the leaves have somewhere to hang.
Seven leaves, each single-domain, each with its own instrument and its own
characteristic failure:

| key | what it measures | instrument | the failure it is named against |
|---|---|---|---|
| `prose-craft` | writing what a thing is | the editor | ⭐⭐⭐ **hollowness** — 41% of EotL rooms have anything to examine; Culhaven is 560 rooms at 8% |
| `composition` | building a kind out of the vocabulary | the Studio | **a class per noun** — EotL had 19,703 room files because the file *was* the object |
| `siting` | where a thing goes and what it connects to | coords, exits, zones | **the connective tissue rots first** — the graph between content had no owner and no artifact |
| `provisioning` | making a venue actually function | the archetype | ⚠⚠ **the checklist city** — mean pairwise institution overlap 0.39, derived by looking at each other |
| `characterization` | a person who behaves | dispositions, brains, dialogue | **2,290 talk, 214 can be asked** — speech without anything behind it |
| `taxonomy` | naming a kind so others can find it | the signature, the catalogue | synonymy sprawl, and the name nobody reused |
| `scoping` | knowing what a thing will cost before building it | the declaration | ⚠⚠ **the Africa cabal** — sixteen labelled institutions, one room built |

⚑ **ISCED-F anchors are open.** The field has real homes (media
production, fine arts, software development, earth sciences) but I will
not assert codes I have not checked, and `advancement.md` is explicit that
the anchor answers *"is this a field of study at all?"* and never gates
the split.

### ⭐⭐⭐ One act, several sub-checks — this is already the engine's model

`ActSignature` is *"the authored decomposition of one messy in-world act
into per-Discipline sub-checks."* Building a venue is **one act** that
decomposes into `prose-craft` + `siting` + `provisioning` +
`characterization`, each recorded as its own Transcript row sharing a
timestamp. The seven leaves are therefore **not** the seven kinds of
content from
[wizardry-curriculum-slate](./slates/builds/wizardry-curriculum-slate.md)
§ 2 — the kinds are what you are making, the leaves are what it exercises,
and the signature is the mapping between them.

## 2. ⭐⭐⭐ The governing instrument: every noun is a promise

The headline, and the thing the rest of the document is an elaboration of:

> **Every noun you put in a description is a promise. Difficulty is how
> many you made. Outcome is how many you kept.**

It comes straight out of the corpus's single strongest finding — *in every
case the unfinished room is the one with an empty or absent detail map;
it is the only reliable completeness signal in 45,000 files* — and it has
every property rule 2 demands:

- ⭐ **It cannot be farmed by relabeling**, because the author sets their
  own difficulty by writing an ambitious description, and **raising it
  raises the bar.** Writing more nouns makes the check *harder to pass*,
  not easier. There is no other difficulty dial in the design with that
  shape.
- ⭐ **Volume is self-defeating.** 500 bare rooms are 500 *trivial*
  checks, and BKT's difficulty-modulated observation means a trivial
  success barely moves the estimate. **The leveling mill is forbidden by
  the math, not by a rule.**
- ⭐ **It is the ZPD curve for free.** The transit rate peaks at hard, so
  the room that teaches you most is the ambitious one you only just
  finished.

⚠ **And the instrument is noisy.** Extracting candidate nouns from prose
is approximate, and it will mis-promise. ⭐⭐ **That is survivable
precisely because of how it is used**: `measurement.md`'s Mara/Aletheia
property — *the feed hides the measurement; the mirror shows you*. It is
**honest enough to show you and not honest enough to rank you**, which is
why § 6 forbids it from ever becoming a gate or a published list.

### ⭐ Scored against our own best exemplar, which it fails

`hearts-delight`'s millsite is the finest location row in the tree, and
its own comment teaches two siting errors and von Thünen. Its long
description promises **fourteen** examinable nouns: *shoulder of rock ·
weir · race · bank · water · wheel · ledge · mill · slope · stones ·
shaft · bin · stairs · flour.*

Counted properly — ⚠ **and the counting is where this got away from me
once already** — its three `details` keys carry keyword *arrays*, so the
`weir` entry alone answers three of those nouns (`race`, `ledge`, `weir`)
and `lamps` answers none of them, being the lighting system's rather than
the prose's:

> **Fourteen promised. Four answered by a detail** (weir · race · ledge ·
> wheel), **one by the room's own name** (mill), **nine unanswered** —
> *shoulder of rock, bank, water, slope, stones, shaft, bin, stairs,
> flour.*
>
> **Four of fourteen is 29%**, against a corpus mean of 41% and
> Gnomelands' 89%. ⭐⭐⭐ **Our best location row is below the ancestor's
> average**, and the nine unanswered nouns are nine readers doing exactly
> what the prose invited and finding nothing. The mirror's sentence is not
> *"this is bad"* — it is **"you promised fourteen things and answered
> four."**

⭐ **And the miscount is the first implementation requirement.** My own
first pass scored this row at 2/14 by counting detail *keys* instead of
their keyword arrays. **The extractor must resolve the whole alias array,
and the room's own `keywords` too** — otherwise the instrument punishes
exactly the authors who did the generous thing and gave one good detail
six names.

## 3. Difficulty, per leaf — all seven read off the world

| leaf | what makes an attempt hard | trivial ⟶ formidable |
|---|---|---|
| `prose-craft` | ⭐ nouns promised; **and position in a series** — room 1 of 1 is easy, room 72 of 144 *still holding the refrain* is not (144/144 carried *"a blanket of heat"*) | 1–2 nouns, greenfield ⟶ 12+ nouns inside an established voice |
| `composition` | the **structural novelty of the signature** against the blueprint index | the 500th `Tool` row ⟶ an unseen signature ⟶ one with a member-name conflict, where order is semantic |
| `siting` | **how many constraints had to hold at once** — zone fields, reciprocal exits, an existing network, a physical argument | a room off a corridor ⟶ a mill that must be at the fall, near the grain, on the lamp rota |
| `provisioning` | **archetype slots satisfied without the default row.** Taking the default is easy; meeting the need with something of your own is not | `materialize()` and ship ⟶ a venue whose every slot is its own |
| `characterization` | **how many systems the person is wired into** — a seat, real hours, dispositions, a brain, something to be asked about | a name and a description ⟶ Sennet, whose idle animation is her economics |
| `taxonomy` | **how contested the name was** — a leaf nobody else is near, or a collision in the commons | a private kind ⟶ a name argued through the `/stuff` committee |
| `scoping` | ⭐ **the size of what you declared.** Declaring big is harder, not easier — the same anti-gaming shape as § 2 | one room ⟶ `timeguess`'s *"a wee bit over 9 months"* |

⚠ **`taxonomy` and `scoping` resolve slowly** — months, sometimes. That is
honest (so does a real reputation) but it means they will be *thin*
Disciplines for a long time, and a thin transcript must read `untrained`
rather than being padded with proxies.

## 4. Outcome — three sources, all observed, none self-reported

1. ⭐ **The build.** Does it boot, does `lint:family` pass, does the suite
   stay green. Binary, free, and **maintained correct for unrelated
   reasons** — the property that makes this Discipline cheap to make
   rigorous where every other one is expensive.
2. **The artifact's own properties.** Promises kept; archetype
   satisfaction; exits that reciprocate; a person who answers an `ask`.
3. ⭐⭐ **Peer reuse.** Somebody `extends:` your row, names your class, or
   lists your pack in `requires:`. **The mint ledger already tracks who
   instantiated whose.**

### ⛔⛔ What outcome may never read — and the answer to the two-judges problem

[wizardry-slate](./slates/builds/wizardry-slate.md) § 10.2 asked how the
band and standing avoid collapsing into one number with two names. The
answer is a clean population split, and it is forced rather than chosen:

> ⭐⭐⭐ **Standing reads CONSUMPTION. Wizardry reads PRODUCTION.**
>
> The consumer says *"I liked it."* The peer says *"I depended on it."*
> Box office and citation are different measurements of different things,
> taken from different people.

So the rubric **never reads** player ratings, use counters, engagement,
renown, or any feedback-mechanism signal. Those are standing's inputs, and
`feedback-slate`'s whole design is that **votes allocate resources** —
they were never a quality verdict and must not become one here. ⛔ Reading
them would merge the two judges, violate the valuation ban at one remove,
and import the payola problem the feedback design spent two firewalls
keeping out.

⚠ **The honest residue:** reuse is not free of social dynamics either —
authors build on each other for reasons including deference. It is still
categorically better than a rating, because **it costs the rater
something**: you only `extends:` a row you are prepared to depend on.

## 5. The bands — descriptions, not requirements

⚠⚠ **Read these as what a transcript LOOKS like, never as what you must
DO.** The estimator assigns the band;
`untrained · novice · competent · proficient · expert` is
`CompetenceBand`'s closed vocabulary and the only surface — `theta` never
crosses the Api boundary.

| band | what the evidence looks like, in any leaf |
|---|---|
| `untrained` | no evidence, or only trivial checks |
| `novice` | easy attempts succeed; the characteristic failure of the leaf is still present in most work |
| `competent` | hard attempts succeed more often than not; the characteristic failure appears only under pressure (the unevenness case — ⚠ *the same author, the same week*, wrote the tanned-leather box seats and *"This is a standard room"*) |
| `proficient` | formidable attempts succeed; the work is reused |
| `expert` | formidable attempts succeed inside constraints the author did not set — somebody else's voice, somebody else's zone, somebody else's deadline |

⭐ **The `expert` line is the one worth defending**, and it is where
`eotl-craft`'s two incomparable 89%-and-144-room authors land in the same
band by different routes: *Malifax made 166 rooms deep; this author made
144 rooms coherent. Both work. **An author should know which one they are
paying for.*** Knowing which you are paying for is the expert behaviour;
neither depth nor coherence is the criterion on its own.

## 6. The firewalls

1. ⛔⛔ **No composite.** The seven are read separately, forever. Nothing
   sums them. *(Rule 1; not amendable here.)*
2. ⛔⛔ **No leaderboard of ours.** `measurement.md`: *we do not ban
   leaderboards, we ban ours.* A guild's masters roll, a commissioning
   house's supplier list, a paper's annual rankings — all legitimate, all
   theirs, all arguable. ⭐ The band is **disclosed by the holder, never
   published by the system.**
3. ⛔ **The band confers nothing but eligibility for the apex.** The
   moment it unlocks a verb, an extent or a vote it is a second franchise
   competing with standing. *(`wizardry-slate` § 7.2.)*
4. ⛔ **Never paid in mana.** The Discipline is named in the fiction and
   funded out of it; the four scarcities stay uncostumed metaresources.
   ⚠ A classroom is where this breaks, so **thaumology and wizardry are
   separate departments.**
5. ⭐ **Seeded is kept separate from derived.** `seededBandFor` already
   exists — what an author *wrote down* about themselves never merges with
   what the evidence says, and the assert-vs-derive check is the point.
6. ⛔ **The mirror is never a gate.** § 2's instrument is noisy by
   construction. It may show you your own number; it may not decide
   anything, rank anyone, or appear in a feed.

## 7. What this deliberately does not measure

| not measured | why |
|---|---|
| **volume** — rows, lines, commits, edits | *"how many times someone made a change to the content database is useless as a metric"* — and BKT already makes grinding inert |
| **speed** | nothing in the corpus suggests it correlates with anything; `timeguess`'s author took nine months and was honest about it |
| **popularity** | § 4 — that is standing's |
| **code** | there is no `mechanism` leaf. ⭐ TypeScript is the apex licence's domain, and measuring it before the licence is granted is circular. What a non-coder can demonstrate instead is **that their work did not need code** — lens 2's test, which is `composition`'s criterion already |
| **compliance with the lenses as a checklist** | ⚠ the lenses are a **scorecard, not a gate**, by their own standing rule. They belong to the crit, where a human argues; they are not a sub-check |

## 8. The apex, and the governance a band judging a person owes

⭐ **Eligibility is a conjunction of single-domain thresholds, never a
weighted sum** — the shape `employment.md` already ships, where hiring
reads `{ gigs, discipline, band }` and `Position.fromData` **throws** on
anything else. A conjunction asks domain questions; a sum asks about a
person.

And the band is still **necessary, not sufficient**: `isArchwizard` is the
conferral axis, so **measured competence makes you eligible and an
admitting body admits you.** ⚠ The admitting body must not be the
institution that taught and graded you — the university grades, an office
admits.

Lens 7 requires three things of anything that judges a person:

- **The criterion** — this document.
- **The appeal** — `chronicle`'s **deed vs claim**. A disputed sub-check
  is answerable by a counter-record; ⚠ and A2 means nothing is edited
  away, only added to.
- **The entrenchment tier** — ⭐ **the rubric is Tier C**, policy,
  amendable by the polity. ⛔ **The firewalls in § 6 are not**: 6.1 and
  6.2 restate `measurement.md`, 6.3 and 6.4 restate the Discipline's own
  two hard lines, and 6.5 restates A3. Amending the criteria is ordinary
  politics; amending the firewalls is not on the table.

## 9. Worked: the millsite as one act

| sub-check | difficulty | outcome | why |
|---|---|---|---|
| `prose-craft` | **hard** — fourteen nouns promised inside an established valley voice | ⚠ **fail** | four answered, nine unanswered (§ 2) |
| `siting` | **formidable** — the fall, the grain, the lamp rota, a reciprocal exit | ⭐ **pass** | `ρ·g·Δh·Q·η`, and the second siting error avoided for a different reason |
| `provisioning` | **hard** — a Business, a seat, real hours, a toll that is the actual income | **pass** | and the `[0, 24]` roster was corrected to `[5, 19]`, which is the failure being fixed |
| `characterization` | **hard** — species, dispositions, two brains, an idle pool keyed to her economics | **pass** | the toll bin levelled *in full view* |
| `composition` | **trivial** | **pass** | ⭐ she named existing classes and authored no new kind |

⭐⭐⭐ **Read the last row carefully, because it is the rubric defending
itself.** Doing the easy thing when the easy thing is right earns a
*trivial* check, and a trivial success barely moves the estimate. **You do
not get credit for composing when you should not have composed** — and
you are not penalised for it either. The author who reaches for a new
class to look clever gets a *hard* check they will probably fail.

And the row that fails is the one we would have defended loudest. ⭐ That
is the instrument working: the millsite is excellent at four things and
unfinished at the fifth, and **the mirror says exactly that, per room,
which is the only grain at which the unevenness finding is actionable.**

## 10. Open

1. ⚑ **The noun extractor.** Candidate-noun detection from prose is the
   one genuinely new mechanism here, and § 2 already records the first way
   to get it wrong (count keys, not keyword arrays). ⭐ Lean: ship it as a
   **report first** — run it over the whole content tree, publish the
   distribution, and only wire it to a Transcript row once the numbers
   look like the corpus's. ⚠ `eotl-census.md`'s own instrument note
   applies verbatim: a first pass measured `add_item` at 158 files and
   would have concluded EotL's rooms were bare; the real idiom was
   `descs` at 8,450. **Validate the instrument first.**
2. ⚠ **Does a detail answered by a *parent* row count?** `extends:`
   resolves at read, so an inherited detail map is a real answer — but it
   is also how an author could inherit their way to a high score.
3. ⚠ **Reuse latency.** `taxonomy` and `scoping` resolve in months.
   Acceptable, or do they need an interim signal that is not a proxy?
4. **Who authors the seven Discipline rows**, and do they ship in the
   platform pack or a `wizardry` pack of their own? The latter is more
   honest — ⭐ the trade should ship like any other.
5. ⚑ **ISCED-F codes** (§ 1).
6. ⚠ **Does the course's instrument and the Discipline's have to be the
   same number?** § 2 is used by both. If they are the same, the firewall
   between *what the craft says* and *what the polity thinks* runs through
   one noisy extractor — which argues for the course keeping a **private**
   mirror that writes no Transcript row at all.
