## Format update (2026-07-15): separate videos, not one

Supersedes [constitution-video.md § Format](./constitution-video.md) ("one video,
chapter stops"): **each chapter is its own YouTube video in a playlist.** So
**length is relaxed** — chapters no longer compress to fit a single runtime; each
can breathe and stand a little more on its own (a bit of self-contained framing
per video). The playlist carries continuity; the doubt-cascade hand-forwards still
chain across videos.

## The reframe: trailer → explainer

The old video was a **trailer** — "omission is the conversion engine," withhold
depth to make the viewer read the constitution. The re-record abandons that.
**Chapter 1 now explains the whole machine in ~5 minutes.** So the rest of the
video is no longer a reveal; it's **depth on demand** — a viewer can stop after
Ch 1, or drill into exactly the part they doubt.

Structure is **fractal**: Chapter 1 is the 5-minute version of the whole thing;
each later chapter is one Chapter-1 beat at higher resolution, adding the two
things Ch 1 skips — **the tension** (the objection a skeptic forms) and **the
mechanism** (how it works + the constitutional principle).

Vocabulary locked this cycle: **capital** (not patronage) for the money/stock/
chamber; **funders** for the people; **chamber** (not house). See the
patronage→capital sweep across the constitution + design docs.

## The deep dive — Chapter 1, exploded (6 chapters, ~18–22 min)

Sequenced by **dependency/doubt**, not Ch 1's process order — deliberation
(Ch 4) sits *after* the chambers (Ch 3) because its punchline ("influence
weights the vote, never the argument") only lands once chambers + standing are
established.

### Ch 2 — Why a game *(expands Ch 1 ①)*
- **Opens on:** "cute — but why wrap government in a game?"
- **Closes with:** the game is the participation engine self-governance always
  lacked.
- **Payload:** the apathy graveyard (DAOs / online democracies wrote the rules
  and nobody came); the game as the engine they never had; the MUD +
  livestreaming origin; *educated electorate = functioning electorate*; the
  unity-of-substance (argument, vote, law, code, record are all **text** →
  legible, hashable, forkable).
- **Content source:** `chapter-0.md`.

### Ch 3 — The Chambers — Earning a Voice *(expands Ch 1 ④ — merged)*
- **Opens on:** "three groups — won't one capture the others? And inside a
  chamber, can't you hoard or fake your way up?"
- **Closes with:** co-equal non-convertible chambers force a cross-class
  coalition; standing is earned and conferred, never owned or minted.
- **Payload (structure):** every creative economy is labor / capital / audience
  in permanent tension, and almost all let one class capture the rest (capital
  buys out labor, ignores users). Co-equal + non-convertible + **2-of-3** forces
  a coalition; no class rules alone. **The firewall in full** — capital earns a
  voice, never an advantage, never conversion (the crypto/token disarm; the
  "not a security" line: capital, no market in it).
- **Payload (currency):** standing is **earned by contributing and conferred by
  others** (make = others' engagement, author ≠ actor; play = participation
  others value) → the **Sybil one-liner** (others give it or it doesn't exist);
  spent as **conviction** without being used up; "no number is an authority."
- **Anchors:** Art. I §§2–5, Art. III §2, Art. IV §3.
- **Content source:** `chapter-1.md` (old "Three voices") + `chapter-2.md`.

### Ch 4 — The Argument Map — Not the Crowd *(expands Ch 1 ③)*
- **Opens on:** "won't the loudest steamroll the debate?"
- **Closes with:** argument is judged by structure, not popularity or reputation;
  influence touches only the final vote.
- **Payload:** the typed argument map (claims attach by `supports` / `objects-to`
  / `responds-to`); **reputation-blind and enforced** (voting refused on an
  argument board, scores zeroed); the **open-objection** signal makes dissent
  structurally un-buryable; the one named **LLM** use (librarian, never judge —
  suggests merges, touches the view never the record).
- **Anchors:** Art. III §5, Art. I §4.
- **Content source:** `chapter-3.md`.

### Ch 5 — Governing is shipping software *(expands Ch 1 ⑤)*
- **Opens on:** "who actually builds and enforces all this — the founder?"
- **Closes with:** the branches are the dev lifecycle of a live system; power to
  ship is held by confidence, checked by review.
- **Payload:** legislature = **requirements** (the what, never the how);
  executive = **the build** + the code/human enforcement split (what can be code
  is code; administration stays human); judiciary = **verification** (code
  review) + **appeal** (was it fair), one async case at every scale; **sortition**
  juries drawn by lot, influence and reputation counting for nothing.
- **Anchors:** Art. I §7, Art. V, Art. VI.
- **Content source:** `chapter-4.md`.

### Ch 6 — The Record — Don't Trust, Verify *(expands Ch 1 ⑥)*
- **Opens on:** "how do I know the numbers are honest / the rules were followed?"
- **Closes with:** the record's integrity is independent of its keeper.
- **Payload:** hash-chained, signed, externally anchored, replicated;
  re-derivable by any member; **the honest limit** — tamper-evidence makes
  falsification *detectable, not impossible*, so the final backstop is
  transparency + exit; the one good idea from crypto (verifiability) minus the
  token/speculation grift.
- **Anchors:** Art. I §6, Art. VII.
- **Content source:** `chapter-5.md`.

### Ch 7 — The Dial — Operator to Republic *(expands Ch 1's close)*
- **Opens on:** "the operator still holds power — isn't this just benevolent
  dictatorship?"
- **Closes with:** every community deserves an honest floor and a real exit; what
  you build above that is yours.
- **Payload:** the platform is a **dial** from operator-fiat to full republic;
  the **floor guaranteed everywhere** = firewall + verifiable record + right to
  fork and walk; "you already run a government — you call it moderation"; founder
  self-binding + dilution + ratification. Lands into the call to action (or hand
  to a separate outro — see `outro.md`).
- **Anchors:** Art. XI, Art. X §4, the eternity clauses.
- **Content source:** `chapter-6.md` + `outro.md`.
