1. **TOEFL was missed — and it's the best fit of all.** It's Study's
   **#2 strategic priority**, they're the **exclusive/official ETS
   partner**, ~**2.3M takers/yr globally**, it's **date-driven**, and its
   **Speaking & Writing are performance tasks** (the "notorious gap" Study
   explicitly wants an **AI-tutor experience** to fill). It never surfaced
   in my scan because **TOEFL isn't in the `Test_Prep_Pillar` type
   breakdown** — a blind spot of ranking by one signal.
2. **The nursing flagship example was the wrong product.** The NGN
   postop-knee case ([study-com-integration-examples.md](./study-com-integration-examples.md)
   #②) is **NCLEX / NGN clinical content (B2B via Elsevier, "out of
   scope")** — *not* the **TEAS / HESI-A2 entrance** exams that are Study's
   B2C nursing test-prep vertical. Those entrance exams are **pre-clinical
   academic recall** (reading/math/science). The case is still a great
   "live-it" demo, but it represents the clinical/exit side, not the
   vertical Study sells.

Both are the same lesson: **rank by fit and market, not by how vivid the
scenario feels.**

## 1. Two rankings, and why they differ

**Content footprint** (`Test_Prep_Pillar` count) ≠ **business priority**:

- **Footprint:** Teaching 880 · Academic 156 · College Readiness 116 ·
  Real Estate 106 · Nursing 95 · Grade School 63 · Finance 34 · College
  Credit 26 · Grad School 25 · Counseling 12 · Admin 11 · HR 10 · PM 10 ·
  ASVAB/Military 7 · Clinical Path 7 · Social Work 6 · … (**TOEFL absent —
  modeled separately**).
- **Business priority (the plan's own order):** **1. Teacher Cert ·
  2. TOEFL · 3. Nursing entrance (TEAS/HESI) · 4. ASVAB.** Everything else
  (Real Estate, SHRM/HR, Social Work, Pharmacy Tech, Counseling, Finance)
  is **"pursue only with a partner in hand."** 2026 revenue targets:
  **Teacher TP 0% YoY (mature), Career Cert −20%** — so even the #1 vertical
  is *big but not a growth engine*; the plan is depth-in-priority-suites,
  not breadth.

Footprint measures *content invested*; priority measures *where Study is
betting*. Saxonberg fit is a third axis again — highest where the exam is a
**performance** and/or there's an **applied gap a page can't serve**.

## 2. The fit framework

A vertical's Saxonberg value ≈ **f( performance/applied gap ·
scenario-density · external-date urgency · credit-vs-prep · (market ×
Study priority) )** — *not* footprint alone.

## 4. The three archetypes (corrected)

- **A. Performance / applied — "perform it and be coached, don't bubble
  it."** **TOEFL Speaking/Writing ⭐** (the wedge), Social Work / Counseling
  clinical simulation, HR situational judgment, and the **downstream
  practicum** for teaching (edTPA) + the NGN/NCLEX clinical content (B2B).
  *These test a performance or judgment a page can't stage — the sweet
  spot.*
- **B. Academic / skills recall — "live the problem + your countdown
  coach."** **Nursing entrance (TEAS/HESI)**, ASVAB, teacher knowledge
  exams, Real Estate, Pharmacy, SAT/ACT/GRE. *Study's actual volume lives
  here; the value is reframing drill as lived problems + the date-anchored
  Proving Ground + anxiety relief (attacking their retention/pass-rate
  problems — nursing retention is 2.7 months, first-attempt pass rates
  45–79%).*
- **C. Credit / CX — "the university experience" + the twofer.** College
  Accelerator / College Saver. The University classroom shape.

## 5. TOEFL is the wedge (the standout miss)

Every fit axis points at TOEFL:

- **Performance-based** — Speaking & Writing are productive tasks needing
  scoring/feedback; the plan calls this "a notorious gap" and explicitly
  wants "an **AI Tutor-driven experience** that builds on the TOEFL
  foundation." Saxonberg's **perform-and-coach** loop (you *do* the task,
  the sim/rubric adjudicates, the agent coaches — classroom-model §7.4,
  §7.8) *is* that experience.
- **#2 priority + exclusive ETS partner** — strategic pull + the
  credibility rail, same as Praxis for teaching.
- **Date-driven + global (2.3M/yr)** — perfect Proving-Ground countdown;
  huge TAM; a **new 2026 adaptive format** creating a re-prep wave.
- **It's already the resident domain** — the in-flight TOEFL work
  (speaking/writing, AI grading) means the content, the AI-feedback
  problem, and the people are all here.

**TOEFL is the beachhead**: the smallest-surface, highest-strategic-fit,
performance-based, partner-backed place to prove the perform-and-coach
model — then generalize to the other performance verticals.

## 6. The twofer / CotF alignment (the strategic frame to pitch into)

Study's strategy is a **"twofer"**: test-prep + **CX College credit** toward
**Careers of the Future (CotF)** — the four pathways **education, nursing,
business, technology**. Saxonberg's **two game shapes are the twofer in one
world**: the **Proving Ground** (prep for the cert, date-driven) *and* the
**University** (earn CX credit toward the pathway). Teaching and Nursing —
the two biggest verticals — are both CotF pathways, so a learner preps the
cert *and* banks credit toward the degree in the same world. **Pitch into
the twofer; don't pitch a standalone game.**
