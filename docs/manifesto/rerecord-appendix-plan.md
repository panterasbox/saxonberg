# ⭐ The spine

> **Chapters 1–7: how a decision moves. Appendices: what the machine may
> and may not do to you.**

That is the constitution / Bill-of-Rights split, the audience already
knows the shape, and it explains why every appendix keeps becoming an
amendment — **they are all rights-and-powers material.** Appendix B reads
as the 9th/10th because the tenure floor **is** a reserved-powers clause
wearing a land costume.

⭐ It also gives the appendices their own **fractal structure**, matching
the chapters: A establishes that everything is a dial; B establishes what
is *not*; the rest are individual floors at higher resolution.

---

# Selection criteria

A candidate earns its own video when **all three** hold:

1. **The game makes it concrete.** Prose-only → a mention. *"The registry
   makes takings concrete"* → a video.
2. **There is a real dilemma.** Officer pay has no tension. Free exercise
   versus sentient sacrifice does.
3. **It teaches something the audience does not already have.** Not
   *"here is our rule"* — *"here is the problem everyone has."*

> ⚠⚠ **And explicitly NOT "is it pressing."** Speedy trial and succession
> are both flagged pressing in the walk, but that is **build** priority.
> **Build-state never selects content** — a repeat correction, and this is
> exactly the shape it takes.

> ⚠ **A fourth, harder filter: you cannot narrate a decision you have not
> made.** Several strong topics are undesigned, not unimportant. They
> belong on the worklist, not the shot list.

# ⭐⭐⭐ Appendix E — the digital-rights video

The gap the walk structurally could not find:

> **User: "there's things missing… right-to-your-data shit like that. We
> have parcels and then some cross-cutting collections — how do we know
> what's yours? People have been talking about digital rights since the
> Clinton admin. Section 230 probably has implications for us as much as
> anyone else."**

**The walk is Bill-of-Rights-shaped, so it inherits 1791's concerns** and
misses everything the last thirty years have actually argued about.

## Five beats, and they are an argument rather than a list

| Beat | Status |
|---|---|
| ⭐ **What's yours** — the five classes | the taxonomy *is* the decision |
| **The record vs. erasure** → *unlink, don't erase* | proposed |
| **What others may say about you** — `beliefs` as data with two subjects | ⚠ **needs one ruling** |
| ⭐ **Being measured** — trait derivation as psychometric profiling | ✅ **answered** — see below |
| ⭐⭐ **Who answers for it** — *carrying is publishing, hosting is not* | ✅ **already decided** |

## The taxonomy (beat 1)

Parcels answer ownership for path extents, chattel for instances. **The
cross-cutting collections have no ownership model at all**, and they do
not all want the same one:

| Class | Example | Whose? |
|---|---|---|
| **things you hold** | parcels, chattel | yours — title answers it |
| **things you did** | `authoring_events`, chronicle deeds | yours, and inalienable |
| **things done to you** | `accountability_events` | ⚠ **shared** — an event has two parties |
| **what others think of you** | ⭐⭐ `beliefs`, `renown_events` | **theirs, about you** — the hard class |
| **what the system measured** | `transcripts`, `participation` | derived; arguably nobody's |

⭐⭐ **`beliefs` is the killer case**: per-*viewer* identity memory, so
*"what Bob believes about Alice"* is a row about Alice stored for Bob.
That is exactly the fight about your friend's contact list containing my
phone number — and the schema already has it.

## ⭐ Beat 4 is answered, and not by a permission flag

[psychology-slate](../slates/builds/psychology-slate.md) resolves it:
**you cannot read yourself, another person can, and you only discover
what you choose to reveal.** Trait visibility stops being a right granted
or withheld and becomes **a relationship you build and a disclosure you
choose** — which is a far better thing to put on camera than a yes/no.

## ⭐⭐ Beat 5 is the closer

> **Carrying is publishing. Hosting is not.**

Derived from push-vs-pull for entirely unrelated reasons, and it lands
where §230 landed — **including the part everyone forgets, that
moderating does not make you the publisher.** A committee can enforce
house rules on its parcel without becoming answerable for everything said
there.

⭐ Audiences like watching a principle pay off somewhere it was not
aimed. **You did not set out to answer §230 and you did anyway.**

## ⚠ One ruling still needed before scripting

**Whose row is a belief about you?** One sentence, and beat 3 cannot be
written without it.
