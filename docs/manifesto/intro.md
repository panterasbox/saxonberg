# Intro — "A modest proposal for gamified government"

> **The full-screen open** — warm, direct-to-camera personal introduction, the
> opening bookend to `outro.md`. Full-screen camera is reserved for these two
> segments ONLY (this intro + the outro); everything between is corner/no-face.
> Leads with the *familiar* (who I am + it's an MMO), sets up what the video is
> (the political model), and hands to Ch 0 (corner) on the cut. Voice: personal,
> warm, confident — eases the viewer in (this is a long video, and we're asking
> for a time commitment), no grievance, no flinch, not about the founder.
> Provisional per process (the opener is the single most-likely line to re-tune).

## Narration (draft)

**[The open — full-screen, to camera]**
"Hello — my name's Bobby Schaetzle. I'm a software engineer from the Bay Area, and for a
while now I've been working on building a platform for learning through play.

There's a lot to it, but here's the short version: it's a MUD which is sorta like a text-based MMO — a big,
persistent, world you log into with other people — and this MUD attempts to model
real-world systems with an academic depth. An economy. An ecology. A society.

This video is about one part of it in particular: the political model. Because
the people who play it don't just play — they run it. What that turned into is a
new system of government for digital communities: a way for a community to write
its own rules, settle its own disputes, and decide how it spends its resources.

A real government — inside a game. Let me show you how it works."

[Hard cut to corner/no-face → **Ch 0** (`chapter-0.md`), which opens on the
origin: "The ideas behind it didn't come from political theory…"]

**Kicker:** *"a new system of government for digital communities — a real
government, inside a game."*

## Notes

- **Full-screen reserved for intro + outro only** (per the framing discipline).
  This is the opening bookend; `outro.md` is the closing one. Ch 0 runs corner
  from its first beat.
- **Warm open, not a cold-open hook.** Leads with the familiar — a person, and a
  thing people already understand (an MMO) — then narrows to the political model.
  The old cold-open paradox ("a new system of government… and it runs inside a
  game") is gone: a paradox teaser suits a 90-second trailer, not a long video
  you're asking people to sit through. The "inside a game" line survives, softer,
  as the button before the setup line.
- **Setup line earns the commitment.** "Let me show you how it works" at the *end*
  of the intro is a signpost into the rest of the doc — distinct from the old
  meta throat-clearing ("in this video I'm going to describe…") at the *start*,
  which is gone.
- **Language discipline (from the voice pass):**
  - *"text-based,"* not "all in text" — the world also has images, diagrams, video;
    text is the foundation, not the whole medium. Steered off "text model(s)" —
    to this audience that reads as *AI language model*.
  - *"models real-world systems with unusual depth,"* never "realistic" — the
    medium is text (a complete abstraction), so realism is a category error; the
    fidelity is in the *depth of the modeling*, not the look. "Unusual" is a
    comparative claim (more than you'd expect), not an absolute one (equals
    reality) — honest, and it dodges "high fidelity."
  - *"learning through play,"* not "gamification" — the latter reads corporate-cheese
    to a DGG-literate crowd (points-and-badges).
  - *"how it spends its resources,"* not "where its money goes" — generalizes past
    money to the other scarcity (compute).
  - No *"actually,"* no *"live in this world"* (no living-online).
- **Origin stays in Ch 0.** The MUDs/livestreaming origin is deliberately NOT in
  this intro — Ch 0 beat 1 opens on it ("the MUDs I cut my teeth on"), so saying
  it here would double it.
- **The "modest proposal" framing lives here** (and in the Reddit post title:
  *"Saxonberg: a modest proposal for gamified government"* — the word "Saxonberg"
  appears ONLY in the post text, never in narration). NB: the title card still
  reads *"gamified,"* which sits in mild tension with cutting "gamification" from
  the narration — worth a later pass on whether the title changes too.
- **Not about the founder.** Personal, yes ("hi, my name's Bobby"), but the frame
  stays on the work, not the person.
