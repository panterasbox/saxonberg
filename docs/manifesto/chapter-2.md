## Model — describe THIS (the shipped system), not the constitution draft

- **Standing** = the unit of influence. Three stocks: **make / fund / play**
  (producer / patron / consumer). **Earned and held** — *no reservoir, no
  lifetime cap, no "decays when moved."*
- **Conferred by others** (the Sybil answer): make counts only *others'*
  engagement (author ≠ actor, no self-credit); play is participation others
  engage with; fund is money (walled to this stock — Ch 1's firewall). You
  can't self-mint.
- **Conviction = the spend.** Commit standing to a position; weight **builds the
  longer you hold it, resets on flip**; **full weight on every issue** (no
  finite pool, no rationing); standing itself never depletes.
- **"Earned and spent, never owned"** = earned as standing (held), spent as
  conviction (commitment over time), never an owned / transferable / cashable
  balance.
- **Vocabulary is the shipped product's** (standing · make/fund/play ·
  conviction) — the video speaks the game's own words.

## Narration (draft)

**[Beat 1 — what it is]**
"Last chapter I kept saying 'a voice,' 'weight,' 'influence' — without telling
you what it actually is. So here it is. Your voice in this is something we call
**standing**, and the first thing to know is how you *get* it — because that's
exactly what stops someone from amassing a pile of it and running their corner."

**[Beat 2 — earned, conferred by others]**
"You earn standing three ways — the same three: make something other people
actually use, chip in to keep the lights on, or show up and take part. Make,
fund, play. But here's the part that matters most: **you can't hand it to
yourself.** Standing is conferred by *other people*. The make side only counts
engagement from people who aren't you — no crediting your own work. The play
side isn't clocking hours; it's taking part in ways the rest of the community
actually engages with. So a thousand fake accounts earn you nothing — they've
got nothing real to confer. **You can't mint influence from accounts you
control. Others give it to you, or it doesn't exist.**"

**[Beat 3 — spent as conviction]**
"And here's the thing about standing: it's a capacity you hold — genuinely yours,
and it doesn't drain away. What it *isn't* is a pile of chips you own: you can't
trade it, you can't cash it out, and you never spend it down. The way you use it
is **conviction**: you put your standing behind a position, and the longer you
hold it, the more it weighs.
Patience counts; a last-second swing doesn't — flip, and the weight starts over.
So nobody ambushes a vote by piling in at the end. And you're never rationed —
full weight on every question, not a budget you spread thin. **You spend it
without ever using it up.**"

**[Beat 4 — land + hand forward]**
"So: earned by contributing, given to you by others so you can't fake it, spent
as patience instead of hoarded. **Earned and spent — never owned.** But that's
just the ballot — the weight behind a yes or a no. None of it says how a
community actually *argues something out* before the vote, or what stops the
loudest voices from steamrolling the conversation itself. That's next."

~2.5 min. **Kickers (say close to verbatim):** *"you can't mint influence from
accounts you control — others give it to you, or it doesn't exist"*; *"you spend
it without ever using it up"*; the creed *"earned and spent, never owned."*

## Notes

- **Tense honesty:** make + play earned **today**; **fund** assumed live by
  publish (founder's call — intake being wired); **conviction** is built backend
  substrate but there are **no live bills to vote on yet**, so Beat 4 frames the
  deliberation/decision layer as "next." Chambers / 2-of-3 are population-deferred.
- **Reputation cut from this chapter, on purpose.** Renown only multiplies the
  play stock (one chamber) *and* its measurement is unsolved (instrumenting
  reactions ≈ the obvious); claiming "we measure reputation" on camera hands a
  debate crowd its softest target. The compelling reputation idea is Ch 3's
  **reputation-blind deliberation** (ignores identity — needs no measurement).
  Renown, if ever mentioned, is an honest open problem, never a pillar.
- **Liquid delegation → Ch 6, not here.** Real point ("you don't have to vote on
  every measure — delegate, per-issue, revocable") but **unbuilt**, and it'd
  re-muddy this chapter. Home: Ch 6 (the dial), paired with operator-defer as
  **graduated participation — nobody carries the whole load** (operators defer to
  the community; citizens delegate to each other). Framed designed/future.
- **[OPEN] throw:** the franchise / personhood gate — the real Sybil-at-scale
  question lives here, *not* in renown.
- **Standing is a held capacity, not "nothing you own" (Beat 3).** The earlier
  draft said "no balance you own" — wrong: you *do* hold a standing capacity, and
  it's yours. The precise claim (per the model): standing is **held**, not **owned
  as a tradeable/cashable asset** — you can't transfer it, cash it out, or spend
  it down (conviction applies full weight everywhere without depleting it). So
  "never owned" in the creed / title means *never a tradeable, cashable
  possession*, NOT "you hold nothing." Affirm the capacity; deny only the
  asset/tradeability.
- **Model B revises draft-constitution Art. IV §2** (the reservoir / "decays
  when moved" language is superseded).
