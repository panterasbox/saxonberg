# Outro — "Let's see what this becomes"

> **The closing segment** — the final YouTube chapter stop ("→ how to get
> involved"). Voice: personal / direct-to-camera. **Full-screen camera
> throughout** — the closing bookend to Ch 0's full-screen open; the two
> direct-address moments of the whole video, nothing in between. Picks up the
> hard cut from Ch 6's last line ("that's the design"). Holds the *human note*
> that doesn't belong in the model chapters: an honest read on where the project
> actually is, what's open to engage with today, the road ahead, and the
> make/fund/play three ways to take part. Not gated on any build.

## What this segment is for (and the tone)

The model is done by the time this plays. This is the close — **state, don't
sell.** People will engage or they won't; a hard sell at the end flips nobody and
reads as desperate. So the register is confident and *honest about the
early-stage reality*: there's a lot still to build, but there's already enough to
start — a draft constitution to deliberate over and a model to design content
against — and the road ahead (a real funded instance, someone to stand behind it)
is named plainly, not pitched. Doors held open, not begged through.

## Narration (draft)

**[Beat 1 — where it actually stands]**
"That's the whole design — on paper. Let me be straight about where it actually
is: this is early. There's a lot still to build, and plenty of work left on my
end. But there's already enough here to start.

**[Beat 2 — what's open today]**
Two things are real right now. There's a draft constitution — so if you want to
argue over how something like this should be governed, there's something concrete
to argue with. And there's enough of the model that you can start imagining the
world you'd build inside it. Deliberate, or design — both doors are open today.

**[Beat 3 — the road ahead]**
The rest is ahead of us. If this has a future — if people actually engage — the
next step is a real instance: somewhere a community runs the whole thing for
real. That'll take money, and someone to stand behind it. None of it's settled
yet. It's just what's on the other side of this, if the interest is there.

**[Beat 4 — three ways + the button]**
Make, fund, play — build it, back it, or live in it. That's how you take part,
whenever you're ready. All the code is open-source, the content Creative Commons, so I would start there if you'd like to know more. Thanks for watching and I look forward to hearing your feedback.

**Kickers:** *"let me be straight about where it actually is: this is early — but

model to design against — both doors are open today"*; *"make, fund, play — build
it, back it, or live in it"*; *"I'll be live — let's see what this becomes."*

## Notes

- **State, don't sell.** Earlier draft read desperate ("what I'm asking for is
  engagement," "the fastest way it gets better…"). Cut. The honesty about the
  early stage replaces the plea — and is more compelling than it.
- **Accuracy fix:** do NOT say "I've taken it as far as one person can" — false.
  The true state: lots still to build, but enough now to start (constitution +
  model). That honesty is the spine of this segment.
- **Patronage / "someone to stand behind it" kept light.** Named as a real future
  hurdle, not a who's-the-face pitch and not pinned on the founder — it's up in
  the air. Make it more explicit only if asked.
- **The closing bookend.** Ch 0 opens full-screen face; the Outro closes
  full-screen face — the two direct-address moments of the whole video. The edit
  the brief asked about (*where does the final full-screen camera happen*) is the
  Ch 6 → Outro hard cut, held to the last frame.
- **make/fund/play is a callback, not new vocab** (Ch 1 / Ch 2) — consumer /
  producer / patron. "Whenever you're ready" = standing doors, not act-now.
- **No AI named here.** The "exactly once" rule holds — AI is named only in Ch 3.
- **Distribution hook:** the chapter an interested community gets pointed at —
  keep it linkable and self-contained enough to stand semi-alone.
