# Chapter 0 — "A better government, and it lives in a game"

> **First-draft narration + visual cue sheet.** Voice: personal / witnessed
> (first-person, earnest, "obsessed with why"). **The opening is provisional
> by design** — per process, openings get rewritten after the full first
> draft exists, once we can see everything they lead into. Ch 0 is not gated
> on any build. Spine + doubt-hinges live in `constitution-video.md`.

## Narration (draft)

**[Beat 1 — grievance / hook]**
"I've watched a lot of online communities try to govern themselves, and I've
watched almost all of them fail — always the same two ways. Either money takes
over: whoever pays the most ends up running things. Or almost nobody shows up,
and the handful who do quietly capture the whole thing. Plutocracy, or apathy.
I got obsessed with *why* it's always one of those two — and this is what I've
come to think the answer is.

**[Beat 2 — the turn / conviction]**
These aren't failures of democracy. They're failures of *conditions*.
Real-world democracies inherited theirs — borders, scarcity, the friction of
having to show up in person — and we never get to redesign them. But online,
you could design the conditions from scratch. A polity built that way, freed
of those old frictions, could keep everything those systems were protecting,
and throw out only the parts they were just *enduring*.

**[Beat 3 — reveal / orientation]**
So I started building one. A real government — real power over a real, shared
world — run by the people who live in it. They write its laws. They hold its
treasury. They sit its courts. It lives inside a game. A *text* game, actually
— and I'll come back to that, because it turns out to be the point, not the
limitation.

**[Beat 4 — why a game]**
Here's why a game. A game solves the exact thing that kills everything else.
Real democracies *beg* you to participate, and you don't, because participating
is a chore. A game never has to beg — showing up *is* the fun part. So all the
participation real systems starve for is just... what people are already doing.
The governance rides on top of it.

**[Beat 5 — platform + invitation]**
And I want to be clear: this isn't really a pitch for my game. It's a pitch for
the machine *underneath* it — something any community could pick up and run.
Maybe even yours. What I've got is a draft, an unfinished constitution, and
over the next few chapters I'm going to walk you through how it actually works
— how you earn power, how decisions get made, who enforces them. And honestly?
I'm hoping some of you will try to tear it apart."

Hands Ch 1 the next doubt: *"it's real and money funds it — so won't the rich
just own it?"*

**Author check (only you can make these true):** Beat 1's "I've watched almost
all of them fail" must be genuinely yours (DGG smells borrowed authority);
Beat 3's "I started building one" sets a timeline — keep it consistent with how
the project's history is told elsewhere.

## Visual cue sheet

Ch 0 is the **intro**, so it earns the rare full-screen treatment — but only at
the two direct-address moments (the opening hook, the closing invitation); the
explanatory middle (Beats 2–4) is **corner / no-face** like the rest of the
video. Client never shown.

- **Beat 1 — face, full-frame.** Only cutaway: on "Plutocracy, or apathy," two
  small hand-drawn icons pop in, one per word (money-bag/crown; empty
  chair/tumbleweed). **Plants the "two diseases" motif** recalled all series
  (Ch 1 firewall kills money; engagement kills apathy). Back to face on "I got
  obsessed with why."
  - *Asset:* 2 labeled hand-drawn icons (transparent PNG), revealed one at a time.
- **Beat 2 — light motif.** "Conditions you could redesign": a quick sketch of
  inherited constraints (*borders / scarcity / showing up in person*) struck
  through / wiped to a **blank drawing board** on "design from scratch." One
  gesture only.
  - *Asset:* "constraints → blank slate" sketch (or a small inset over face).
- **Beat 3 — orientation.** On "write its laws / hold its treasury / sit its
  courts," three icons appear in sequence (scroll, coffer, gavel) — quietly
  pre-seeds the branches. Then on "a text game," cut to a **stylized text-world
  card**: representative game prose (room description / command / dialogue) in
  hand-styled monospace — a designed evocation of "a world made of words," NOT
  the client. (Sample game text, not narration subtitles.)
  - *Assets:* 3 function icons; 1 styled text-world card.
- **Beat 4 — the one built diagram.** Two-layer stack: broad base "what people
  already do — play," then a slab "governance" settling on top exactly on
  "rides on top of it." Build base → top. Optional lead-in: "real democracy: ↑
  begging → people walking away."
  - *Asset:* "governance rides on engagement" layered diagram, staged.
- **Beat 5 — platform + invitation.** "The machine underneath": one box (your
  game) on a base labeled "the machine," then **ghosted extra boxes** ("your
  community," "another") dropping onto the same base — previews the dial
  chapter. On "a draft… constitution," a doc title card with a "DRAFT" stamp.
  On "try to tear it apart," cut back to **full-frame face**.
  - *Assets:* "machine-underneath" sketch (staged); "draft constitution" title card.

Rhythm: face → motif → orientation icons + text card → built diagram →
platform sketch → face. Visual change ~every 10–15s. Motifs planted: two
diseases, three functions, machine-underneath.
