THE REAL CONSTRAINT

Storage is a mailbox — delete something to make room, and it never expires on you. That matters because a dispute can take weeks, and a retention window would erase the evidence exactly when it is still being argued.

Attestation is the thing on a clock. The server keeps a rolling chain of frame hashes, not your content — cheap to run, but it only reaches back so far. Attest while it still covers the span, or the clip stays just your word.
What the recipient sees the distinction has to be unmissable

AUTO Attested on filing — the chain still covers this span

Filing attests automatically rather than offering it as a choice. Someone reporting harassment should not have to understand a hash chain, and the window closes while they are deciding whether it is worth reporting at all.

It also means a moderator never receives an unverifiable report, which is the failure mode that makes moderation queues worthless.
