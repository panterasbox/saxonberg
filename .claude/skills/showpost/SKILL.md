# Show post

Read `docs/shows/README.md` and the week's rundown (`docs/shows/YYYY-MM-DD.md`) first.

## Inputs

- **The chapter log** (from the chapter command, once built; until then, the owner's
  timestamps or the rundown's planned times);
- **The transcript** (Descript exports one; ask the owner for the file).

## Produce

1. **YouTube chapters** for the VOD description: `00:00` first, then one line per segment
   that ran, in the segment's chapter title. YouTube needs at least three chapters, each
   at least ten seconds.
2. **The cut list for Descript:** per segment, the in and out timestamps for its long clip
   (8–12 minutes), trimmed to start at the hook and end at the payoff.
3. **Shorts candidates:** 30–60 second moments, each a single surprising fact or a one-line
   payoff, with timestamps and a vertical-framing note.
4. **Per clip:** a title, a description of two or three sentences, and thumbnail text of
   three to five words. Brand line in every description: *Bobalu Smallberries, founder of
   Pantera's Box, home of Saxonberg.* Credit the AI plainly; the posture is unapologetic.
5. **Notes back into the docs:** anything decided on air (The Fork's verdict, a Live Design
   result) goes to its slate; a media response to `docs/media-responses.md`.

Write the outputs into the rundown page under a **Post** heading, commit and merge it like
any design doc. The owner does the edit in Descript.
