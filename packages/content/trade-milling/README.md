# trade-milling

Grind, then bolt.

⭐ **The decision is the extraction.** How much of the grain you keep is
the miller's judgment, made per grind, and it is continuous: 0.61 and
0.62 are different flour. Take less and you keep the pure inner stock —
white, and it keeps, because the growth floor is below its water
activity. Take more and you keep the outer matter with it: darker, more
nourishing, and quicker to go off, since the bran carries the oil and
the water.

There is **no material row per band**. One flour per cereal; the
extraction rides the flour's own payload as a composition and a water
state, so the label, the tags and the keeping all move continuously
through machinery that already existed.

⭐⭐ **The capital ladder buys back your TIME.** A hand quern claims your
`hands` for the whole grind — you are cranking, and you can do nothing
else. A water mill claims nothing of yours: set it going and walk away,
because the river does the work. Faster is a consequence; freedom is the
purchase.

**The toll is in kind.** A share of the flour stays in the mill's bin and
the miller sells it. That is a multure, and it pays for the premises with
no banking code at all.

⚠ This pack does **not** depend on `content-water`. The mill reads its
power by duck-typing a room sibling that answers `generationW` — so a
mill with no wheel grinds at zero and says so, and a realm with no water
pack still installs.
