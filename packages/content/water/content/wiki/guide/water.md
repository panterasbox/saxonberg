# Water {#water}

Water comes from somewhere, and where it comes from decides everything
about it: whether it runs, how hard, and whether it is fit to drink.

## Where your water comes from {#where}

A tap reads its premises' supply — the city main, a district tank, a
roof cistern, a well. `analyze water tap` says which, and how much head of
water stands over the tap: a low tank runs weak, a main runs hard. A piped
outlet has a valve; when the supply runs short, the public standpipes and
the fountain are served first and the houses and trades share what is
left. A house with no pipe into a room has no water in it at all.

## Is it fit to drink? {#fit}

By eye, water is water. Fouled water looks and tastes like clean, and a
draught of it can give you dysentery. Only the city analyst's water test
kit reads what is in it — at the infirmary, for the health officer.

## Wells and pumps {#wells}

A well stands on ground water, and the ground water rises and falls with
the year. A suction pump cannot lift water from very far down — the air
does the pushing — so a deep well needs a bucket on a rope long enough to
reach. Butts on a roof fill when it rains, and from the snow as it melts.

## Watering the land {#irrigation}

The Delight valley's ditch is a company's: its headgate and turnouts open
by an amount (`open turnout to a quarter`), each turn on the rota is a
member's, and every gate keeps a record of who set it. A lined ditch loses
less on the way. Ground that is given more than it can take stands in
water, drowns what grows in it and, as it dries, leaves its salt behind.

## Hoses and sprinklers {#sprinklers}

A rubber hose screws onto a tap (`tie hose to tap`). Carried, it waters a
bed from the tap; screwed onto a **sprinkler** too, it throws the water
round in a circle — over the ground the sprinkler stands on and the beds
beside it, the way rain falls. How far and how fast is the tap's head, and
what lands is the weather's: a calm dawn wastes little, a windy noon a
good deal. A potted plant is watered by hand. Both are sold at the general
store.
