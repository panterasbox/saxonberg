# trade-coopering

The cooper (`/trade/coopering`). Staves are riven from a forestry billet
(`rive billet into stave`), hoops come from the smith, heads from boards
and dowels the carpenter makes, and a cask is raised with the kernel's
`fit` verb. Three products by competence, each a different joint row:

| product | joint | band | holds |
|---|---|---|---|
| slack barrel | `slack-hooped` | novice coopering | things (nails, apples) |
| dry cask | `hooped` | competent | granular bulk (flour, meal) |
| cask | `tight-hooped` | proficient | liquor |

The commons casks live in `generic-objects` (`/stuff/thing/vessel/…`), so
brewing and distilling name them without depending on this pack.

`measure capacity <cask>` (this pack's Reading row + `CapacityReading`)
answers anyone; a holder of a polity's `gauger` seat writes the figure of
record onto the cask.
