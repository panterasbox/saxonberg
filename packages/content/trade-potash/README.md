# @saxonberg/content-trade-potash

The ashery. Wood ash (sized and strengthened by the wood that burned) is
leached to lye in a hopper; an egg reads the lye's strength; the lye boils
down in an iron pot to a cake of black salts; the kiln calcines the cake to
pearl ash, the glassmaker's flux. Rows on shipped mechanisms — the Burner,
the hopper, the boil, `fire` — with the pack's own `AshPit` and `IronPot`
classes, the test egg, the rain butt, the `calcine` recipe, the `ashery`
Discipline and the ash-man's round brain. A second ashery is rows.

See `docs/subsystems/potash.md`.
