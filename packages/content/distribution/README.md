# distribution

The cash-and-carry — the distributor as its own pack (fermentation
D10). The counter at `/trade/distribution/thing/counter` is the
consignment shelf every producing trade's floor hand consigns onto
(the `consigns` brain) and every venue's keeper buys from
(`wallet use house`, `buy gin`). It moved here from trade-distilling
because a distributor is distribution infrastructure, not distilling:
with the move, no sibling trade depends on another — every
producer and venue points at this pack, which depends on the platform
alone.

Also here: the **malt line** — the one stocked line on an otherwise
consignment-only counter. Malt the material is base-library's (a shared
input whose owning trade does not exist yet lives in the commons); the
sack row and its par live here. It is the honestly-labelled
imported-input faucet (a stub upstream inside de-stubbed trades) the
mash tun and the wash draw from until a malting trade earns its pack.

Venue: the hall (`location/cash-and-carry`) off the Counting-Houses
avenue, Tam Ferrier at the counter, the independent Business
(`idea/business`) the Prime Minister's office appoints until the trade
has a seat of its own.
