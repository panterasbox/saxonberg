# trade-farming

**Growing, end to end.** A trade is a *process*; **produce is one of
farming's outputs**, not a trade of its own — so this pack owns both
halves, the way `trade-distilling` owns the still and the bottle.

- **Production** — the growing apparatus the husbandry/smallholding
  substrate drives: three pots, the garden bed, the seeds, and the plants
  they become. (A pot is horticulture's vessel the way a keg is
  brewing's. A dorm room owning one no more makes pots household content
  than the bar owning a keg makes kegs hospitality's.)
- **Output** — eight food materials tagged by category (lime, lemon,
  orange, grapefruit, mint, cherry, olive, cranberry), each a graded
  `Provision` item; the `crate`/`basket` presets over the platform
  `Crate`; a crate of each at target in the farm's stock; and the farm
  outfit consigning them onto `/trade/distilling/thing/counter`.

**Horticulture is farming too.** The ornamentals — peace lily, snake
plant — are grown here rather than filed under food, and they are the
reason the pack is named for the *process* and not for the groceries.

No serving recipe: produce is an input (`press` is hospitality's).
