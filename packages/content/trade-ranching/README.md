# trade-ranching

Keeping animals. The ground they stand on is `trade-farming`'s — *pasture
is a field* — so this pack depends on that one; what lives here is the
keeping.

- **The herdbook** (`HerdRegistry`) — a filed register on a branch titled
  to this trade. ⭐⭐ **You file; you do not hold the pen.** A record
  about you lives on somebody else's branch, because its value depends on
  its subject not being able to edit it.
- **The boundary acts** — `draft` a head out of the tally into its own
  page, `return` it to the tally. The stockman's own word is what moves
  it, and the asymmetry is honest: returning folds its condition into the
  mean and destructs the object, because its identity was the record and
  not the flesh.
- **Handling** — kernel substrate (pets want it too); this pack ships the
  acts that exercise it.

⚠ **The species rows are not here.** A sheep exists whether or not
anybody ranches. The husbandry is the trade; the animal is a thing in the
world.
