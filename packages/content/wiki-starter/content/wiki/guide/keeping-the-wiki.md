# Keeping the wiki {#keeping-the-wiki}

Four reports keep a wiki from rotting. None of them needs
permission to run.

- `wiki wanted` — what readers have asked for and nobody has
  written, ranked by how many pages want it. Start here.
- `wiki orphans` — pages nothing links to. Usually a sign
  something should mention them.
- `wiki links <page>` — what links here.
- `wiki dangling` — articles whose subject was renamed or deleted
  out from under them.

## Vandalism {#vandalism}

`wiki history` then `wiki rollback`. It takes about four seconds,
which is the whole reason editing can be open in the first place:
there is no review queue because undoing is cheaper than
reviewing.
