# Backpack {#backpack}

A canvas pack with broad straps. Wear it and the load rides your
shoulders instead of your hands, which is the whole point of one.

## What it is made of {#architecture}

Read out of the running world, not written down here — so what the
panel says is what the pack actually is today.

## Carrying {#carrying}

A load stowed in a worn pack couples at the worn floor: it still
weighs what it weighs, but it costs you no held surcharge and it
leaves both hands free. Carrying the same load loose does neither.
