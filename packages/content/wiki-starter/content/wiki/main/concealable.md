# Concealable {#concealable}

The capability of **not being obvious** — a band, from plainly
there to genuinely buried, that decides whether a thing announces
itself to somebody standing in the room.

It is not invisibility. Every concealed thing can be found; what
the band sets is how hard, and who notices without trying. A
stashed pouch under a loose board, a trap set across a doorway, a
creature waiting in the dark.

## What cannot {#what-cannot}

**The place itself.** You cannot hide a floor, a hearth or a yard
wall — there is nowhere for them to be hidden *from*. Concealment
is a property of something loose in a place, so it composes on
goods, on bodies and on ways through (a secret door is a hidden
way, not a hidden object).

Until 2026-09-29 every piece of matter in the game carried a
concealment band, including the ground. Five rows in the whole
world had ever set one, and all five were things you can pick up.
