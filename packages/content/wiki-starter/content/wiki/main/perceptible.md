# Perceptible {#perceptible}

The capability of **being addressable by a word** — keywords, and the
one a thing answers to first.

It is what makes `look lantern` find the lantern and `go north` find
the way. Without it a thing is perfectly real and perfectly present and
you cannot refer to it, which reads to a player as the thing not being
there at all.

## What does not {#what-does-not}

**Ideas.** A discipline, a quantity marshaller, a locomotion mode, a
help topic: real things the world is built out of, and none of them
something you point at. They are reached by a key or a path, never by
a word you type at a thing in front of you — and a spell is cast by
naming the spell, not by addressing it.

⚠ A **place** was in an odd position until 2026-09-30: rooms were
addressable only if their class had remembered to say so, and three
docstrings carried a warning about exactly that. Two classes had not
remembered, and their rooms' keywords went nowhere. Every place is
addressable now, because every place is a thing you can point at and
name.
