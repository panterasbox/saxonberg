# Combatant {#combatant}

The capability of **fighting back**.

⭐ Note what it is *not*: it is not what makes you a target. Anything
with a body that can be engaged can be attacked — that is the honest
rule, and it is why a thing does not become safe by being harmless.
What this adds is the other half of the exchange: poise, a stance, a
natural attack, an answer.

## The asymmetry {#asymmetry}

A corpse can be struck and does not strike back. Neither does a
sapling, a sack of grain or a sleeping town. Being hittable and being
able to hit are two different facts about a thing, and the world keeps
them separately on purpose.

⭐ A cat gained this on 2026-09-30, and it was the correction of an
asymmetry rather than a promotion: a pet could always be attacked. What
it lacked was the half that answers.
