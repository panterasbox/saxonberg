# Chattel {#chattel}

The capability of **being somebody's property** — a durable identity
a thing carries for its whole life, so that who owns it is a fact
about the thing and not about who happens to be holding it.

A good that has never changed hands carries no stamp. One that has
carries its whole chain of title, which is why a stolen thing is
awkward to sell: the provenance goes with it, and fencing is the
hard part, exactly as in life.

## What cannot {#what-cannot}

**People.** A player, a townsperson, a passer-by, a shade and a
corpse are not property. The mixin sat on the creature base until
2026-09-29 and quietly said otherwise about every body in the
world; nobody had ever stamped one, which is exactly why it went
unnoticed for so long.

**Anything you cannot carry off.** A floor, a hearth, a forge, a
shop counter, a yard wall. These belong to whoever holds the
*land*, which is a different kind of ownership with its own
records — see the parcel. The panel above will not list one, and
if it ever does, something has been composed on the wrong class.

The line is portability, and it is the same line that decides
whether a thing can be hidden.
