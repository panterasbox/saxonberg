# Avatar {#avatar}

**Your handle in the world — and you have three of them, for three
phases of play.**

An avatar is not *a character*. A character is the person; an avatar is
the body that person is acting through right now. You only ever drive
one at a time, and which one you are driving is a fact about the phase
of play you are in, not about who you are.

All three are the same you. Every ledger in the game — the chronicle,
the bank, the transcript, your standing — keys on your identity, not on
the body holding the pen. A deed done as a ghost is your deed.

## The three bodies {#the-three}

| | are your acts canon? | can you fully act? |
|---|---|---|
| **Primary** | yes | yes |
| **Shade** | yes — tagged as having happened while ghosted | no — a ghost cannot advance |
| **Sandbox** | no — the whole visit is rolled back at the door | yes |

⭐ **Each of the two non-primary bodies gives up exactly one thing, and
a different one.** That is why there is no single word for the
difference, and why the primary body is simply the one that gives up
neither.

## Primary {#primary}

The one you play. It registers under your name, it is what the world
means when it says *you*, and it is the only one that is written down
between sessions — your gear, where you were standing, what you were
carrying.

## Shade {#shade}

The body you wear while dead. It walks, it speaks, it is seen. What it
cannot do is anything that needs hands or standing: no keys, no
credential wallet, no gear — so the ordinary locks keep it in the
commons without anyone writing a rule for ghosts.

It is still you, and what you do still counts. You come back through
`passage`.

## Sandbox {#sandbox}

The body you wear inside a circle — your own private workshop, where
you can build and break anything without the world noticing.

In the fiction you *step onto the wire*. You can do everything there
that you can do outside; what is different is that none of it comes
back with you. The boundary is symmetric: nothing crosses in, nothing
crosses out. The one exception is what you *learned* about people —
your contacts come home, because you cannot un-know a face.

## Composition does not differ {#composition}

⭐ The three bodies are made of **exactly the same parts**. That is
deliberate and it is load-bearing: a verb that exists for one exists
for all three, so the game can always tell you *why* you cannot do
something instead of pretending the ability was never there.

A shade is refused `take` because it has no hands — not because `take`
was removed from ghosts. The refusal is the thing you can read.
