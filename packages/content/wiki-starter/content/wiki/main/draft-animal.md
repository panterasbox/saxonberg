# Draft Animal {#draft-animal}

**A beast kept in the shafts: it pulls, and it can be ridden.**

A heavy horse, a pit pony. Everything a beast is, plus two couplings —
a hitch for the cart and a place for a rider — and neither of them is
strength. Strength is mass, and mass is a number on the row; what the
class provides is the two places a load can attach.

## Why not simply a beast {#why-not-a-beast}

Because a wolf must refuse the cart, and the refusal has to happen
somewhere a player can read. Ask to hitch a wagon to a wolf and the
answer is *it can't pull a cart* — said plainly, before anything is
attempted. If every animal could be hitched, the refusal would move to
the moment the rig breaks away on a hill, which is a worse answer to a
question asked in a stable yard.

The same is true of riding. Mounting something mints a place to sit;
give that to every beast and the world quietly grows a saddle on every
fox.
