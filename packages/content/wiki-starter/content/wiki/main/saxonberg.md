# Saxonberg {#saxonberg}

This is the encyclopedia of the world — what things are, what
they are made of, and what you can do with them.

It is written by the people who play here, which includes you.
Every page has an edit button and a full history; nothing you
write is unrecoverable, and neither is anything you overwrite.

## Where to start {#where-to-start}

- [[guide:how-to-write-here|How to write here]] — the markup, in
  about two minutes.
- [[oak|Oak]] — a worked example of an article about a thing in
  the world.
- Type `wiki wanted` to see what other readers have asked for and
  nobody has written yet. That is the to-do list, and it is
  written by its readers.

## What goes here {#what-goes-here}

The nouns: materials, biomes, species, places, tools. Also lore,
guides, and anything else worth writing down.

What does NOT go here is what a verb does — that is `help`, and a
page can transclude it rather than restating it. The distinction
is worth keeping: help tells you what `plant` does; this tells
you what soil IS, and therefore why planting in it works.
