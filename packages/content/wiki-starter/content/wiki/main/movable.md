# Movable {#movable}

**A good: matter you can carry off.**

Everything solid in the world is *matter* — it is made of
something, it stands somewhere, it gets wet, you can look at it
and name its parts. A **movable** is matter with one more thing
true of it: somebody can pick it up and walk away.

That single fact has two consequences, and they are the whole of
what this rung adds:

- it can be **owned**, because a thing that moves needs its
  ownership to travel with it — see the chattel;
- it can be **hidden**, because a thing that moves can be put
  somewhere nobody is looking — see concealable.

Matter that stays put has neither. A floor is not stolen, it is
*trespassed on*; a forge is not hidden, it is *found*. Ownership
of a thing you cannot carry belongs to whoever holds the land.

## What in this world can be carried off {#composed-by}

The two capabilities below are what a movable HAS, and a thing that

## The test {#the-test}

It is not weight, and it is not whether the thing is bolted down.
A shop's table is screwed to the floor and is still stock the
shopkeeper bought and can sell. A wash basin is plumbed into the
wall and never was. The question is only ever *can this change
hands*.
