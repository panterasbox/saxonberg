# Employed {#employed}

The capability of **holding a post, and of somebody answering for you**.

Two things ride together here, and they are the same thing seen from
opposite sides: the record of what work you do, and the institution
that is named when you are harmed or when you do harm. A person with no
employer still has the second — you belong to *somebody*, even if the
somebody is only yourself.

## Who cannot {#who-cannot}

Animals. A draft horse works harder than most clerks and holds no post:
the work is somebody else's, the wage is somebody else's, and when the
horse is hurt the party that answers is its owner's, not its own.

⚠ Until 2026-09-30 a pit pony carried this, along with a faculty for
magic and a field for its aspirations, because it had been filed on the
rung where people live. Nothing ever read any of it — which is exactly
why it went unnoticed for so long, and exactly why it was worth fixing:
what a thing *claims* it can do is the part an author reads.
