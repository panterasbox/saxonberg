# Atmospheric {#atmospheric}

The capability of **being somewhere with its own air** — a
temperature, a pressure, a humidity, a wind, and a biome that
those come from when nobody has authored them.

It is why a cellar is cooler than the street above it and why a
shut room with a fire in it stops being as cold as outside.

## What has it {#composed-by}

The panel below answers the inverse question — *what in this world
has its own air?* — by reading every row there is, every time
somebody opens the page.

## What does not have it {#what-does-not}

A container is not a place. A pack, a jar, a till, a counter and a
wagon hold things; they do not hold weather. A thing inside one
reads the air of the nearest thing that has any.
