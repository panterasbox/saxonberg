# Hauler {#hauler}

The capability of **pulling a cart** — a hitch, and something in the
shafts strong enough to move what is on the other end.

It is not strength and it is not a vehicle. It is the coupling: the
thing that says *a load can be attached to this and dragged along
behind it*. What happens next is ordinary physics — the load is weighed
against what the puller can carry, and an overloaded rig breaks away.

## What cannot {#what-cannot}

A wolf. A cat. A canary. An animal that acts is not thereby an animal
that works, and the panel above is where that distinction is kept
honest — **you cannot hitch a wagon to something that was never bred to
pull one.**

Until 2026-09-30 this lived only on the person rung, which meant a
draft horse had to be filed as a *character* — with a name, a job, a
faculty for magic and a written biography — in order to be hitched at
all. The horse is a beast now, and pulling is a thing beasts of a
certain kind do.
