# Beast {#beast}

**An animal: a body that acts, and is nobody.**

A wolf on the road. A fox in the yard, a boar in the wood. It has a
brain and uses it. It moves, it notices you, it fights back when you
hit it. And it answers to nobody, has no name, and never will — not
because the world is withholding one, but because a name is the
promotion that turns an animal into somebody's animal, and a wild thing
has not had it.

## The three tiers {#tiers}

Everything alive in this world stands on one of three rungs:

- **a body** — it can be hurt, it has organs, it cools when it dies. A
  corpse is exactly this and no more.
- **a body that acts** — it moves, it perceives, it can be engaged, it
  fights. A wolf, a horse, a cat, and every person.
- **a body that is somebody** — it has a name, a job, a voice, a
  faculty, a history. Only people.

A beast is the middle rung with a brain on top. ⚠ It is not a lesser
person; it is a different question. Until 2026-09-30 the wolf was filed
as a *role somebody fills*, which meant it could cast spells and take
employment — and nothing ever noticed, because nothing ever asked.
