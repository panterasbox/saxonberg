# Scrolls {#scrolls}

A scroll is a working written down. Reading one and saying one are
different acts: `read` tells you what is on it and nothing happens;
`recite` says it aloud, and saying it is what sets it off.

The scroll carries the working, not the power for it. Whoever recites it
pays, so a scroll of a working beyond you is a gamble you take with your
own reserves. And you have to make out the marks to say them: an inked
scroll wants light to read by, an embossed one does not.
