# corpo-veshko

The Corpo mark (`/stuff/idea/corpo/Corpo/veshko`), every Brand whose
`data.owner` is `veshko` (Volk), and the `/corpo/veshko` Organization
(the chart; `maintainers`).

**A corpo pack is capital + the mark, nothing else.** Veshko's distillery
yard — the operating Business, its stock, its hand, the floor bottles —
is a product of the distilling *process* and lives in `trade-distilling`
(`content/trade/distilling/location/veshko-yard/`), owned from here through
`parentOrganization: /corpo/veshko`. The only localities a corpo pack
would ever ship are corpo-shaped buildings (a branch, an HQ); a store
franchised by the corpo belongs to its neighborhood pack.
