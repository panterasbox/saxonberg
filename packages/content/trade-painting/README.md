# trade-painting

The colorman's trade (`/trade/painting`). Paint is a binder with pigment in
it, and both come from what the world already makes: linseed oil off the
stones from flax seed, ochre dug and ground, milk of lime from the
chandlery's kettle, an egg for tempera, lampblack from a swept flue. One
pot (`PaintPot`) mixes every paint; its makeup rides the liquid to the
coat. No new verb — `mill`, `pour`, `stir`, and the kernel's `paint` with a
brush in hand. See `docs/subsystems/painting.md`.
