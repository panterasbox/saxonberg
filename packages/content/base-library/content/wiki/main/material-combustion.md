# Burning {#burning}

A substance that **burns** gives heat when it does — so much per
kilogram — and lights at a temperature of its own. A fire reads both:
whether a fuel will catch, and how much warmth it gives. Burning leaves
ash, gives the room its smoke and the air its smell, and puts some of
itself into whatever is worked over the fire (peat into malt).

This is a property of a substance, named for its physics; a kind of
material has it when every member does.
