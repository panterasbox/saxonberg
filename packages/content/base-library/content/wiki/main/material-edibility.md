# Being food {#being-food}

A substance that **can be eaten** has tastes (sweet, salty, sour,
bitter, umami — amounts a tongue can tell), nutrients and their amounts,
and a serving. Whether a thing is food is whether its material has this
at all: there is no "edible: false".

This is a property of a substance, named for its physics; a kind of
material has it when every member does.
