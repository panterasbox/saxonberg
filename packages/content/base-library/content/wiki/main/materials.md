# Materials {#materials}

Everything is made of something, and what it is made of decides what it
does: whether it burns, melts, rots, floats, or poisons you. A material
has exactly the properties it has. The pages below explain each property
once and list what has it, and each kind of material says what it is and
lists its members.

## What it is chemically {#identity}

A material is an [[Elements|element]], a [[Compounds|compound]] or a
[[Mixtures|mixture]] — or, for most organic matter, none of the three
that anyone has measured.

## Kinds {#kinds}

- [[Mineral]] — what was never alive: metal, stone, salt, alkali, water, gas
  - [[Metal]] — a metal — it has a mechanical response, melts and boils
    - [[ElementalMetal]] — a metal that is one element (iron, copper, gold)
      - [[ToxicMetal]] — an elemental metal that poisons (lead)
        - [[RadioactiveMetal]] — a toxic elemental metal whose nuclei decay (uranium)
    - [[Alloy]] — a metal that is a mixture of metals (bronze, steel)
      - [[ToxicAlloy]] — an alloy that poisons — what its face gives its contents (pewter)
  - [[NonMetal]] — a solid element that is not a metal; it burns (carbon, sulfur)
  - [[Stone]] — rock and earth — solid, porous, melts at a forge heat and changes in a kiln
    - [[Crystal]] — a stone that is one compound (quartz)
    - [[Ore]] — a stone worth smelting: a mixture whose composition names the metal in it
    - [[Clay]] — a plastic earth — a mixture with the smell of wet ground, which fires
    - [[Soil]] — earth you grow in; it smells
    - [[Plaster]] — a set mixture of gypsum and water
    - [[Ceramic]] — a fired body (brick, stoneware, porcelain)
    - [[Glass]] — a stone that light passes through
  - [[Salt]] — an ionic compound — it dissolves, has a pH, crystallizes, melts and boils
    - [[EdibleSalt]] — a salt you season food with (halite, salt)
  - [[Acid]] — a corrosive compound with a pH well below 7 (vitriol); it poisons and fumes
  - [[Lye]] — an alkali — caustic, poisonous, dissolving (lye, potash, quicklime)
  - [[Water]] — water in all its states, clean or carrying what is dissolved in it
  - [[Gas]] — what fills a room rather than lying in it; it boils at a low temperature, may smell and may poison
    - [[FuelGas]] — a gas, or an airborne dust, that burns (coal gas, firedamp, flour dust)
- [[Organic]] — what was alive, or was made of what was (fossil fuels, preparations); every organic substance has a smell of its own, even where a row has not authored it yet
  - [[Plant]] — organic matter from a plant; it carries the species and tissue it came from
    - [[Wood]] — wood — it burns, seasons and shrinks, and changes in the fire
    - [[Fiber]] — plant fiber — cellulose, which burns and rots
      - [[Pulp]] — fiber beaten into water: a slurry that freezes
    - [[Herb]] — leaf, root and bark gathered for what is in them; it wilts
      - [[EdibleHerb]] — an herb a mouth takes (mint, juniper, hay)
  - [[Animal]] — organic matter from an animal; it carries the species and tissue it came from
    - [[Flesh]] — animal flesh — food that spoils, a mixture of lean, fat and water
      - [[Muscle]] — a muscle that worked, and so carries the work a cut's texture derives from
    - [[Blood]] — blood — it spoils, and freezing ruins it
    - [[HideHorn]] — the hard and fibrous parts of an animal (leather, bone, wool, silk)
      - [[Hide]] — raw skin, which spoils until it is tanned
      - [[Horn]] — horn, which a lantern pane is pressed from because light passes through it
  - [[Food]] — what a body eats: it spoils, may poison, has a pH
    - [[SolidFood]] — a food with a texture — grain, dough, bread, sausage, curd
    - [[Syrup]] — a food that is a solution boiled down — maple syrup, molasses, caramel
    - [[Drink]] — a food that is poured
      - [[Spirit]] — a drink that burns
  - [[FatOilWax]] — fat, oil and wax — they melt, boil and burn
    - [[Fat]] — a fat a body eats, which turns rancid
  - [[Polymer]] — rubber and its kin — a mixture that cures, burns and melts
  - [[SolidFuel]] — what a fire is fed (coal, charcoal, peat, ash)
  - [[Oil]] — mineral oil and its distillates — they burn, boil and poison
  - [[Soap]] — a salt of a fat — it cures by drying
  - [[Residue]] — what is left over from a process (lees, manure, sweepings); it rots
- [[PigmentPaint]] — a coat: pigment, paint, glaze, ink — mineral and organic alike, so it sits outside that split
  - [[CoatingOil]] — a coat that burns (pitch, tar, linseed oil)
- [[Glue]] — a coat that holds two things together; it sets and melts
  - [[Resin]] — a glue that burns (rosin)
