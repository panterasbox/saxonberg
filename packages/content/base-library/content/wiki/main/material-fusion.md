# Melting {#melting}

A substance that **melts** has a melting point, and the same number is
its freezing point read the other way: iron runs at a forge heat, wax in
a warm hand, water freezes below its own. Melting takes heat without
warming (the latent heat), which is why ice holds a drink cold. What a
frozen pool sets as, and how a metal sets against a cold mould, belong
here too.

This is a property of a substance, named for its physics; a kind of
material has it when every member does.
