# Elements {#elements}

An **element** is one kind of atom: iron, copper, carbon. It has a
symbol, an atomic number and a molar mass.

This is a property of a substance, named for its physics; a kind of
material has it when every member does. It is one of the three chemical
identities — element, compound, mixture — and a material is exactly one
of them or none.
