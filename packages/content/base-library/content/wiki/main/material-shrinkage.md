# Seasoning {#seasoning}

A substance that **seasons** dries over days and shrinks as it does: a
green board, a bar of hard soap. How long a 25 mm piece takes in good
drying air, and how much it shrinks.

This is a property of a substance, named for its physics; a kind of
material has it when every member does.

## Kinds that have it {#kinds}

[[Wood]], [[Soap]]
