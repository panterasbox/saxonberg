# Mixtures {#mixtures}

A **mixture** is made of other materials in proportions: bronze is
copper and tin, a slip is clay and water. Its constituents are listed by
weight, and what elements are in it is worked out from them.

This is a property of a substance, named for its physics; a kind of
material has it when every member does. It is one of the three chemical
identities — element, compound, mixture — and a material is exactly one
of them or none.
