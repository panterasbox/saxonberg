# Laying a coat {#laying-a-coat}

A substance that **lays a coat**: a bath of it leaves a layer on what is
dipped, the word a look uses for that layer, how fast a set coat wears
away in the weather — and, when it is one, its pigment (the color it
gives back and how well it hides) or its glue (how strongly it holds).

This is a property of a substance, named for its physics; a kind of
material has it when every member does.

## Kinds that have it {#kinds}

[[PigmentPaint]], [[Glue]]
