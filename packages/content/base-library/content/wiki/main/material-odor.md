# Smell {#smell}

A substance with a **smell of its own** gives off so much of it per
kilogram at room warmth. Iron, glass and clean water have none.

This is a property of a substance, named for its physics; a kind of
material has it when every member does.

## Kinds that have it {#kinds}

[[NonMetal]], [[Clay]], [[Soil]], [[Acid]], [[Lye]], [[Water]], [[Gas]], [[Organic]], [[PigmentPaint]], [[Glue]]
