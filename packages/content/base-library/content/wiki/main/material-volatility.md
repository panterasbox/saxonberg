# Boiling {#boiling}

A substance that **boils** has a boiling point — water at a hundred
degrees, spirit well below — and an open vessel of it evaporates into
the air at a rate of its own. A still separates by it; a gas is
something whose boiling point is below the room.

This is a property of a substance, named for its physics; a kind of
material has it when every member does.
