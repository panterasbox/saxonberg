# Light through it {#light-through-it}

A substance **light passes through** absorbs some of it per meter
(Beer–Lambert): a pane of clear glass lets most through, a green one
less, horn less again.

This is a property of a substance, named for its physics; a kind of
material has it when every member does.

## Kinds that have it {#kinds}

[[Glass]], [[Water]], [[Horn]]
