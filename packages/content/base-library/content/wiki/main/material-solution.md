# Dissolving {#dissolving}

A substance that **dissolves, or holds what is dissolved**: how much it
takes up by temperature, what crystallizes out when it holds too much,
what a filter strains it into, and — boiled down — what it becomes at
each concentration (syrup to sugar, brine to salt).

This is a property of a substance, named for its physics; a kind of
material has it when every member does.

## Kinds that have it {#kinds}

[[Salt]], [[Acid]], [[Lye]], [[Water]], [[Syrup]], [[Drink]], [[PigmentPaint]]
