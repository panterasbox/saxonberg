# Taking up water {#taking-up-water}

A **porous** substance holds water in itself — wood, cloth, paper,
earth. How much it takes, how much weaker it is wet, and what is left
when it is soaked through are its facts. Metal holds none.

This is a property of a substance, named for its physics; a kind of
material has it when every member does.
