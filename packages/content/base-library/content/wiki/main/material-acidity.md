# pH {#ph}

A substance's **pH** — acid below seven, alkali above, one scale read
both ways. Acids draw lead out of a glaze; strong acids and alkalis eat
what they touch, and what they eat is the corrosion list.

This is a property of a substance, named for its physics; a kind of
material has it when every member does.

## Kinds that have it {#kinds}

[[Salt]], [[Acid]], [[Lye]], [[Water]], [[Food]], [[Soap]], [[PigmentPaint]]
