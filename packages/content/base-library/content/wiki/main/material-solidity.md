# Hardness and toughness {#hardness-and-toughness}

A solid answers being worked: how **hard** it is to scratch or dent, how
**tough** it is to break, how much it springs back, how cleanly it
splits along its grain, how much it grows as it warms, and what a broken
piece of it becomes. A liquid has none of this.

This is a property of a substance, named for its physics; a kind of
material has it when every member does.
