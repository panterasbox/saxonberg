# Changing with time and heat {#changing-with-time-and-heat}

A substance that **changes at a rate its temperature sets**: clay
firing, wood charring, a glue setting, curd draining in a hoop. The
change is one rule — so much change per second at a temperature — and a
kiln, a larder and a cane field all read it.

This is a property of a substance, named for its physics; a kind of
material has it when every member does.
