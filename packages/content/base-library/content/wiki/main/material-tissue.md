# Living source {#living-source}

A substance that **came from a living thing** remembers the species and
the tissue it came from: oak from the oak, mutton from the sheep.

This is a property of a substance, named for its physics; a kind of
material has it when every member does.

## Kinds that have it {#kinds}

[[Plant]], [[Animal]], [[Food]], [[Residue]]
