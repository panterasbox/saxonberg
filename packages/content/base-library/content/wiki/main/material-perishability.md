# Spoiling {#spoiling}

A **perishable** substance spoils: how steeply its rot answers to
warmth, and how much free water it holds for a microbe to use. Salt,
sugar and drying slow it; a cold larder slows it more.

This is a property of a substance, named for its physics; a kind of
material has it when every member does.
