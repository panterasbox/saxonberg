# Tooltips {#tooltips}

Hover anything you can click (a name in the scrollback, a chip on a card, a
way out, a button) and a **tooltip** opens beside it after a moment. It
says what the thing is and what a click on it does. It never does anything
on its own: clicking the thing still does what it always did.

## What a tooltip tells you {#what-it-tells}

- **What it is**: its name, and what kind of thing it is.
- **What you can make out of it**: a line of description, a picture if it
  has one, how many there are. It only says what **you** perceive **now**.
  In the dark it says *Too dark to make out.* If the thing has gone, it
  says *You can't see that now.* It never shows you an old description.
- **What a click runs**: the boxed command at the bottom is exactly what
  clicking will send.
- **What is this, really?** This line is folded shut. Open it to see where
  the thing comes from. How much is open from the start is up to you:
  `settings set spoiler.appetite 2` opens these lines for you everywhere,
  and the same setting decides how much of the wiki you see unfolded.

A tooltip is a snapshot. It does not change while it is open; hover again
for a fresh one.

## Reading deeper {#reading-deeper}

Linger on a tooltip, or press **Shift**, and it **locks**: you can move the
pointer into it without it closing. Dotted words inside it are **terms**.
Hover one and its own tooltip opens on top, with a trail showing how you
got there. **Esc** closes one level at a time; clicking anywhere else
closes them all.

## Without a mouse {#without-a-mouse}

- **Keyboard:** Tab into the scrollback and its newest link is focused; the
  arrow keys move between links, each one's tooltip opens as you reach it,
  and Enter runs it.
- **Phone:** press and hold a link to open its tooltip as a sheet from the
  bottom of the screen. Tap a term inside it to go deeper, and use *back*
  to return. A thing's sheet has an **actions** button for everything you
  can do with it. A quick tap still asks before it sends, as it always has.
