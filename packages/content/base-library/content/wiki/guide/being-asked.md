# Being asked to do something {#being-asked}

Your character never does or says anything you did not choose. Nobody
else's code can make you speak, give something away, join a group, or
hand over money or ground.

## When someone offers to do it for you {#offers}

A few things may be *asked* of you, because asking saves you a walk:
buying a lot at the land office is one. The realtor tells you the lot
and the price in his own words. Then the game, not the realtor, asks you
the question:

```
a land agent in a good coat gone slightly shiny would have you run `title buy lot-4`. Let them?
```

The question shows the exact command that would run, so it cannot be
dressed up as something else. Say yes and it runs as if you typed it,
with your money and your gates. Say no, or say nothing, and nothing
happens. If you cannot cover the price, you are told why.

`help <verb>` says when a verb may be asked of you: *Another character
may ask you to do this; you are always asked first.*

## Characters nobody plays {#keepers}

A shopkeeper, a land agent, an animal: these are driven by their
**keeper**, the person who holds title over the place they were written,
because the keeper wrote their minds. Owning an animal is not keeping
it: your cat answers to its own nature, not to your typing. If its
keeper ever makes it do something, you are told.
