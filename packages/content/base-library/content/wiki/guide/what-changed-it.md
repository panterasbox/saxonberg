# What changed it {#what-changed-it}

Some of what you see keeps itself up to date: a **live card**, and the
**navigator** (where you are, the ways out, the weather outside). Nobody
has to type anything for them to change. That would be a mystery if they
did not also say **why** they changed — so they do.

## The mark {#the-mark}

When a live card or a part of the navigator changes, it glows for a moment
and a small mark appears beside it for a few seconds. Hover over the mark
(or tap it) and it tells you what changed it:

- **Changed by:** a line from your own scrollback — the command you just
  ran (`north`, `open door`, `sit`), or something somebody else did that
  you saw (*Mara opens the oak door.*). Tap **show in scrollback** to jump
  to that line.
- **You didn't see what changed this.** Something did — a door unlocked
  from the other side, somebody you cannot see walked in — but nothing of
  it reached you. The card is telling you the truth about the world without
  telling you more than you perceived.
- **Time passed.** No one did anything: the hour turned, the weather
  changed, the light went.

Everything a card or the navigator shows changes for one of those three
reasons. A card never changes because the game decided to look again.

## Who is here {#who-is-here}

A live card about a place lists who is **Here** as it is now: someone who
walks in appears, someone who leaves goes, and a person who sits down shows
where. The description of the place and the things in it are as they were
when you looked.

A card that is **not** live says when its answer was taken; click its age
to look again.

## When something does not change {#when-it-does-not-change}

A card you have minimized, scrolled away from, or put behind another one is
not being kept current — the game does not spend anything on what you are
not looking at. The moment you bring it back it catches up, all at once.
