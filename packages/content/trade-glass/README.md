# @saxonberg/content-trade-glass

The glass trade. Batch (sand + ash + lime) → a pot of molten glass, fired
in the shipped kiln through the platform's `fire`; a blowpipe gathers and
works it hot through the **hot-work window** (`gob`/`shape`/`reheat`/
`crack`); the cold bench scores, snaps, grozes, flattens and glazes
(`scribe`/`snap`/`groze`/`flatten`/`glaze`). The iron in the sand is the
colour (derived, never authored); cullet re-melts whole but only ever goes
greener; a stained pane colours the light in its room.

Rides four kernel seams shipped in Stage A of this build: Colour on
`Light`, the firing's mass/alloying carry, the meltable-non-metal salvage
branch, and light-strike on spoilage. Ships one Discipline (`glasswork`),
a pack `lib/` (`Tinted`, `HotWork`), thing classes, a `Reading`, rows,
recipes, dials and two archetypes — so a second glasshouse is rows.

See `docs/subsystems/glass.md`.
