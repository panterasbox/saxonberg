Cinematic concept art, atmospheric and reverent, painterly with moody chiaroscuro. The vast after-hours library of a scholarly society at blue hour: a Gothic vaulted hall with towering two-story oak bookshelves, a timber rib ceiling, rolling library ladders, an antique brass armillary sphere, and manuscripts left open on reading tables. A massive pointed-arch cathedral window dominates one side; cold blue moonlight and soft volumetric god-rays pour through it. The window’s stone tracery and stained glass form three interlocking rings (Borromean rings) as a subtle heraldic motif, catching the light — a maker’s mark worked into the architecture. Deep navy palette (Old Glory Blue, #002147) in the shadows, cool white and faint silver highlights, near-monochrome and desaturated, with only the faintest warm candle accent. Fine drifting dust motes in the light shafts. Empty of people, hushed, scholarly, contemplative. Wide establishing shot. No text, no letters, no watermark, no modern objects.
